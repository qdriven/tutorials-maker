# Generate two weeks of social content in three hours

> The poster uses Claude to produce text prompts, images and posting suggestions from a predefined schedule and content strategy.

**Category:** Content & Creative  
**Agent:** Claude  
**Source:** Reddit · Jan 1, 2026

## What they did

The poster batches two weeks of content in a Saturday session by giving Claude a markdown brand guide, weekly themes, and platform-specific rules for Instagram, X, and LinkedIn. Claude generates post copy, hashtags, image prompts, and posting recommendations; the poster then reviews every draft, adds personal examples, fixes tone and facts, and loads the edited posts into a scheduler with images.

## How it went

They say it cut content work from about 10 hours a week to 3 hours every two weeks, while improving consistency and quality. It fell short when they asked for too much at once or skipped editing, which made posts sound obviously AI-written.

## Worth knowing

Their stated sweet spot is two weeks of content at a time; they also spent about 2 hours once making the brand guidelines that keep later batches consistent.

## Prompt

```text
Here is my content strategy: [audience, tone, themes, platforms] and my posting schedule: [days and frequency]. Create two weeks of social content in one pass: for each slot, write the post text, an image generation prompt, and a suggested posting time. Put it all in a table I can copy into a scheduler, and stop once all [number] slots are filled.
```
