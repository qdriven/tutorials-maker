# Set tax and contract reminders and review contracts

> Karina Chow had Claude Cowork set email reminders for quarterly tax deadlines, contract end dates, and state law changes, and help with contract review and market rate analysis.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** Claude Cowork  
**Source:** X · Oct 8, 2026

## What they did

Karina told Claude about falling behind on quarterly taxes, contract end dates, state laws for employees/subcontractors, and related admin. Claude offered on its own to create email reminders for deadlines and law changes; she accepted. It also reviewed and wrote contracts and ran market rate analysis.

## How it went

No specific results or numbers are stated. She notes the agent handles 80% of such tasks adequately but falls short on the final 20% that sometimes matters.

## Worth knowing

If 80% quality suffices, the agent work is perfect, but the last 20% usually needs a person.

## Prompt

```text
Read my contracts in [folder path] and set email reminders for [quarterly tax deadlines, contract end dates, and changes to [state] law]. Also review [contract name] and compare its rates against typical market rates for [role or service], noting any unusual terms. Show me the list of reminders you're about to create before setting them, and finish with a short summary of the contract review.
```
