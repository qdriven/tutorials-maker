# Sweep Gmail spam folder for real business mail

> The author has Grok Bot open Gmail in the browser, read the spam folder and flag legitimate mail like affiliate replies and payment notices.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** aliakbarfakhri.com · Undated

## What they did

The author connected the Google Workspace accounts for two businesses to Grok Bot, which reads, triages and drafts but does not send. Because it drives a real browser, it opens Gmail like a person, goes into the spam folder, reads the messages and flags real ones. Typing credentials is left to the author, who takes over the screen for that.

## How it went

In the first month it found 48 real messages in spam, including a network approval the author had been chasing for two weeks.

## Worth knowing

Every bot shares one cloud computer and its logins, so the author recommends a separate account per business entity. The weekly usage allowance is also unpublished, so costs are hard to predict.

## Prompt

```text
Open Gmail in the browser, go to the spam folder, and read the messages from the last [number] days. Make a list of any that look like legitimate business mail, such as [replies from partners, payment notices, customer messages], with sender, subject, and why you think each is real. Don't move, delete, or reply to anything; just give me the list so I can decide.
```
