# Order healthy meals twice a day automatically

> Flo Crivello's agent has decided and ordered his meals twice a day for two weeks, following instructions to pick the healthiest options.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** X · Nov 17, 2025

## What they did

Flo set up an agent with instructions to pick the healthiest options and order food. The agent decided and ordered meals twice a day. No details are given on tools, access, or any human steps after setup.

## How it went

Every meal over the last two weeks was decided by the agent. The user hates the arrangement.

## Prompt

```text
Find lunch and dinner options for me on [delivery app or restaurant site] delivering to [address], choosing the healthiest choices that fit [dietary goals] and cost under [budget] each. You are done when the meal is in the cart with the delivery time shown. Pause and show me the order and total before paying, and wait for my confirmation.
```
