# Fight a hospital bill with an appeal letter

> A user had ChatGPT review a hospital bill and draft an appeal letter; the insurer and hospital then acknowledged billing errors and replaced the bill.

**Category:** Money, Bills & Benefits  
**Agent:** ChatGPT  
**Source:** Reddit · Apr 15, 2025

## What they did

The user fed ChatGPT their hospital bill, had it review the charges, and used its draft appeal letter to challenge the bill with the insurer and hospital. ChatGPT flagged six line items as extremely high and helped translate the bill into the billing language the user needed; the user then handled the calls and appeal.

## How it went

By the next morning, both the insurance company and hospital said there were billing errors, threw out the bill, and said a new one would come. The post does not say what the corrected bill was or how much was saved.

## Prompt

```text
I received a medical bill from [hospital/provider] for [amount] related to [procedure or visit date]. I will paste the bill, my insurance Explanation of Benefits, and any notes. Review them line by line for errors like duplicate charges, wrong codes, or services I did not receive, then draft a polite but firm appeal letter to [hospital billing department/insurer] listing each issue and requesting a corrected bill. Show me the letter and a checklist of what to attach before I send anything.
```
