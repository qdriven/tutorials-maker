# Book a restaurant table and add it to the calendar

> A reporter had Meta's Muse find a Midtown Manhattan restaurant for two at 6 pm, book it and add it to the calendar; holiday travel booking went clunkily.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** gizmodo.com · Undated

## What they did

On the free tier, the reporter connected Muse to his personal Gmail and Google Calendar. He asked for a reasonably priced Midtown restaurant with a table for two at 6 pm. Muse suggested a Mediterranean place, and he then told it to book the table and add it to his calendar. A confirmation arrived in his inbox.

## How it went

Booking and calendar entry were quick, but the restaurant's prices were well above the budget he'd given. Muse's slow replies during follow-up frustrated him, so he walked into a favorite restaurant instead.

## Worth knowing

Check the menu prices yourself. Muse ignored the stated budget, and the free tier has a weekly token limit.

## Prompt

```text
Find a [cuisine] restaurant in [neighborhood, city] with a table for [number] people at [time] on [date]. Show me your top pick with the menu highlights and price range before booking anything. Once I approve, make the reservation and add it to my calendar with the address and confirmation details, then tell me when it's done.
```
