# Draft parent-teacher communications

> A teacher says ChatGPT handled all their parent-teacher communications for a month, and they also tried it for lesson planning.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** ChatGPT  
**Source:** Reddit · Undated

## What they did

The teacher said they first used ChatGPT for lesson outlines, then for weekly comments, then to refine those comments from added input, and eventually to draft a parent letter. In comments, they said the tool worked when they gave very specific requirements, and they still checked factual information themselves while using it mainly for procedures, suggestions, and the filler around an email’s main point.

## How it went

They said ChatGPT handled all of their parent-teacher communications for the previous month and cut a lot of typing. For lesson planning, they said results were good with enough specificity but still needed materials, edits, and fact-checking.

## Worth knowing

Their clearest practical takeaway was that specificity mattered, and for kindergarten lesson work they estimated it saved about 70% of their usual typing time.

## Prompt

```text
I'm a [grade/subject] teacher. Draft parent emails for these situations: [e.g. progress update, behavior concern, upcoming event], using a [warm/professional] tone and the details in [notes or file]. Save each draft in [folder] and do not send anything; show me the drafts so I can edit and send them myself.
```
