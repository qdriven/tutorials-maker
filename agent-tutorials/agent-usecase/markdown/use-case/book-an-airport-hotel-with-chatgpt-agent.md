# Book an airport hotel with ChatGPT Agent

> Author had ChatGPT Agent pick and book a Gatwick hotel on Booking.com; it reached the card confirmation screen, then needed the author to take over.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** ChatGPT Agent  
**Source:** wow.pjh.is · Undated

## What they did

The author pasted a long instruction block into ChatGPT Agent. It limited the agent to Booking.com, used Booking.com's own filter names, and set ranking and tie-break rules. The agent used his logged-in account and searched, ranked and picked a hotel. It stopped at the CVC confirmation screen with links to the runner-ups. He then took control.

## How it went

Three Gatwick runs each picked the hotel he would have chosen, taking 5-10 minutes each. Stansted worked too. Reykjavík failed until he dropped the air-conditioning filter. The agent made minor errors, such as a wrong star rating.

## Worth knowing

Agent mode ignores project instructions, so paste the prompt every time. UK banks likely block 3D Secure, so book through a saved Booking.com account.

## Prompt

```text
Find and book a hotel near [airport] for the night of [date] for [number] guests, under [budget] per night, with free cancellation and a good rating of at least [score]. Compare at least three options on [booking site] and recommend one with reasons. Stop at the payment screen and show me the room, total price and cancellation terms, and wait for me to confirm and enter payment details.
```
