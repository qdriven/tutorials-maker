# Reclaim forgotten crypto airdrops and deposits

> A user had Claude/GPT agents scan blockchain history to locate and reclaim forgotten airdrops and deposits, recovering 10x their annual AI cost.

**Category:** Money, Bills & Benefits  
**Agent:** Claude, ChatGPT  
**Source:** X · Oct 12, 2026

## What they did

The user gave Claude and GPT agents access to their full blockchain transaction history. The agents scanned every past transaction and attempted to recompose each one to check whether any deposit lacked a matching withdrawal.

## How it went

This recovered forgotten airdrops and deposits worth 10x the annual cost of the AI subscriptions.

## Prompt

```text
Here are my wallet addresses: [addresses] on [chains, e.g. Ethereum, Arbitrum, Solana]. Review my on-chain history to find forgotten airdrops, unclaimed rewards, and deposits or stuck funds I could still reclaim, and list each with the protocol, estimated value, and the official claim steps. Never ask for my seed phrase or private keys, and flag anything that looks like a scam. I'll do the transactions myself, so stop once you've given me a verified list.
```
