# Plan Saturday errands and book haircuts

> ChatGPT Agent planned errands, found salons with online booking and worked on booking kids' haircuts, with some steps needing human confirmation.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** ChatGPT Agent  
**Source:** tomsguide.com · Undated

## What they did

Amanda Caswell asked ChatGPT to plan a Saturday of errands: a haircut, an Amazon return, groceries and Target. Memory was on, so it already knew her location and her kids' ages. The agent found nearby salons with online booking and opened their booking pages. It narrowed the choices to the earliest slots that fit her schedule. It also grouped the errands by location into an ordered route with time estimates.

## How it went

She got a few solid salon options and a one-loop errand itinerary within minutes. It couldn't always finish bookings on its own, so some steps still needed her.

## Worth knowing

Booking sites often require logging in, SMS verification or payment details, and those steps still need you to confirm them.

## Prompt

```text
Plan my [day] errands: [list of errands and locations] in an efficient order with estimated drive times starting at [time]. Find [number] salons or barbers near [area] that offer online booking, and look for haircut openings for [number] kids on [date]. Do not book anything until you show me the time slot, price and the details you plan to submit and I confirm.
```
