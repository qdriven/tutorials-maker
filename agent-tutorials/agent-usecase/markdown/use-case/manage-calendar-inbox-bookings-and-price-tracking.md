# Manage calendar, inbox, bookings and price tracking

> A poster describes Muse handling calendar, inbox, bookings, and price tracking across connected apps.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** Reddit · Sep 16, 2026

## What they did

The poster says they had just set up Muse and immediately used it to draft the Reddit post itself. They describe Muse as a personal agent with its own computer that can take over calendar, inbox, bookings, and price tracking, and say it can keep working even when they are away from the app; in comments, another user adds that you choose how much information to connect, such as whether to grant email access.

## How it went

The source gives no concrete completed task or metrics beyond the poster’s early impression that Muse might finally take work off their plate. A commenter’s main limitation was trust: they would not give it access to their email.

## Prompt

```text
Each morning, check my calendar and inbox and give me a short brief: today's meetings, emails needing a reply, and any bookings coming up. Also track the price of [product or flight] and tell me if it drops below [price]. Draft replies and calendar changes but don't send or accept anything until I confirm.
```
