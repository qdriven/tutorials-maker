# Complete an online course assignment in the browser

> Perplexity Comet was prompted to complete a Coursera web design assignment and appeared to finish it in a short clip; the CEO told people not to do this.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Perplexity Comet  
**Source:** fortune.com · Oct 10, 2025

## What they did

A developer used Perplexity's agentic Comet browser on a Coursera course page. The only prompt shown was a short instruction to complete the assignment. Comet read the page and worked through what looked like a 45-minute web design assignment by itself. The developer posted a screen clip on X and tagged Perplexity and its CEO.

## How it went

The 16-second clip shows Comet apparently finishing the whole assignment. The article doesn't say whether the work was graded or correct. CEO Aravind Srinivas replied publicly: "Absolutely don't do this."

## Worth knowing

Fortune cites security audits that found Comet can follow hidden instructions on web pages and complete purchases on fake sites without human checks, so supervise it closely.

## Prompt

```text
Open my [course platform] course [course name] and find the assignment [assignment name]. Walk me through what it asks, summarize the key concepts, and draft suggested answers that I can review and learn from. Do not submit anything. Show me the draft and wait until I have read it and told you what to submit.
```
