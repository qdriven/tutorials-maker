# Rebook a delayed flight connection mid-flight

> OpenAI's Dot chatted with Alaska, Delta and KLM, found a new route and rebooked Denis for free after he approved, while he was still on the plane.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Dots  
**Source:** X · Oct 3, 2026

## What they did

Denis gave Dot from OpenAI access to its own computer. While he was still on the delayed Alaska flight, the agent simultaneously messaged Alaska, Delta, and KLM, located a new routing, and requested the free rebooking. Denis approved the change.

## How it went

The agent secured the rebooking and avoided an overnight stay in Seattle. It later repeated the process mid-Atlantic when the Seattle-Munich leg was also delayed.

## Prompt

```text
My flight [airline and flight number] is delayed and I may miss my connection at [connecting airport]. Contact [airlines involved] through their chat support, find the best free rebooking option to reach [final destination], and show me the new itinerary and any fees before you confirm anything. Once I approve and the rebooking is confirmed, email me the new details.
```
