# Book restaurants and a hotel dinner in Japanese

> Muse booked every restaurant for a Japan trip and went back and forth in Japanese with the Hakone hotel to secure a dinner slot not on the website.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** X · Sep 21, 2026

## What they did

Muse received the Japan trip details and was tasked with handling all restaurant bookings. It made the reservations on its own across multiple sites, then exchanged messages in Japanese directly with the Hakone hotel to request and confirm a dinner slot that did not appear on the hotel website. The agent switched between booking tools and communication channels without further input.

## How it went

Every restaurant reservation was completed. The off-menu dinner slot was secured through the hotel exchange.

## Prompt

```text
Book restaurants for my trip to [destination] on [dates] based on my picks: [restaurant names or cuisine and budget]. Where booking isn't available online, contact the place in [local language] to request a table or a dinner slot at [hotel or restaurant] for [number] people. Show me each reservation request and the time before sending, and give me a list of confirmations when done.
```
