# Fix a booking made on the wrong date

> Dot spotted that David had booked Paris to Barcelona on the wrong date for an upcoming Europe trip and changed the booking to the right dates.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Dots  
**Source:** X · Sep 30, 2026

## What they did

Dot had access to David's travel bookings for an upcoming Europe trip. It proactively scanned the details, identified the Paris to Barcelona flight on the incorrect date, and updated the booking to the correct dates on its own.

## How it went

The booking was changed to the right dates.

## Prompt

```text
Check my email for my upcoming trip bookings to [destination or trip name] and compare the dates on each flight, hotel and train against my plans of [intended dates]. If any booking is on the wrong date, find the change options and fees, and show me before making any change. When all bookings match my dates, send me a short summary of what's now confirmed.
```
