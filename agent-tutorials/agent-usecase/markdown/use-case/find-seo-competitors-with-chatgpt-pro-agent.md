# Find SEO competitors with ChatGPT Pro agent

> The poster uses ChatGPT Pro for SEO tasks, with the pro agent finding competitors.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** ChatGPT  
**Source:** Reddit · Jun 21, 2026

## What they did

The poster says they paid $100 a month for ChatGPT Pro, uploaded their website, and prompted it to optimize the site, research competitors, make new pages, and add or change content. They also set it to extended thought and asked for a DOCX report and changelog; in comments they say the pro agent found competitors, and they reviewed the changes afterward.

## How it went

They report visits rose 25% within a few days and later said the gains were still holding strong. The account is thin on specifics, and the poster also says they do not yet know exactly what the agent changed.

## Worth knowing

The clearest practical catch is that the poster says they still had to review the changelog manually, despite paying $100 per month for ChatGPT Pro.

## Prompt

```text
Find my real SEO competitors for [website URL] in the niche of [topic]. Search for [number] of my main keywords, such as [keyword 1, keyword 2], and list the domains that consistently rank, including how they differ from my site in content type and focus. Finish with a table of the top [number] competitors, why each matters, and one opportunity I could pursue against each. Do not sign up for tools or submit any forms without asking me first.
```
