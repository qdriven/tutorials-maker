# Browse the web and compare products with an agentic browser

> A user relies on Comet to click through painful websites for common tasks and to compare products and places to stay.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** Perplexity Comet  
**Source:** HN · Nov 5, 2026

## What they did

The commenter uses Comet, an agentic browser, as a hands-off way to deal with websites where finding the right buttons for common tasks is a hassle. They simply ask Comet to do the task. They also have it compare products, compare Airbnb places, and write personalized Instagram messages.

## How it went

They say they can't live without Comet for agentic browsing. No numbers or specific results are given, and the comment doesn't describe any failures with Comet itself.

## Prompt

```text
I want to compare [product type or places to stay] for [my needs, budget, dates/location]. Browse at least 5 options across different sites, and give me a table with price, key features, reviews, and a link for each, plus your top pick and why. Do not buy or book anything or enter payment details; stop at the comparison.
```
