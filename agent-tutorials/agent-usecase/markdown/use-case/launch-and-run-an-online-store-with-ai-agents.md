# Launch and run an online store with AI agents

> The author used an AI store builder to launch a retail shop taking real payments in one sitting, with five agents watching orders, stock and customers.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** ryrob.com · Undated

## What they did

Ryan Robinson described his Little River Studios shop (desk gear and studio equipment) in one prompt to Bluehost's AI store builder. It produced a template with categories and product pages in about a minute. He typed a chat request to add checkout, then connected Stripe, taxes and shipping through a wizard, and placed a test order. Five agents (catalog, order, inventory, customer, coordinator) run in the background.

## How it went

A live store taking card, Apple Pay and PayPal payments went up in one sitting. The starter products were placeholders, and product choices and marketing stayed with him. The post gives no sales results.

## Worth knowing

Checkout wasn't added by default, and edits use monthly AI credits, so batch your changes. The post is a Bluehost partnership.

## Prompt

```text
Build me an online store selling [product type] with a product catalog, cart, and checkout, using [Stripe or payment provider] in test mode first. Add simple admin views for orders, stock levels, and customer questions. Stop when I can place a test order end to end, and pause and show me everything before switching on real payments or publishing the site.
```
