# Research restaurants and hotel availability for trips

> A writer used Comet to browse Michelin restaurants for Copenhagen, check Japan hotel availability, and look for deals.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Perplexity Comet  
**Source:** techradar.com · Undated

## What they did

While writing an article, the writer gave Comet, Perplexity's AI browser, several tasks. It browsed Michelin Guide restaurants for a Copenhagen trip against his criteria, checked hotel availability for a Japan honeymoon next year, and looked for the best deal on a game preorder. He left it running and carried on with his work.

## How it went

The tasks ran in the background while he wrote, which he found freed his attention. The source gives no results for the restaurant or hotel searches. Some prompts crashed when the AI hit a hurdle, and he held back from letting it book a restaurant.

## Worth knowing

Comet was in beta and limited to waitlist users or the $200 a month Perplexity Max plan. At first, watching it work was slower than doing the tasks himself.

## Prompt

```text
I'm planning a trip to [city or country] from [dates]. Find [number] well-reviewed restaurants in [style, e.g. Michelin-starred] and check hotel availability for [number] guests in [area] within [budget]. Give me a short comparison table with prices and booking links, and do not book anything.
```
