# Find Prague apartments and contact landlords

> Manus browsed listings and returned five apartments, but looped and made mistakes when asked to email landlords to schedule viewings.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Manus  
**Source:** autogmail.com · Undated

## What they did

The author gave Manus one prompt: a 2-bedroom in Prague 2 or 3, up to 30,000 CZK, near a metro, pet-friendly. Manus searched Google, set filters on Sreality, and used browser translation on Bezrealitky, without asking questions. When asked to email landlords for viewings, it went back to the listing and looked for contact details. The author had to step in.

## How it went

After about 5-6 minutes it returned five listings with prices, links and contacts. One "2 bedroom" was really a 2+1. The landlord emails failed: it looped on page refreshes, probably at a login wall, then gave a generic error.

## Worth knowing

Check Czech layout terms yourself. Manus read 2+1 as two bedrooms, and its Czech intro email to a landlord was awkward.

## Prompt

```text
Search [listing sites] for apartments to rent in [city] with [bedrooms], under [budget] per month, near [area or transit]. Return the five best with price, size, link, and the contact details. Then draft a short message to each landlord asking for a viewing on [dates], and show me the drafts before sending anything.
```
