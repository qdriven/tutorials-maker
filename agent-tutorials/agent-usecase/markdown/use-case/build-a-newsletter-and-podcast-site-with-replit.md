# Build a newsletter and podcast site with Replit

> A creator got a clickable prototype in 10 minutes but spent 10+ hours failing to put it on a custom URL, until Val Town staff helped launch the MVP.

**Category:** Content & Creative  
**Agent:** Replit Agent  
**Source:** thenextnext.substack.com · Undated

## What they did

The author chose to build the site for their podcast and newsletter with AI tools instead of Squarespace. Replit produced a clickable prototype in about 10 minutes. Connecting it to a custom URL then stalled for more than 10 hours. Val Town staff offered to pair program with them using Val Town's tools, and together they got the MVP site live.

## How it went

The MVP site launched with Val Town's help. The Replit custom-URL step was the failure, costing over 10 hours of going in circles. Episode population is still untested.

## Worth knowing

Budget for the custom domain step: the prototype took minutes, but deployment to a custom URL took over 10 hours and outside help.

## Prompt

```text
Build a simple website for my [newsletter / podcast] called [name] about [topic], with a home page, an archive of [issues / episodes], and a signup form for [email list tool]. Use placeholder content I can replace later. When the clickable prototype works, walk me step by step through publishing it on my custom domain [yourdomain.com], and pause before any step that changes DNS settings or costs money.
```
