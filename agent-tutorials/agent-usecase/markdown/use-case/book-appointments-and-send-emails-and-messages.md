# Book appointments and send emails and messages

> The poster had ChatGPT agent book appointments and send emails and messages to their boss, pausing for them to sign into email.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** ChatGPT Agent  
**Source:** Reddit · Undated

## What they did

The poster said they used ChatGPT agent for two days to book appointments, send emails, and send messages to their boss. In the comments, they explained one concrete workflow: they had it find tutor contact details and write 10 personalized outreach emails in one prompt, and when it needed mail access it paused to ask them to sign into their email before continuing.

## How it went

They said the agent actually sent the emails and felt dramatically more useful than expected. The clearest result they gave was sending about 10 tailored tutor emails; the thread also shows limits around design quality and some uncertainty from others about autonomous email access.

## Worth knowing

The practical catch was email access: it asked the user to sign into their email manually, then carried on and sent the messages.

## Prompt

```text
Book an appointment with [provider or business] for [service] sometime during [dates and times that work for me]. Then draft a message to [my boss] about [topic, e.g. my being out for the appointment]. Pause and ask me to sign into my email when needed, and show me the appointment details and the message before booking or sending anything.
```
