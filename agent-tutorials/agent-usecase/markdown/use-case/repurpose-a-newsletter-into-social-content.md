# Repurpose a newsletter into social content

> One Cowork prompt turns a finished newsletter into a carousel, reel script, LinkedIn post and Substack note; it also built a Notion hub.

**Category:** Content & Creative  
**Agent:** Claude Cowork  
**Source:** taylaburrell.substack.com · Undated

## What they did

When a newsletter is finished, Tayla Burrell gives it to Cowork with one prompt, and it produces a carousel, a reel script, a LinkedIn post and a Substack note. This relies on per-platform repurposing skills, reference examples of her best past work, and a voice guide she has refined for months. Separately, she had Cowork design and populate a Notion hub for upcoming mini guides, then tweaked it by hand.

## How it went

She says the repurposing has saved her a great deal of time, though she gives no figures. The Notion hub is described only as built, with her making final manual tweaks.

## Worth knowing

Setup takes real time. Without the skills, references and voice guide, she says the output turns generic.

## Prompt

```text
Take my finished newsletter at [paste text or link] and turn it into a carousel outline, a short reel script, a LinkedIn post and a Substack note, each matching my usual voice. Organize the outputs into a Notion page or folder so I can review and edit before anything goes out. Stop once all four formats and the hub page are ready for my review.
```
