# Book restaurants and snipe campsites and tickets

> Grok Bot read calendars, picked a time and booked a restaurant; roundup also lists watching recreation.gov, ReserveCalifornia and AMC ticket drops.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** X · Undated

## What they did

Grok Bot received calendar access and booking site logins. It read schedules, selected a dinner time, booked the restaurant reservation, and negotiated in mixed Chinese and English. For sniping, users pointed separate bots at recreation.gov, ReserveCalifornia, and AMC ticket drops; the bots monitored availability and booked after a quick chat confirmation from the owner.

## How it went

The restaurant example received 937 likes and reached 4.6M views after an Elon quote-tweet. Users copied the ticket and campsite sniping patterns within a day.

## Prompt

```text
Look at my calendar and find a time that works for [number] people to have [meal] at a [type of restaurant] near [area] on [date range]. Show me the restaurant, time and booking details before you reserve anything. Also watch [recreation.gov, ReserveCalifornia or a ticket site] for [campsite or event] openings on [dates], and message me the moment something is available.
```
