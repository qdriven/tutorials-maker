# Order Starbucks by voice from a Tesla while driving

> A user had Grok Bot in a Tesla place a Starbucks order, confirming the drink and total by voice before ordering.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** Reddit · Sep 22, 2026

## What they did

The poster said they had been testing Grok Bot in a Tesla for a couple of weeks and used the in-car version, not a phone, while FSD was driving. They had already signed the bot into Starbucks, then ordered by voice; Grok confirmed the drink and total before placing the order, and the poster said “Barista” was just the name of one of their Grok Bots.

## How it went

It successfully placed a Starbucks order hands-free, and commenters noted the total shown was $7.30. The thread does not spell out the full linking flow, and the poster said this ordering setup was currently limited to paid Grok tiers.

## Worth knowing

The main catch in the thread is access: the poster said this was available to SuperGrok Heavy subscribers at the time, though other commenters said some Tesla Grok features worked on the lower $30 SuperGrok plan.
