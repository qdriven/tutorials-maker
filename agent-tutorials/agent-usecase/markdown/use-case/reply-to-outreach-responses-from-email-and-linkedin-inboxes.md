# Reply to outreach responses from email and LinkedIn inboxes

> Claude Cowork scans email and LinkedIn inboxes and replies to outreach responses, while another workflow writes first lines from prospect site and LinkedIn research.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Claude Cowork  
**Source:** Reddit · Undated

## What they did

One commenter said they switched from Claude Code to Claude Cowork and connected it to their Gmail or Apollo-sent mailbox through MCP, plus enrichment tools including Apollo, Clay, Zero Bounce, LinkedIn, and LinkedIn Jobpost. Claude Cowork scanned email every 10 minutes and LinkedIn inboxes through Phantombuster every 60 minutes, then replied to outreach responses or expressions of interest automatically.

## How it went

The thread gives no hard numbers for this specific setup, only that the commenter uses it to answer interested replies from email and LinkedIn. The source does not describe approval steps or where a human reviews messages before sending.

## Worth knowing

The clearest catch is the stack complexity: it depended on MCP connections plus Phantombuster polling LinkedIn every hour and email every 10 minutes.

## Prompt

```text
Go through my [email and LinkedIn] inbox for replies to my outreach campaign about [product/offer]. Show me all the drafts together and wait for my approval before sending any message.
```
