# Research crypto assets and execute swaps and perps trades

> The Midas Markets agent researches assets, analyzes portfolios and PnL, finds tokens by momentum, and executes swaps and perp positions on Hyperliquid.

**Category:** Money, Bills & Benefits  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** X · Sep 30, 2026

## What they did

The agent received full access to the user's portfolio data including balances, allocations, open positions, entry prices, liquidation levels and running PnL. It performed deep asset research covering price action, volume, funding, fundamentals and catalysts, conducted portfolio analysis, discovered tokens by momentum or other filters, prepared swaps with cross-chain routing and bridging, and executed perp positions including opening, closing and limit orders on Hyperliquid.
