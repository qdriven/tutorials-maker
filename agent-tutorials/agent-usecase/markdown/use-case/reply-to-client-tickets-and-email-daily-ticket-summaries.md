# Reply to client tickets and email daily ticket summaries

> An AI agent replies to new emails and tickets when a response was already sent, and emails each team member a summary of the previous day's client tickets.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** Reddit · Jan 1, 2025

## What they did

One commenter said they use SyncriTix to turn previously closed, resolved tickets into KB articles, then let its AI agent reply to new emails or tickets when a response has already been sent for that issue. Another said they pair Hatz.ai with Rewst to email each Proactive team member a daily summary of the previous day’s client tickets, with issue summaries, the tech’s resolution from the narrative, and recurring or emerging problems flagged.

## How it went

The SyncriTix user said the AI replies work about 90% of the time, though some responses are completely bizarre. The Hatz.ai and Rewst setup was described as imperfect but time-saving, replacing a once-a-week manual ticket review with near-real-time TLDR updates.

## Worth knowing

A key catch is that the summaries depend on the tech’s ticket narrative, and the auto-reply setup can still produce odd responses.

## Prompt

```text
Set up an agent for my help desk [tool name]. When a new email or ticket arrives and a reply was already sent, draft a follow-up response and save it for my review rather than sending it. Every morning, email each team member a summary of yesterday's tickets for their clients, then stop.
```
