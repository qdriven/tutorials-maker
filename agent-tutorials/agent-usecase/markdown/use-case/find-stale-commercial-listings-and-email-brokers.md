# Find stale commercial listings and email brokers

> A Hermes bot scrapes lease listings, filters by days on market and zoning, calculates commissions and emails brokers.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Hermes Agent  
**Source:** X · Apr 24, 2026

## What they did

The Hermes bot scrapes every commercial lease listing in real time. It filters by days on market, suite sqft, and zoning fit; pulls permitted-use lists from city zoning code; renders 12 AI walkthroughs of each empty suite as different tenants; calculates broker commission plus expected 5-year lease value; then sends a personalized email containing the walkthroughs. Every step from sourcing through outreach runs fully automated.

## How it went

The post states the system enables brokers to close $15k+ commissions but reports no actual results, metrics, or outcomes achieved.

## Prompt

```text
Search commercial lease listings in [city or region] and keep only those on the market more than [number] days in [zoning type] zones. For each, estimate the likely broker commission and draft a short email to the listing broker. Stop when you have a spreadsheet of listings and drafts, and show me the emails before sending any.
```
