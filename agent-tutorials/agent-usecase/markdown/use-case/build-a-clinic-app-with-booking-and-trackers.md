# Build a clinic app with booking and trackers

> A doctor with no coding experience used Replit to build a clinic app in 5 days with booking integration, prescription requests, health trackers and an AI symptom checker.

**Category:** Software & DevOps  
**Agent:** Replit Agent  
**Source:** goodreads.com · Jun 5, 2025

## What they did

A doctor with no coding experience built a clinic app on Replit in 5 days. It connects to the clinic's booking system and handles prescription requests. It has habit, nutrition, sleep, medication, workout and weight/BMI trackers, some with built-in databases. It also has two AI agents, one for medication questions and one for symptom checking, plus a premium content and course area styled to match his website.

## How it went

The doctor estimates this would have taken 1–2 years and £100–150k to build conventionally. It cost him £75 including extra usage. The source is a secondhand account of his own claim, with no detail on the prompts or workflow.

## Worth knowing

The £75 figure includes additional usage charges beyond the base plan, so budget for overage. The source gives no information on testing or medical safety checks.

## Prompt

```text
Build a simple website for my [type of practice, e.g. small clinic] with an appointment booking page, a form for [requests, e.g. prescription refills], and a basic health tracker for [metrics, e.g. blood pressure or weight]. Use placeholder data only and do not store real patient information. You're done when I can open the preview, book a test appointment, and see a tracker entry saved; pause and show me your plan before adding anything that sends emails or messages.
```
