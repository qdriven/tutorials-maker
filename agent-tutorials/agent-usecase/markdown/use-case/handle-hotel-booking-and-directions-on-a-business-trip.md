# Handle hotel booking and directions on a business trip

> A traveler used Gemini on an international business trip for last-minute hotel reservations matching company policy, plus walking directions through Heathrow and to a venue in Switzerland.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Gemini  
**Source:** X · Oct 8, 2026

## What they did

User prompted Gemini with company policy restrictions, exhaustion level, and next-day flight for an unexpected Heathrow layover hotel. It selected a compliant room and output step-by-step walking directions from arrival gate through immigration and trains. User later described visible landmarks to get navigation help to a Swiss venue.

## How it went

Hotel and directions matched needs exactly; user was surprised at the usefulness. Occasional fabrications occurred but were spotted due to prior LLM experience.

## Worth knowing

Catch was needing experience to detect when it made things up.

## Prompt

```text
I'm traveling to [city] for business from [dates]. Find hotels near [venue or address] that fit this policy: [max nightly rate, required amenities, cancellation rules], and give me the top three with prices. Also give me walking directions from [starting point, e.g. airport terminal] to [destination]. Pause before making any reservation and show me the details to confirm.
```
