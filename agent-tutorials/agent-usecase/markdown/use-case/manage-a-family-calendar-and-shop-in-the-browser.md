# Manage a family calendar and shop in the browser

> A reviewer tested Meta Muse on family calendar management (including deleting soccer practice), goal setting, a family morning newsletter and browser shopping.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** lennysnewsletter.com · Undated

## What they did

Claire Vo gave Meta's Muse access to her calendar, email and her kids' schedule, then spent a few hours on a first-pass test. She had it manage the family calendar, including deleting soccer practice, and set up goals such as sleep training. She also asked for a one-shot family morning newsletter PDF and tried two browser shopping tasks.

## How it went

The one-shot family PDF looked better than anything she had made with Claude or Codex. Browser shopping was mixed: the New Balance 9060 search went poorly, while buying IMAX tickets for The Odyssey went much better.

## Worth knowing

Browser shopping results varied a lot by task, so test a low-stakes purchase first. The source is a 38-minute episode, and the page offers only a summary and chapter list.

## Prompt

```text
Connect to my family calendar and list everything on it for the next [number] weeks, flagging conflicts between [family member names]. Then draft a short family morning summary for each day covering events, weather, and reminders. Before you delete, move, or add any event, show me exactly what you plan to change and wait for my OK.
```
