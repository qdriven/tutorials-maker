# Fill slide decks and turn notes into formatted documents

> Claude in Chrome fills a PowerPoint template from a short brief and turns scattered meeting notes into a formatted Word doc exported as PDF.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** Claude in Chrome  
**Source:** marketsmarterwithai.substack.com · Undated

## What they did

The author uses the Claude in Chrome extension in a side panel. For slides, they open their PowerPoint template, say what the presentation should cover, and Claude fills in structure, headings and body copy. For notes, they hand over scattered meeting or planning notes and ask for a clean Word document exported as PDF. The author does the final polish.

## How it went

The author says the slide work now takes a fraction of the hour it used to, and the PDF can go straight to a team member. No figures are given. The tool is slow and sometimes needs a nudge.

## Worth knowing

It needs a paid Claude plan (Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise), is still in beta, and is slow on complex tasks.

## Prompt

```text
Using this brief: [short description of the presentation, audience, and key points], fill in my slide template at [file or link] with content for each slide. Then take my meeting notes from [location] and turn them into a formatted document with headings, decisions, and action items, exported as a PDF. Stop when both files are saved and show me them for review before you share or send them.
```
