# Draft social posts from trending topics for approval

> A reviewer tested Lindy's browser-driving Computer Use to check trending topics each weekday, draft three LinkedIn posts in brand voice and send them for approval.

**Category:** Content & Creative  
**Agent:** Lindy  
**Source:** posteverywhere.ai · Undated

## What they did

The reviewer describes a Lindy agent that uses Computer Use to drive LinkedIn through a browser. The prompt asks it to check trending topics in the user's niche at 8am each weekday, draft three LinkedIn posts in brand voice, and message the user the drafts for approval. Lindy chains the steps itself, and the user designs the workflow.

## How it went

The reviewer calls it genuinely agentic and gives it 2.5 out of 4. The source reports no run results. It names caveats: browser automation breaks when platform interfaces change, and credit use is unpredictable.

## Worth knowing

Lindy is credit-based, from a free tier up to $49.99–$299.99 a month. Simple tasks cost 1 credit and multi-step research 5–10 or more, so budget for unpredictable use.

## Prompt

```text
Each weekday at [time], check trending topics in [my industry] on [LinkedIn, X, or news sites] and pick the three most relevant to my audience. Draft a LinkedIn post for each in my brand voice, using [link to past posts] as examples. Send the drafts to me at [email or Slack] for approval, and do not post anything yourself. It's done when I have three drafts to review.
```
