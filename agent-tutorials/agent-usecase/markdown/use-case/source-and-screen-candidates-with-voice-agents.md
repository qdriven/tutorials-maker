# Source and screen candidates with voice agents

> An unnamed staffing firm used voice AI agents to contact and screen candidates, reportedly reaching 2,000 in a week; weak, vendor-adjacent account.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** thehirehub.ai · Undated

## What they did

The source gives only a short account of a finance and accounting staffing firm that deployed voice AI to source and engage candidates. It doesn't name the vendor or describe the setup. Elsewhere, the article describes the general workflow: the agent calls or invites candidates, asks qualifying questions, scores the answers, and books interviews with a human recruiter, who gets a summary.

## How it went

The firm reportedly sourced 2,000 candidates in seven days, against four weeks before. It held 455 conversations and made 10 placements, and claims $215,000 in added revenue. The firm is unnamed and the figures are unverified.

## Worth knowing

The article is a vendor blog post that promotes its own platform. Treat the figures as unverified marketing claims, and note that it gives no cost or setup details.
