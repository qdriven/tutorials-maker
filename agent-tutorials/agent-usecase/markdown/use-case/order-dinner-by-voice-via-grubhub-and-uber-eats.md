# Order dinner by voice via Grubhub and Uber Eats

> A writer ordered dinner conversationally with Alexa+ through linked Grubhub and Uber Eats accounts, including customizing and changing the order.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** Alexa+  
**Source:** tomsguide.com · Undated

## What they did

The article describes the feature, not a hands-on test. You link a Grubhub or Uber Eats account in the Alexa app (More, Alexa+ Store, Food & Reservations), and saved restaurants and past orders sync. On a recent Echo Show, you then browse restaurants, build and change the cart by voice, ask menu questions, and review the cart and total before buying.

## How it went

The source reports no results or timings from an actual order. It only says the feature is starting to roll out, and it doesn't say how well the ordering worked.

## Worth knowing

It needs Alexa+ and a modern Echo Show speaker or display, and the rollout is gradual. Prime members get Grubhub+ at no cost.

## Prompt

```text
Help me order dinner for [number] people from [Grubhub or Uber Eats] near [delivery address]. Suggest a few options for [cuisine or craving], apply my customizations like [no onions, extra sauce], and let me change items as we go. Read back the full order, delivery address, and total, and wait for my explicit okay before placing it.
```
