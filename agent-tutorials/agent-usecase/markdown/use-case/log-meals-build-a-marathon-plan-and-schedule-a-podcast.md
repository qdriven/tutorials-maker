# Log meals, build a marathon plan, and schedule a podcast

> A reviewer had Meta Muse log a meal from a photo, turn a marathon goal into a PDF plan, and schedule an AI-news podcast; calorie estimates were rough.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** nocode.mba · Undated

## What they did

The reviewer gave Muse three tasks. It logged a meal from a photo after asking a follow-up about dressing. It turned a from-scratch marathon goal into a 24-week PDF plan and flagged that the goal was ambitious. It also made a scheduled two-host AI-news podcast, saved to the Library with an RSS feed for a podcast player.

## How it went

All three worked as low-setup, persistent tasks. The calorie figure was only a rough estimate, the plan was a starting point, and the podcast episode was dated two days before publication.

## Worth knowing

Check generated dates and claims on scheduled briefings. Treat calorie numbers and training plans as drafts, and don't connect a primary inbox on day one.

## Prompt

```text
I'm going to send you a photo of my meal: please log it with an estimated calorie and protein count and tell me how confident you are in the estimate. Then build a week-by-week training plan for my [race distance] on [race date], starting from [my current weekly mileage], and give it to me as a PDF. Finally, set up a [weekly/daily] [topic] news podcast summary for me at [time]; show me the plan and the schedule before you save anything.
```
