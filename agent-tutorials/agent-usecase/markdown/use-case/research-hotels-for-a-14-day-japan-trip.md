# Research hotels for a 14-day Japan trip

> Manus searched Booking.com for well-rated hotels, cross-checked reviews and transit, and returned three options per city; the author rated it 7/10 on availability handling.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Manus  
**Source:** flowith.io · Undated

## What they did

The author gave Manus a brief for 2 adults: 3-4 star hotels rated above 8.0 on Booking.com, in four cities. Checkpoint mode let it pause between phases. For each city it searched Booking.com, cross-checked TripAdvisor reviews, checked Google Maps for distance to transit, and verified prices. It returned a comparison table with links.

## How it went

The picks were useful, and two matched hotels the author had seen recommended on forums. Several had limited availability for the dates. Manus noted this but didn't factor it into its recommendations. The author scored it 7/10.

## Worth knowing

Manus couldn't check availability in real time, so confirm room availability on the booking pages before committing. The author estimated the whole planning run saved 8-12 hours.

## Prompt

```text
I'm taking a [number]-day trip to [country] visiting [cities and dates]. For each city, search Booking.com for well-rated hotels within [budget] per night, cross-check recent reviews and distance to the nearest station, and give me three options per city with price, rating, and a pro/con line. Don't book anything; end with a comparison table.
```
