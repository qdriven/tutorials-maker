# Triage inbox and prep meetings (with a failed attempt)

> A Trustpilot user said Lindy made their inbox worse by missing urgent mail and hiding meeting invites, while the review says triage, briefs and CRM updates work.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** Lindy  
**Source:** hackceleration.com · May 25, 2026

## What they did

Hack'celeration's reviewer tested Lindy, a no-code agent builder, on Gmail, Google Calendar and HubSpot. He described the work in plain English or started from a template, then let agents triage email, draft replies, write meeting prep briefs and update the CRM. The Trustpilot user who gave it a bad review had it move invites to a hidden folder, and they fixed that by hand.

## How it went

A template meeting-notes agent went live in under 15 minutes, and the review scores Lindy 3.4/5 overall. Under heavier use, reviewers reported missed urgent mail, mislabelled messages and emails sent to the wrong recipient.

## Worth knowing

Replies the agent proposes need your approval before they send, but fully automated workflows have no audit trail. Add your own confirmation step, and test on the 7-day trial first because credit costs aren't published.

## Prompt

```text
Triage my inbox [email account] each morning: flag urgent messages from [VIPs or keywords], list meeting invites separately, and draft replies for routine items. Prep a brief for each meeting on today's calendar. Don't archive, delete or send anything; give me a digest, and I'll confirm what to act on.
```
