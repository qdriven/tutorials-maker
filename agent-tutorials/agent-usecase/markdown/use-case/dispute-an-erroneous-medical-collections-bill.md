# Dispute an erroneous medical collections bill

> A finance bot logged into a healthcare account, found the EOB, spotted a wrong $13K collection bill and faxed a dispute that cleared it.

**Category:** Money, Bills & Benefits  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** X · Aug 25, 2026

## What they did

The finance bot Warren received the user’s healthcare login. It logged in, located the EOB for the year-old surgery, determined the collection agency was in error because insurance had paid, and faxed a dispute letter that included the EOB document.

## How it went

The bot targeted an unknown $13K medical collections debt and initiated the dispute process to remove it.

## Prompt

```text
I received a collections bill of [amount] from [collection agency] for a medical charge I think is wrong. Log into my [insurer or provider] account, find the relevant EOB, and check whether the bill matches what I actually owe. If it's wrong, draft a dispute letter, and show me the letter and the fax details before sending anything.
```
