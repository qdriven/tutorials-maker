# Run LinkedIn outreach from ICP and intent signals

> Grok Bot with an MCP and LinkedIn built outreach agents that found, enriched and messaged 97 prospects, yielding 15+ replies and one demo.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** X · Undated

## What they did

User connected Grok Bot to GojiberryAI MCP and LinkedIn accounts after defining a specific ICP via research on job titles, company traits, keywords, competitors, and signals. Grok Bot created outreach agents that directed Gojiberry to discover 97 matching prospects, then the workflow enriched contacts, researched each person and company, generated personalized messages, and launched LinkedIn outreach. User left the agents running with no further input.

## How it went

In under 24 hours the campaign reached all 97 prospects, secured 33 connection accepts, drew more than 15 replies, and booked one demo from a ~50-person company. Sample size was noted as too small for firm conclusions.

## Worth knowing

User stressed the value was speed from hypothesis to live test rather than the single demo result.

## Prompt

```text
Our ideal customer is [ICP description] and the intent signals to watch for are [signals]. Find matching prospects on LinkedIn, enrich them with role and company details, and draft a short personalized message for each. Pause and show me the full list and drafts before sending any messages, then report replies afterwards.
```
