# Produce a sourced research document with parallel agents

> Grok coordinated 16 specialized agents that searched and parsed 896 sources, debated and peer reviewed, and delivered a complete research document in one minute.

**Category:** Research & Knowledge  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** X · Feb 19, 2026

## What they did

Grok 4.20 Heavy coordinated a team of 16 specialized Grok 4.20 agents. The agents searched and parsed 896 sources, then ran a real-time internal debate that included peer review and consensus building. The finished research document was delivered to the user after one minute.

## How it went

It produced a complete sourced research document in one minute.

## Prompt

```text
Produce a thoroughly sourced research document on [topic or question] for [audience]. Split the work across several parallel agents to search and read sources, have them challenge each other's findings, then merge everything into one document with citations and a short executive summary. Stop when the document is complete and tell me how many sources you used and which claims are weakest.
```
