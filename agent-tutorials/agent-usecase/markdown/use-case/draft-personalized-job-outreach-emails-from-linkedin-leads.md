# Draft personalized job outreach emails from LinkedIn leads

> Pratyush Pandey gave Claude Cowork LinkedIn leads and his resume, and it drafted personalized emails per role and prepared them in Gmail.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Claude Cowork  
**Source:** X · Oct 3, 2026

## What they did

Pratyush uploaded an Excel of LinkedIn leads plus his resume to Claude Cowork. The agent drafted one personalized email per role, matching the JD to the resume, and placed each draft into Gmail ready to send. He supplied the files; the agent handled the rest without further input.

## How it went

Job outreach that had taken hours was completed in minutes.

## Prompt

```text
I'm giving you a list of LinkedIn leads in [file] and my resume at [file]. For each person, write a short personalized outreach email tied to their role and company and what I can offer. Save each as a draft in my [Gmail] and do not send any. Done when every lead has a draft and I have a summary list to review.
```
