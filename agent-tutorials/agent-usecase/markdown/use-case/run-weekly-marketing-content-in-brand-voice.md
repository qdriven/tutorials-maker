# Run weekly marketing content in brand voice

> Poster had AI write 5 LinkedIn posts, 5 X posts and 3 blog posts weekly in their brand voice and automate sends, saying website traffic doubled.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Claude  
**Source:** Reddit · May 26, 2025

## What they did

The founder, on a two-person team, set up Claude routines aimed at their marketing channels plus ProspectZero for outbound. The system auto-monitored and replied on X and LinkedIn, commented on niche blogs, generated 5 LinkedIn posts, 5 X posts, and 3 blog posts each week from a hand-written newsletter and example posts, and the founder did a quick cleanup pass before publishing.

## How it went

They said site traffic rose from about 250-300 visits a day to around 500 a day over the two-week test, with more calls booked and AI search starting to increase. Revenue did not double; they said MRR growth was around 30% and the content still needed some tweaking.

## Worth knowing

The main catch was that this was not fully hands-off: they still wrote the weekly newsletter that seeded the content and did light editing on the drafts.

## Prompt

```text
Here are 3 samples of my writing and brand voice: [paste samples]. I'm promoting [business or topic] to [audience]. Write 5 LinkedIn posts, 5 X posts and 3 blog post drafts of about [length] words for the coming week, all in this voice and each with a clear call to action. Give them to me as a numbered list that I can review and edit; don't schedule or send anything until I approve it.
```
