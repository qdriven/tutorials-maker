# Draft email campaigns in Klaviyo

> Grok Bot writes campaigns in brand voice, pulls product photos from Dropbox, builds hero art in Figma and drops an editable draft into Klaviyo.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** X · Oct 10, 2026

## What they did

The Grok Bot received brand voice instructions plus access to Dropbox product photos and Figma. It wrote full campaigns in that voice, pulled the photos, created hero artwork, and inserted an editable draft directly into Klaviyo. The user reviewed the output for five minutes then sent.

## How it went

The setup replaced the user's prior email agency. One automated campaign's subject line alone produced a click on a verified customer review.

## Worth knowing

User review takes five minutes per draft.

## Prompt

```text
Draft an email campaign in Klaviyo for [product or promotion], written in my brand voice described in [brand guide or sample emails]. Pull product photos from [Dropbox folder], create hero art in Figma, and assemble an editable draft in Klaviyo. Do not schedule or send it, and stop when the draft is ready for me to review with a link to it.
```
