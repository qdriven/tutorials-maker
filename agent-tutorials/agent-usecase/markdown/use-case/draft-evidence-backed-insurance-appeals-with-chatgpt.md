# Draft evidence-backed insurance appeals with ChatGPT

> The poster used ChatGPT to build an employment insurance appeal that was accepted and to help with an evidence-backed home-insurance flooding appeal.

**Category:** Money, Bills & Benefits  
**Agent:** ChatGPT  
**Source:** Reddit · Undated

## What they did

The poster says they used ChatGPT to draft two structured appeals: one for an employment insurance denial and one for a home-insurance flooding denial. In the EI case, they describe building an evidence-backed appeal with legal citations and tagged image evidence, then submitting it; in the flooding case, they used ChatGPT to write an appeal email with factual and legal context after the insurer initially refused to inspect.

## How it went

They say both appeals succeeded: the EI appeal led to $8,500 in payments, and the home-insurance appeal led the insurer to review the case, apologize, and cover roughly $15,000 to $20,000 in repairs. The post does not give much detail on the exact prompts or workflow.

## Worth knowing

Their main catch was that the process felt messy because they had to switch between ChatGPT, legal websites, and a document editor with no built-in structure for citations or evidence.

## Prompt

```text
Help me write an appeal letter to [insurer or agency] about [denied claim, e.g. flood damage or employment insurance decision]. I'll paste the denial letter, my policy or eligibility details, and my evidence list: [paste or describe]. Draft a clear, polite appeal that cites the specific reasons for denial, answers each with my evidence, and states the outcome I'm requesting. Also give me a checklist of attachments and the deadline to file, and flag anything you're unsure of so I can verify it before sending.
```
