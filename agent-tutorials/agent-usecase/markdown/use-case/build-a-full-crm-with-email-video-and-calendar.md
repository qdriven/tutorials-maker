# Build a full CRM with email, video and calendar

> A community member used Replit Agent over a couple of weeks to build a CRM with email integration, video sales letter recording and meeting scheduling.

**Category:** Software & DevOps  
**Agent:** Replit Agent  
**Source:** replit.discourse.group · Undated

## What they did

A tech sales professional with no coding background built GreyCRM with Replit Agent, prompting in natural language. A first version had a Twilio-based telephony system, which took a couple of hours. Over the next couple of weeks they added email integration, video recording for sales letters, and a calendar for scheduling meetings, and deployed it on Replit.

## How it went

They call it a v0.2 with core functionality present and more features planned. Agent seemed better at one-shotting features. Commenters reported the mobile layout was poor, contact creation failed for one tester, and there was no login page.

## Worth knowing

The demo runs on a Twilio account with limited credits, so the author asked visitors not to trigger lots of phone calls.

## Prompt

```text
Build a simple CRM for my [type of business] where I can store contacts, log notes and deal stages, and schedule meetings on a calendar. Start with contacts and deal stages, then add [extra feature, e.g. email sending or meeting scheduling] once the basics work. Stop when I can add a contact, move it through stages, and book a test meeting; pause and show me before it sends any real email.
```
