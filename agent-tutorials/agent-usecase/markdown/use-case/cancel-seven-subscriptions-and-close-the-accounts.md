# Cancel seven subscriptions and close the accounts

> A user describes an AI agent that cancelled seven subscriptions by completing the job and closing the accounts instead of just listing them.

**Category:** Money, Bills & Benefits  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** X · Oct 13, 2026

## What they did

The Grok Bot received a task to cancel subscriptions. It handled the full process autonomously and closed seven SaaS accounts rather than stopping after listing options.

## How it went

The user had kept postponing the task; the bot finished the job and closed the accounts.

## Prompt

```text
Here are the subscriptions I want gone: [list of services and account emails]. For each one, log in, cancel it, and close the account if the site allows it, instead of just listing it. Finished means a table of each service, whether it's cancelled and closed, and any confirmation numbers or emails. Pause and show me what you're about to do before each cancellation or account deletion, and tell me if a site needs my password or a verification code.
```
