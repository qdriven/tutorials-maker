# Write follow-ups and update CRM deals from the inbox

> Commenters say HubSpot's AI Copilot writes and sends follow-ups, updates deals, and schedules meetings, while Revenue Grid automates Salesforce CRM updates.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** Reddit · Nov 23, 2025

## What they did

This thread does not give a worked example from one team; it gives commenters’ descriptions of what these tools do. One commenter says HubSpot’s AI Copilot can write and send follow-up emails, update deal records, and schedule meetings from the inbox, while another says Revenue Grid’s stronger use case is automating Salesforce CRM updates; a different commenter adds you should still review AI-written messages manually.

## How it went

The reported upside is less manual follow-up and CRM admin, but no commenter shares a concrete rollout, metrics, or before-and-after results. Several comments also warn that personalization and closing still need human judgment.

## Worth knowing

The main catch repeated in the thread is to keep a manual check on outbound messages so they do not sound robotic or poor-quality.

## Prompt

```text
Go through my inbox for the last [number] days and find sales conversations where I owe a follow-up. For each one, draft a reply in my voice that references the previous message and proposes a next step or meeting time from my calendar. Also list the CRM deal updates each thread implies (stage, next step, notes). Show me all drafts and updates for approval before sending or changing anything.
```
