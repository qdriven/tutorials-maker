# Order groceries from Amazon Fresh and Whole Foods

> Alexa+ orders groceries, tracks past purchases and remembers recipes and dietary preferences.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** Alexa+  
**Source:** grocerydive.com · Undated

## What they did

This is a news report on Amazon's announcement, not a hands-on test. Alexa+, Amazon's generative-AI assistant, can place grocery orders from Amazon Fresh and Whole Foods Market. It also tracks past purchases. Users can ask it to remember things such as recipes and dietary preferences. It runs on a new mobile app, Alexa.com and Echo devices.

## How it went

The article reports no usage results. Amazon said rollout would begin across the U.S. in early access, then continue in waves over several months from Feb. 26.

## Worth knowing

Alexa+ is free for Prime members and $19.99 per month for nonmembers, and early access meant it might not be available to everyone yet.

## Prompt

```text
Build a grocery order from [Amazon Fresh or Whole Foods] for this week's meals: [recipes or meal ideas]. Use my past purchases and respect these dietary preferences: [preferences]. Finish with a complete cart and total, and wait for my approval before checking out.
```
