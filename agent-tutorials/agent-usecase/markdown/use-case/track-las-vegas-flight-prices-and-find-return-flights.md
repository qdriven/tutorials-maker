# Track Las Vegas flight prices and find return flights

> The author asked Muse to check Vegas fares daily and find returns to San Diego; it gave only three options and ignored his preferences, so he found it underwhelming.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** theautomatedoperator.substack.com · Sep 1, 2026

## What they did

For the outbound JSX flight, Muse navigated the booking site, read the price and set up a daily check that notifies on changes. For the return, the author gave a date window and later said he had Amex points. When he suggested British Airways award flights, Muse logged in with his credentials but got stuck on a Captcha until he pointed out it was solvable.

## How it went

The outbound tracking worked. The return search gave only three options, including Frontier, which he avoids, and later a layover flight. It wrongly said Amex points wouldn't work, and the BA search hit site errors.

## Worth knowing

Muse has no memory of your preferences, so state airline dislikes, layover limits and points programs up front. A black bar over the password field blocked its Captcha solving.

## Prompt

```text
Every morning, check flight prices from [origin city] to [destination] for [dates] and message me a short summary of the cheapest fares. Also find return options to [home airport], keeping to my preferences: [preferred airlines, time of day, max stops, budget]. Give me at least five options with prices and links, flag any price drop of more than [amount], and do not book anything without asking me first.
```
