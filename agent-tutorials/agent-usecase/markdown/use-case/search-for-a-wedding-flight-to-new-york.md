# Search for a wedding flight to New York

> The author gave Comet and ChatGPT Atlas the same flight-booking task; Comet mostly pointed to aggregators instead of specific flights.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Perplexity Comet  
**Source:** medium.com · Undated

## What they did

The author gave Comet and ChatGPT Atlas the same prompt: nonstop SFO to NYC flights leaving Friday April 24, 2026 after 2pm and returning Sunday April 26 evening. She named preferred airlines (United, JetBlue, Alaska) and preferred airports (JFK, LGA). Atlas failed on the first try, then offered browser mode. She chose logged-out browsing and watched it apply filters.

## How it went

Atlas returned 11 round-trip combinations with times, airports and prices, which was a bit much but easy to narrow. Comet gave general statements and links to aggregator sites, not specific flights.

## Worth knowing

The author says her prompting may have been at fault. Both tools failed on the first attempt, and Atlas only worked after she accepted its browser-mode offer.

## Prompt

```text
Find me specific flights from [departure city] to [destination city] arriving by [date], returning [date], for [number] travelers. Compare at least three options with airline, times, total price and a direct booking link, not just aggregator homepages. Stop at the booking page and show me the options; do not enter payment details or book anything.
```
