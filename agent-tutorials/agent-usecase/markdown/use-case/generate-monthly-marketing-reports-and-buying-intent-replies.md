# Generate monthly marketing reports and buying-intent replies

> Commenters upload SEO, social, GA4 and GSC reports into Claude for monthly update reports, and monitor Reddit, Quora and X for buying-intent signals to draft tailored replies.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Claude  
**Source:** Reddit · Undated

## What they did

One commenter said they upload SEO, social, GA4, and GSC reports into Claude each month and give it substantial business context so it can turn those inputs into a monthly update report, saving them analysis and interpretation time. Another said they monitor Reddit, Quora, and X for buying-intent threads, then have Claude draft tone-matched replies for human review before posting.

## How it went

The thread reports clear time savings rather than hard business metrics: one person said monthly reporting saves them hours, and another cut community-response work from 3–4 hours to about 20 minutes. The tradeoff mentioned elsewhere in the thread is that Claude still lacks product knowledge and campaign history, so users review and add nuance.

## Worth knowing

The main catch is context: the reporting workflow only worked well when the user supplied a lot of nuanced background each time.

## Prompt

```text
I'm uploading this month's SEO, social, GA4 and Search Console reports for [business name]. Write a monthly marketing update for [audience, e.g. my boss or client] covering the headline results, what changed versus last month, what worked, what didn't and three recommended next steps. Keep it under one page and flag any numbers you're unsure about instead of guessing.
```
