# Order bakery pickup and find couches on Marketplace

> In a multi-day test, Meta Muse filled a bakery pickup cart, pausing for final approval at checkout, and found local couches on Facebook Marketplace and followed up on a listing.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** neoteo.com · Sep 20, 2026

## What they did

In a several-day hands-on test, the reviewer asked Meta Muse to handle web errands. It added a bacon, egg, cheddar and garlic-aioli biscuit sandwich to a Kahnfections pickup cart, and checkout stopped for the user's final approval. Separately, it searched Facebook Marketplace for local couches and followed up on a listing the user liked.

## How it went

Both tasks worked as delegated browsing, and the user only had to approve checkout. Muse also kept prompting the user to connect bank accounts, email, documents and ID expiration details. The source says these were prompts, not confirmed connections.

## Worth knowing

Muse's training-on-your-interactions setting is on at first use. It can be turned off under Data controls, and Memory has no off switch in the tested version.

## Prompt

```text
Go to [bakery name or website] and fill a pickup cart with [items and quantities] for [date and time]. Stop at checkout and show me the cart, pickup time, and total before you pay. Then search Facebook Marketplace within [distance] of [city] for [item, e.g. couch] under [budget], shortlist the best [number] with price and photos, and draft a message to the seller of my top pick for me to review before sending.
```
