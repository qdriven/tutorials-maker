# Audit AI tool subscriptions from email receipts

> A tester had Muse scan connected accounts and receipts and list over a dozen AI tool subscriptions, flagging overlaps, though it missed some accounts.

**Category:** Money, Bills & Benefits  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** mindstudio.ai · Undated

## What they did

During onboarding of Meta's Muse agent, the tester connected accounts such as Gmail and calendar. They then asked it to audit AI tool subscriptions. Muse searched the connected accounts and email receipts without step-by-step instructions and compiled a list of the tools.

## How it went

It returned more than a dozen tools across image generation, video generation, automation platforms and API spend, and flagged overlaps such as multiple image generators and meeting-note tools. It missed some accounts entirely.

## Worth knowing

Check the list against your own records, since some accounts were missed. Muse's integrations were also limited to Meta platforms, email and calendar at launch.

## Prompt

```text
Scan my connected [email and accounts] for receipts and renewal emails from the past [12] months for AI tools and software subscriptions. Give me a table with service, price, billing frequency, next renewal date, and flag any that overlap in function. Note which accounts you could not check, and do not cancel or change anything.
```
