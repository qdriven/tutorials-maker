# Find online income ideas with ChatGPT agent mode

> A Forbes contributor tried ChatGPT agent mode on tasks for her online business; it returned job title suggestions but couldn't view her LinkedIn contacts. · Nov 5, 2026

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** ChatGPT Agent  
**Source:** forbes.com · Nov 5, 2026 · Submitted by from search gap: 赚钱

## What they did

Using a ChatGPT Plus account, she switched on agent mode and asked it to analyze her LinkedIn profile from its URL. She watched it open a tab and read each section. In a second task she asked for 10 decision-makers in her network who could book her for speaking. It could only list job titles, so she logged in herself and let it browse her connections.

## How it went

The profile review gave about 10 recommendations, such as adding revenue metrics to experience entries. After she logged in, the agent listed 10 contacts and an outreach template. The article reports no income results yet.

## Worth knowing

The agent may not get past an 'I'm not a robot' check on some sites, even if you take over and complete it yourself.

## Prompt

```text
I run an online business selling [product or service] to [audience]. Research [number] realistic ways I could add income, such as new offers, partnerships or job titles of buyers to target, and put them in a ranked table with effort, cost and the first step for each. Use only public sources, tell me anything you couldn't access, and stop once you have a table I can act on.
```
