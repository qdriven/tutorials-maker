# Build a mobile app turning photos and voice into reminders

> The poster built Loopi, an app that turns photos, text and voice into reminders, notes and expenses, in about 45 days using Replit and AI agents.

**Category:** Software & DevOps  
**Agent:** Replit Agent  
**Source:** Reddit · Apr 2, 2026

## What they did

The poster used Replit and its AI agents to build Loopi, a mobile app that takes a photo, pasted text, or voice input and turns it into reminders, notes, and expenses. They say they had no prior mobile app development experience, used TestFlight for over a month with a handful of testers plus their own daily use, and stepped in to judge the agents’ output because earlier agents could overdo changes or break things.

## How it went

Loopi reached a live App Store release in about 45 days. The poster says coding was not the main bottleneck; App Store submission, in-app purchases, reviews, and iteration around release slowed things down most.

## Worth knowing

They estimate spending about $500 on Replit for Loopi, and say the app was tested extensively for over a month before publishing.
