# Create a marketing plan and deploy it as a website

> A marketer had Manus produce a marketing plan with personas and HubSpot CRM steps, then build and deploy it as a website.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Manus  
**Source:** loopagency.ai · Apr 8, 2025

## What they did

The author gave Manus parameters for a marketing plan for his company and answered its clarifying questions. Manus then produced named personas, a plan based on his marketing tech stack, and HubSpot CRM workflow steps. It offered to turn the plan into a website, and he said yes. It built the pages, wrote the content, added stock images, set up navigation, tested the site and published it to a temporary server.

## How it went

The site took under 10 minutes, which the author says he couldn't have matched. He declined the final offer to publish permanently, since he didn't know where or how it would be hosted.

## Worth knowing

Manus asks before permanent publishing. The author flags brand, data security and legal risks from giving an agent free rein, so review the temporary site first.

## Prompt

```text
Build a marketing plan for [product or business] aimed at [audience], including [number] customer personas, channel strategy, and step-by-step setup instructions for [CRM tool]. Then turn the plan into a simple website and deploy it, giving me the live link. Before publishing, show me the content and tell me which facts you could not verify.
```
