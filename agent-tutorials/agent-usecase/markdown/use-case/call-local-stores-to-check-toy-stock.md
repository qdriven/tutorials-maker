# Call local stores to check toy stock

> A writer used Gemini's calling feature to phone local stores about a Labubu figure and got text or email summaries of availability, price and hours.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** Gemini  
**Source:** tech.yahoo.com · Undated

## What they did

After striking out at a few stores herself, the writer tapped the "Have AI check availability" button under a store listing in Google's AI Mode in Search Labs. She answered a few questions about the toy and how far she would travel. Gemini then phoned the store using Duplex and identified itself as an AI. She didn't listen in.

## How it went

About 40 minutes later she got a message confirming Labubu figures were in stock, with pricing and store hours. The toy was being shipped, and she hadn't received it when she wrote.

## Worth knowing

It's an experimental Search Labs feature for US users, with higher limits for AI Pro and Ultra subscribers. Businesses can opt out of AI calls.

## Prompt

```text
Please call [number] local stores near [my city or zip code] and ask whether they have [item, e.g. a Labubu figure] in stock. For each store, find out availability, price and today's closing hours, and whether they can hold one for me. When you are done, send me a short summary listing each store with what you learned. Do not reserve or pay for anything without checking with me first.
```
