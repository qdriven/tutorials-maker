# Make a dinner reservation with Operator

> Marily recorded her first try using Operator to make a dinner reservation, liking that it skipped searching and detail entry, but found it slower than expected.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Operator  
**Source:** marily.substack.com · Undated

## What they did

Marily used OpenAI's Operator for the first time and recorded the session while it booked a dinner reservation. Operator searched for the restaurant and entered the details itself, using a built-in browser she could watch live. She could take control and hand it back whenever she wanted.

## How it went

The reservation got made and she called it pretty amazing. It was slower than she expected, though it didn't bother her much. She wanted it to be more proactive, with memory, and wanted voice interaction.

## Worth knowing

The source gives no cost, timing figures or restaurant details. It is a short video post with notes, so expect impressions rather than a walkthrough.
