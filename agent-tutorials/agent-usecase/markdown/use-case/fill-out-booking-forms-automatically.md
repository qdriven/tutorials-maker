# Fill out booking forms automatically

> A reviewer had Comet fill out booking forms and a questionnaire across several sites, pausing only to confirm completion.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Perplexity Comet  
**Source:** tech.yahoo.com · Undated

## What they did

The reviewer used Perplexity's Comet browser assistant for repetitive booking forms. He gave it the details for a recycling centre appointment and told it to book. It filled in the forms itself. He then repeated this on other booking sites and a questionnaire. The assistant lists each step it takes.

## How it went

Comet worked through the forms with only occasional prompts asking whether he still wanted to finish the booking. The reviewer admits the task is unnecessary, but says he found it satisfying. He gives no timings or error rates.

## Worth knowing

Comet checks with you before completing a booking or purchase, so expect to confirm at the end, and review its step log, since it handles your accounts.

## Prompt

```text
Fill out the booking forms on these pages for me: [list of URLs]. Use these details: [name, email, phone, address, preferred dates]. Before you submit any form, pause and show me what you entered on each one so I can confirm.
```
