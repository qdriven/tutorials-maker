# Find buyer-intent leads and research them for outreach

> One bot spots buyer-intent signals and a second judges each opportunity and finds contacts, feeding a list for human email outreach.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** Reddit · Oct 10, 2026

## What they did

A commenter said they run two bots: the first scans the web with multiple sub-agents for more than 30 buyer-intent signals, such as companies hiring many of the same roles or rumors of an announcement. The second bot reviews those signals, scores each one as a good or bad opportunity, and finds relevant people to contact; the human then uses the resulting weekly lead list for manual email outreach rather than AI-written messages.

## How it went

They said this is working for them because prospects are more receptive when buyer intent is already present and the outreach email is written by a person. The thread gives no conversion numbers, setup details, or examples of the contact-finding process.

## Worth knowing

The clearest catch is not to automate the outreach itself: the commenter says they keep emails human-written and use AI mainly for web reading, list-building, and sorting.

## Prompt

```text
Find people or companies showing buyer intent for [my product or service], such as posts asking for [problem I solve] on [Reddit, X, forums]. For each one, judge how strong the fit is, find the likely decision-maker and public contact info, and add them to a spreadsheet with a note on why they're a good lead. Stop at [number] qualified leads and don't contact anyone yourself.
```
