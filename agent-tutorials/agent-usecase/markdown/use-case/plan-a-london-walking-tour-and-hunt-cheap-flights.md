# Plan a London walking tour and hunt cheap flights

> The author used Comet as default browser for a month to plan a walking tour, search flights in set windows and draft emails, noting where it struggled.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Perplexity Comet  
**Source:** pub.towardsai.net · Undated

## What they did

Felix Pappe made Perplexity's Comet agentic browser his default for 30 days and used it as a search assistant. Tasks included summarising long YouTube videos, planning a one-day walking tour through London, searching for cheap flights within specific time windows, and drafting real emails.

## How it went

He says it saved time in some places and struggled in others, pointing to its screenshot-driven workflow as a weak spot. The accessible text gives no per-task results or numbers.

## Worth knowing

The article is member-only and only the intro was readable, so check the full piece for the specific London and flight results and the pitfalls.
