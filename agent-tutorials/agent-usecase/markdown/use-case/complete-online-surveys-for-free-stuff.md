# Complete online surveys for free stuff

> A user had Grok Bot fill out a survey to get free stuff; it has worked once so far.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** Reddit · Sep 4, 2026

## What they did

The poster says they had a Grok bot handle a Burger King receipt survey after getting a code on the receipt. They gave the bot the task, and about 30 minutes later it messaged back a link to download the coupon for the free Whopper; the post does not describe any other setup or human steps.

## How it went

It worked once: the bot completed the survey and returned the free-coupon link. In comments, another user who tried scaling this to web reward surveys said it was too complicated and used too many tokens to be worth it so far.

## Worth knowing

The only concrete timing in the thread is roughly 30 minutes from assigning the survey to getting the coupon link.

## Prompt

```text
Fill out this survey for me using the answers I'd realistically give: [survey link]. Use my profile details [age range, location, interests] and don't invent anything unusual. Before you submit, show me the answers and whether it asks for payment or sensitive info, then tell me what reward to expect.
```
