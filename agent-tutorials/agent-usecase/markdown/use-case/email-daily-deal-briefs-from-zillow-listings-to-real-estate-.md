# Email daily deal briefs from Zillow listings to real estate agents

> A Zillow scraper feeds Claude to produce daily briefs on new listings, price cuts and outreach scripts, emailed to 47 paying real estate agents.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Claude  
**Source:** X · Jun 17, 2026

## What they did

A Zillow scraper feeds yesterday's listings into Claude each morning. Claude generates a one-page Deal Intel Brief listing five same-day showings, price cuts that indicate motivated sellers, and three copy-paste outreach scripts. The finished brief is emailed automatically at 6:45am; the operator only manages subscriptions and sales.

## How it went

47 agents pay $149 monthly, producing $10,237 in revenue last month at a total stack cost of $74. No shortfalls are stated.

## Worth knowing

The entire stack costs $74 per month.
