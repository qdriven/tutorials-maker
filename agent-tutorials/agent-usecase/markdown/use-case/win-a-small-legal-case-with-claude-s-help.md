# Win a small legal case with Claude's help

> The poster said Claude helped them win an $8,000 legal case at the cost of only the filing fee; details are sparse.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Claude  
**Source:** Reddit · Jan 6, 2026

## What they did

After getting nowhere for six months and finding a rural attorney unhelpful, the poster used Claude Opus 4.5 to research the dispute, plan the case, and draft a civil suit they filed pro se in district court. They say they personally checked every statute and case citation Claude surfaced before using it, then argued the case themselves before a judge against a county government.

## How it went

The poster says they won an $8,000 civil case and that Claude found relevant references without hallucinations in this instance. Details on the legal theory, filings, and judgment are still sparse because they chose to stay vague until things were finalized.

## Worth knowing

Their stated out-of-pocket cost was only the court filing fee, but they stress they manually verified every reference rather than trusting Claude on its own.
