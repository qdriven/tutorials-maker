# Send a daily competitor ad research report to Slack

> @cjfeth's Manus automation mines target avatar data, scrapes competitor ad libraries, benchmarks them and sends a report of underserved areas to Slack.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Manus  
**Source:** X · Mar 18, 2026

## What they did

The Manus agent runs daily in the background. It mines data from target avatar locations, segments findings into angles, concepts, failures and awareness maps, scrapes competitors’ ad libraries for text and headlines, benchmarks against the mined data to spot underserved areas, compiles a master report and posts it to Slack. The user sets up the automation and receives the reports.

## How it went

It automates research and idea delivery to creative strategists, increasing their output. No specific metrics or limitations are stated.

## Prompt

```text
Every morning at [time], research [target customer description], then scrape the public ad libraries of these competitors: [competitor list]. Benchmark their messaging, offers, and formats, and identify angles that look underserved. Send a short report to Slack channel [channel name] with the top [number] opportunities and links to example ads. Show me a sample report and the Slack message before turning on the daily schedule.
```
