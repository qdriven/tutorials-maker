# Plan and book a movie date night

> ChatGPT Agent picked seats on a theater site, paused for payment details, bought tickets after confirmation, and created a calendar event.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** ChatGPT Agent  
**Source:** techradar.com · Undated

## What they did

The author gave ChatGPT Agent one request: tickets to Superman at a favorite theater, calendar entries for childcare drop-off and showtime, and an invitation for his wife. The agent asked about showing and seats, then opened the theater site and picked seats. It paused so he could enter payment details, and he confirmed twice. Through a linked calendar it created events and sent invites, and it built a Slides invitation.

## How it went

The whole job took under ten minutes. The agent first misidentified the theater and had to be corrected, and the invitation's wording was overblown.

## Worth knowing

You must type payment details yourself, and the agent asks for confirmation before buying. Check that it has the right theater, since it guessed wrong at first.

## Prompt

```text
Plan a movie date night for [date] in [city]: find showtimes for [movie or genre] around [time] at [theater], and pick two good seats together. Stop and let me enter payment details myself, and show me the full order summary before you buy anything. After I confirm, add the showtime to my calendar and invite [partner's email].
```
