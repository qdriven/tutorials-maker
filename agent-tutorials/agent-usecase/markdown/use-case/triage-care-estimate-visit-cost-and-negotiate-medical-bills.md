# Triage care, estimate visit cost, and negotiate medical bills

> An app directs people to the right site of care, estimates insurance-based visit costs, and uses an AI voice agent to negotiate bills down after the visit (US only).

**Category:** Money, Bills & Benefits  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** HN · Nov 10, 2025

## What they did

The source is a brief comment from the builder describing CareRoute. The app triages a person to the right site of care. It then estimates their visit cost based on their insurance before they go. After the visit, an AI voice agent negotiates the bill on the person's behalf.

## How it went

The source gives no results, savings figures, or user numbers. It says only that triage works in all regions, while cost estimates and bill negotiation are US-only.

## Prompt

```text
I have [symptom or situation] and insurance with [plan name]. Help me work out the right kind of care (urgent care, ER, primary care, or telehealth), list in-network options near [city or ZIP], and estimate my likely cost. Finish with a short comparison table, and don't book or call anything without showing me first.
```
