# Research market trends and deliver an editable document

> A reviewer had Manus research 2025 coffee market trends from live sources and produce an editable .doc file.

**Category:** Research & Knowledge  
**Agent:** Manus  
**Source:** cybernews.com · Undated

## What they did

The reviewer gave Manus one prompt asking for 2025 coffee market trends, including popular brands and new consumer habits. Manus browsed live websites, pulled data and summarized it while the reviewer watched. Without being asked, it delivered the findings as a downloadable .doc file that could be edited directly. The source describes no other human involvement.

## How it went

It finished in about 50 seconds and used 59 of the 300 free daily credits. The reviewer called the output solid and well structured, and gave no accuracy check of the data.

## Worth knowing

The free tier's 300 daily credits covered this task easily, but the reviewer says heavy usage burns credits quickly and the agent sometimes assumes details that need correcting.

## Prompt

```text
Research current trends in the [industry or market, e.g. coffee] market for [year or region] using live, reputable sources. Write a clear report covering market size, key trends, major players, and risks, with a source link for each major claim. Deliver it as an editable .doc file and stop when the file is complete.
```
