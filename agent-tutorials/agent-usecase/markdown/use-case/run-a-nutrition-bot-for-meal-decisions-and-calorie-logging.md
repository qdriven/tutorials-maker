# Run a nutrition bot for meal decisions and calorie logging

> Zack built health agents, including a nutrition bot for his mom that handles meal decisions and calorie logging to support her weight loss.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** X · Oct 5, 2026

## What they did

Zack created health agents for his mom including a nutrition bot. The bot handles meal decisions and calorie logging so she can focus on her health and weight loss without stressing about the nuances of those tasks.

## How it went

She is putting her nutrition bot to good use.

## Prompt

```text
Build me a nutrition assistant for [person's name] who is working toward [goal, e.g. losing weight, about X calories a day]. Through [SMS/WhatsApp/Telegram], she should be able to send meal photos or descriptions and get a calorie estimate, a log entry in [Google Sheet/app], and a quick suggestion for what to eat next. Send a short daily summary at [time] with calories against the target. Before it sends any messages to anyone other than her, pause and show me what it plans to send.
```
