# Dodge car dealership upsells and find gift cards

> A user said Muse helped avoid upsells and fees at a car dealership, saving $1,250, and another said it found an unused $200 Verizon gift card.

**Category:** Money, Bills & Benefits  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** uk.news.yahoo.com · Undated

## What they did

Users of Meta's Muse agent, as part of Alexandr Wang's #MuseMoneyChallenge, prompted it to review their finances and find savings. Coinbase product manager Nick Prince said Muse helped him dodge dealership upsells and a large fee. Another user said it turned up an unused Verizon gift card. The source gives no detail on prompts or steps.

## How it went

Prince reported saving $1,250 at the dealership, and another user reported finding a $200 Verizon gift card. These are self-reported posts, and many challenge posts came from Meta's own staff.

## Worth knowing

Amazon blocked Muse from placing orders in its shopping carts, so agent purchasing on some sites may be restricted.

## Prompt

```text
I'm buying a [car make and model] at [dealership]. Here is the dealer's quote: [paste quote or attach file]. Also search my email and files for unused gift cards or credits from [stores or carriers], and list what you find with amounts and expiry dates.
```
