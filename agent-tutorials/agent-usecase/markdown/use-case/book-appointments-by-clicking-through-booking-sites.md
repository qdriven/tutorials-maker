# Book appointments by clicking through booking sites

> ChatGPT Agent Mode opens a real browser and books appointments by clicking through the booking site; the post gives a setup guide.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** ChatGPT Agent  
**Source:** Reddit · Aug 8, 2026

## What they did

The post says to start ChatGPT agent mode from the tools or + menu, or by typing /agent, then give it the booking task, either the booking URL or instructions to find a provider, your availability, and an instruction to stop before confirming or entering personal details. In the browser it opens inside chat, the agent clicks through calendars and checks slots on its own, while you step in for logins, CAPTCHA checks, and the final personal or payment fields.

## How it went

According to the post, it can handle bookings like dentists, car service, tables, classes, or viewings, and usually takes five to thirty minutes to work through a site. The catch is that it is limited by plan access and the author says it should not finalize the booking for you.

## Worth knowing

The practical limit the post gives is that Plus users get roughly 40 agent runs a month, so this is best saved for bookings you really want to avoid.

## Prompt

```text
Open [booking site URL] and book a [type of appointment] for me on [preferred date and time window]. Use my name [name] and contact [email or phone]. Before you submit the booking or enter any payment details, pause and show me the chosen slot and the details; you're done when I approve and you've captured the confirmation.
```
