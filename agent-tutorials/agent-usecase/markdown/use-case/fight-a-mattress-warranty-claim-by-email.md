## What they did

User emailed the mattress company about a two-year-old warranty issue and spotted ChatGPT-style replies after one or two exchanges. They then set their own agent to handle the ongoing email thread, giving it instructions to cite laws and respond as an aggressive pain-in-the-ass customer while the company's side stayed polite.
