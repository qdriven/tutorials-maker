# Run founder-led sales with single-job bots

> A founder uses an outreach bot staging daily cold emails for approval, an inbox bot ranking and drafting replies, and a social bot passing warm leads on.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** x.ai · Undated

## What they did

Simon Lackowski installed a Founder Mode pack of one chief-of-staff bot and eight single-job bots, connected to Gmail and Calendar (drafts, read-only), a deal-tracker CSV, and LinkedIn and X (read and draft only). Outreach Bot stages a 7am batch, skipping anyone in an active deal. Inbox Bot ranks and drafts replies. Social Bot passes engaged people on as warm leads. He approves everything.

## How it went

In his sample week, Outreach Bot staged 8 email and LinkedIn pairs overnight, and call briefs and recap drafts were ready on time. The source gives no conversion or reply-rate figures.

## Worth knowing

Onboarding asks about 20 questions, and all 16 routines stay paused until you confirm a schedule. Nothing sends, posts or merges without approval.

## Prompt

```text
Help me with founder-led sales using three single-job bots. One stages [number] cold emails each morning to [target audience] for my approval, one ranks my inbox and drafts replies, and one watches [social platform] for warm leads and passes them to me. Never send anything without showing it to me first.
```
