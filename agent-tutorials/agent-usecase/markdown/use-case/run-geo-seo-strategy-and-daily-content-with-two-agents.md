# Run GEO/SEO strategy and daily content with two agents

> A founder used two internal agents, one for GEO/SEO diagnosis and strategy and one for daily content, and reports 100x traffic in 30 days with no ad spend.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** Reddit · Jan 1, 2026

## What they did

The founder says they ran a 30-day test on their own site using two internal agents: one audited SEO, AI-engine visibility, technical structure, social signals, and competitors, then produced a scored report and a 5-week ROI-ranked to-do list. They manually executed only the top technical and metadata fixes, while a second agent generated 1–2 GEO-formatted posts per day for blogs, LinkedIn, X, Reddit, and structured answers; the system also monitored rankings, citations, and technical health and suggested adjustments every few days.

## How it went

They report all-organic gains of +9,380% active users, +9,604% new users, and +16,614% total views, with daily organic traffic reaching 1,000–5,000 and no ad spend. The post gives little hard evidence beyond the screenshots and stays high-level on exact implementation details.

## Worth knowing

Their claimed human workload for the initial prioritized fixes was under two hours, and Reddit promotion still needed subreddit analysis to avoid looking spammy.

## Prompt

```text
Act as my GEO/SEO strategist for [my site URL]. Review the site and [competitor URLs], then write a diagnosis of where I'm missing visibility in search and AI answer engines, plus a prioritized plan for the next 30 days. Then draft [one] piece of content for each day of week one from that plan, saved as separate files in a folder called [folder name]. Do not publish anything; show me the drafts and wait for my approval.
```
