# Call insurers for appeal status updates

> Counterforce Health's voice-controlled agent Maxwell is designed to contact insurers for appeal status; the platform is free to patients and caregivers.

**Category:** Money, Bills & Benefits  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** en.wikipedia.org · Undated

## What they did

The source only says Counterforce Health is developing Maxwell, a voice-controlled agent meant to contact insurers for appeal status updates. It sits alongside the company's main web platform. That platform reads denial letters, policies and medical records, then drafts appeal letters. The source doesn't describe how Maxwell works.

## How it went

The source gives no results for Maxwell. It reports only on the letter tool: an estimated 70% claim reversal rate (ABC11) and use by thousands of patients and several clinics.

## Worth knowing

The platform is free to patients and caregivers, paid for by grants and donations, but clinics may be charged for advanced features later.

## Prompt

```text
Set up an assistant that checks on my insurance appeal for [claim or appeal number] with [insurer name] at [phone number or portal URL]. Every [number] business days, have it look up the current status, and send me a short update with the status, date, and any next steps requested. Pause and ask me before it submits any form, sends any message to the insurer, or shares personal details.
```
