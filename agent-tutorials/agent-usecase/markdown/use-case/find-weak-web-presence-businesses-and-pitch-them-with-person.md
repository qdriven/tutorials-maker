# Find weak-web-presence businesses and pitch them with personalized outreach

> Six agents find businesses with weak online presence, write personalized outreach, and produce scripts, voiceovers and talking-head videos to sell web services.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** Reddit · Mar 1, 2026

## What they did

The Reddit post lays out six specialized agents in a pipeline: one finds local businesses with weak or outdated websites, another researches each business and drafts personalized outreach, and later agents turn that pitch into sales assets including scripts, voiceovers, and talking-head videos. The poster says the agents run from fixed instructions and repeat the workflow automatically, with the human mainly setting them up and presumably handling the overall business side.

## How it went

The post presents it as a 24/7 web-services sales system, but the fetched thread excerpt does not give hard performance numbers or verified revenue in the visible source. Comments push on proof, including asking for the last 12 months of income and profits.

## Worth knowing

The biggest catch from the visible thread is that it describes the workflow at a high level but, in the fetched source, does not provide concrete results or economics to benchmark against.

## Prompt

```text
Find [number] local [business type] in [city] with a weak online presence, such as no website, an outdated site, or few reviews. For each, note what is missing and write a short personalized outreach email offering [your web service]. Finish with a spreadsheet of the businesses, the gaps, and the email drafts. Do not send any messages or submit any forms; show me the list and drafts first.
```
