# Produce a finished music release from one chat

> Claude Code wrote lyrics, generated and mastered the song, made cover art and a 10-second video via an MCP connector in about 6 minutes; clip joined with ffmpeg outside. · Oct 11, 2026

**Category:** Content & Creative  
**Agent:** Claude Code, Claude  
**Source:** media.aetherwavestudio.com · Oct 11, 2026 · Submitted by Aetherwave Studio

## What they did

Claude Code, connected to the AetherWave Studio connector, wrote the lyrics in the chat. It then made four tool calls: two song takes with Suno V5.5, cover art with the title lettered on it, a 10-second 1080p clip animated from the cover with Seedance Pro, and a streaming-preset master. Files saved to the account's gallery.

## How it went

All four calls worked first try in about 6 minutes for 129 credits (about $1.29), and the cover title was spelled correctly. The video is one 10-second clip looped under the song, not a multi-shot edit.

## Worth knowing

The song and clip were joined with ffmpeg outside the connector. Mastering cost 0 only because the plan includes it; otherwise it is 20 credits.

## Prompt

```text
Help me make a short music release about [theme or mood] in the style of [genre]. Write the lyrics, generate the song with my connected music tool, create cover art, and make a 10-second video clip to go with it. Save all the files in one folder and list what you made. Do not publish or upload anything anywhere. Show me the results and wait for my feedback.
```
