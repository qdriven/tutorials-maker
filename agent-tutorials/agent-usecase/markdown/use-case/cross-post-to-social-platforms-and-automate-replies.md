# Cross-post to social platforms and automate replies

> The poster used a free Make workflow to repost content across Reddit, Facebook and X, and also tried AI-automated community replies.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Make  
**Source:** Reddit · Apr 22, 2026

## What they did

The poster tested three automations. The one relevant here was a Make.com workflow on the free plan that reposted content from one source to Reddit, Facebook, and X instead of posting manually at different times; they also tried AI-generated community replies, but stopped after readers reacted badly, keeping engagement human.

## How it went

Cross-posting cut posting time from about 45 minutes a day to 10 minutes. The reply automation failed fast: people noticed, they got 2 negative comments in one day, and shut it off.

## Worth knowing

Their practical takeaway was to automate scheduling, not audience interaction; the free Make plan was enough to start.

## Prompt

```text
Build a Make scenario that watches [my source, such as a blog RSS feed or Reddit account] and, when I publish something new, reposts it to [Reddit subreddit, Facebook page, X account] with formatting suited to each platform. Run a test with a recent post and show me the output for each platform. Don't publish anything live and don't turn on automated replies until I've approved the test output.
```
