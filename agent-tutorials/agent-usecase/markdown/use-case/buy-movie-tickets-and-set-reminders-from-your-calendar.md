# Buy movie tickets and set reminders from your calendar

> A custom Meta Muse skill checks the calendar, confirms movie times and seats, buys tickets, adds the booking to the calendar, and sends a traffic-aware departure reminder.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** X · Oct 8, 2026

## What they did

User asked their Meta Muse agent to build a custom skill for consistent theater bookings. The skill reviews the user's calendar, confirms suitable movie dates and times, recalls preferred seat locations, purchases the ticket via the built-in Link integration and wallet, adds the reservation to the calendar, and sends a departure reminder that factors in traffic.

## How it went

The skill executes the full sequence on demand with no further reported outcomes or metrics.

## Prompt

```text
Check my calendar for a free evening this week, then find showtimes for [movie] at [theater or area] that fit. Pick good seats for [number] people, and before you buy anything, show me the time, seats, and total price and wait for my OK. After I approve, buy the tickets, add the booking to my calendar, and set a reminder to leave that accounts for traffic from [starting location].
```
