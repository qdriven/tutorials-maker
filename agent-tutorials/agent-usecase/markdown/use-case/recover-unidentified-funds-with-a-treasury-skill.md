# Recover unidentified funds with a treasury skill

> Cornell finance and AI teams used a treasury Claude skill to identify and claim unidentified funds, recovering $100k in back payments.

**Category:** Money, Bills & Benefits  
**Agent:** Claude  
**Source:** X · Jun 23, 2026

## What they did

Cornell's finance and AI teams created a /treasury Claude skill. The skill was deployed to identify and claim unidentified funds.

## How it went

It recovered $100k in back payments.

## Prompt

```text
I work in finance at [organization]. Here is an export of [bank deposits / payments received / ledger entries] for [time period]: [paste or attach the data]. Help me identify unidentified or unapplied funds, match each to a likely payer or invoice, and rank them by amount. Finish with a table of what to claim or follow up on and a draft message for each, and don't contact anyone until I approve.
```
