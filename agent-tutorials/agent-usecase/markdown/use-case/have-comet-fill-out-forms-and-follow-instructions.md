# Have Comet fill out forms and follow instructions

> Poster says Comet's agentic mode followed instructions, including commanding other AI tools, and filled out a form for them.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Perplexity Comet  
**Source:** Reddit · Apr 1, 2025

## What they did

The poster says Comet’s agentic mode followed detailed instructions, including telling other AI tools what to do and filling out a form for them. In the comments, another user gives the clearest concrete example: Comet took over the browser, clicked through 18 training modules, watched them, opened each quiz, and retried questions it missed until it passed.

## How it went

People in the thread report it completed tasks successfully, including all 18 modules in one assessment flow and form-filling. The main limits mentioned were slower-than-manual speed, occasional wrong quiz answers on the first try, and missing OCR/Google Lens-style features.

## Worth knowing

One commenter said the assessment run was slower than doing it manually, and another said you need the Pro plan after hitting an assistant usage limit.

## Prompt

```text
Open [form URL] in this browser and fill it out using these details: [name, email, and other answers]. Follow any instructions on the page about format or required fields. Stop before submitting and show me every answer you entered so I can approve it, then submit only after I say go.
```
