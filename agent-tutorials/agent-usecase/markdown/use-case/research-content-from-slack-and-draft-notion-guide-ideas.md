# Research content from Slack and draft Notion guide ideas

> A team DM'd Lindy in Slack to summarize a year of guides and research a model, and it created a Notion guide idea after asking for approval.

**Category:** Research & Knowledge  
**Agent:** Lindy  
**Source:** app.therundown.ai · Undated

## What they did

With Slack connected, the team DM'd Lindy to review this year's Rundown University guides and summarize where AI products are heading and what content they make. In a second request they asked it to research Claude 5.1 and turn it into a Notion guide idea. Lindy asked for approval before creating the page, then filled in the database properties, added the source, and posted the Notion link in Slack.

## How it went

The guide analysis found shifts toward agents that do work, rising coding agents, OpenAI and Claude dominance, and outcome-first framing. The Notion page was created correctly after approval. The guide gives no quantified results for these two tasks.

## Worth knowing

Keep approvals on for anything Lindy creates, sends, or updates through someone's connected account. Heavier builds run in the background; a Monday staff deck took about 10 to 15 minutes.

## Prompt

```text
When I message you in Slack, research [topic or model] and summarize [past content, e.g. last year's guides] in a short brief with sources. Then propose a guide idea and ask for my approval before creating a page in [Notion database]. Once I approve, create the page and link it back in Slack.
```
