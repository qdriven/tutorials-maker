# Write Meta campaign copy as a senior copywriter

> A poster had Manus act as a senior direct-response copywriter to generate ad copy for a Meta campaign.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Manus  
**Source:** Reddit · Nov 1, 2025

## What they did

The poster gave Manus a single prompt to act as a senior direct-response copywriter for a fictional Meta campaign for a 12-week Python bootcamp, with a target audience, lead goal, and three required ad angles. Manus returned three Facebook ad variations with headlines, primary text, a CTA, and visual creative suggestions; the person’s role was just to frame the brief and judge the output from the generated example shown.

## How it went

The post says the copy was generated instantly, and the author thought the pain-point ad was genuinely impressive because it matched Meta ad structure and added creative direction. But the thread shows pushback: a commenter said it showed a poor understanding of a Facebook ad.

## Prompt

```text
Act as a senior direct-response copywriter. Write ad copy for a Meta campaign promoting [product/offer] to [target audience], with the main benefit being [key benefit] and the tone being [tone]. Deliver 5 primary texts, 5 headlines, and 3 description lines, each tagged with the angle it uses, and keep within Meta's character limits.
```
