# Build a profitable online business from scratch with an agent

> Poster gave an OpenClaw/Claude agent $50 and 60 days to build an online business, using content marketing on Reddit, Twitter and LinkedIn that drew 400+ visitors.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** OpenClaw, Claude Code  
**Source:** Reddit · Mar 1, 2026

## What they did

The poster put an OpenClaw/Claude agent in a workspace with a personality config, a $50 budget, and a 60-day goal to start an online business with no audience. The agent explored business models, bought a domain, built a site, wrote a 58,000-word guide, then after 12 days of no sales used traffic/conversion data to pivot to a done-for-you agent setup service, adding a free diagnostic, email course, and four sub-agents for social, analytics, customer monitoring, and creative work.

## How it went

It became profitable on day 15, with two overnight sales worth over $600 after organic Reddit posts drove traffic; the post says both buyers spent time with the free guide first. The original paid guide sold nothing in 12 days, Reddit ads got 35 clicks and 0 conversions, and a broken email form wasted 800+ visitors until fixed.

## Worth knowing

Their clearest catch was instrumentation: a silent CORS bug broke email capture for about a week, so 800+ visitors produced zero signups until a server-side proxy fix.

## Prompt

```text
I want to test building a small online business in [niche or idea] with a budget of $[amount] and [number] days. Research the market, pick one simple offer, and draft a content plan with posts for [Reddit / Twitter / LinkedIn] plus a basic landing page. Finish with a written plan and the drafts. Before posting anything publicly, sending any message, or spending any money, stop and show me exactly what you're about to do and wait for my approval.
```
