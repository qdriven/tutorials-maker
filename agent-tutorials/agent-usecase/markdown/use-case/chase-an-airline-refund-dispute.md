# Chase an airline refund dispute

> An agent sent firm follow-ups every 48 hours, built a paper trail and prepped a regulator grievance; the $1,200 flight refund was processed within a week.

**Category:** Money, Bills & Benefits  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** X · Sep 28, 2026

## What they did

User handed the full $1,200 flight refund dispute to the Instinct agent after two months of airline silence and stall tactics. The agent sent automated firm follow-ups every 48 hours to keep the thread alive, logged every broken promise and missed callback into a clean paper trail, and prepared a formal grievance letter to the aviation regulator.

## How it went

The airline processed the full refund within one week.

## Prompt

```text
Help me get a refund of [amount] from [airline] for [flight details and booking reference]. Draft a firm follow-up email every 48 hours until they respond, keep a dated log of every message, and prepare a complaint for [regulator name] if there is no result after [number] days. Pause and show me each email or complaint before it is sent, and finish when the refund is confirmed or the complaint is ready.
```
