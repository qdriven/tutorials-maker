# Run a commercial real estate business with an AI agent

> Drew McAllister's agent flagged counteroffers by text, built owner follow-up lists, tracked bids, drafted emails, read lease redlines and flagged deadline conflicts.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** X · Oct 1, 2026

## What they did

Agent lived in inbox and calendar. It monitored emails to text counteroffer details on arrival, built owner follow-up lists and best-and-final trackers, drafted escrow emails and thank-you notes, extracted names from 6 months of email plus 13 contact screenshots into a protected list, flagged lease redline issues and deadline conflicts like SBA funding, and wrote Claude prompts from its outputs. User reviewed and sent everything.

## How it went

Ran 8 days with no reversals required; prevented at least one closing conflict and kept buyer-agent relationships active after selection.

## Prompt

```text
Act as my assistant for my [commercial real estate] business. Watch my email for counteroffers and text me when one arrives, build a follow-up list of [property owners] from my notes, track open bids, and flag any lease redline or deadline conflicts. Draft emails for me but do not send any until I approve them.
```
