# Run a small business calendar and daily briefing

**Category:** Work & Operations

## What they did

In the thread, the detailed calendar use came from a commenter describing their setup, not the original poster. They linked Muse to email so it could add and delete calendar events, then used it for a daily briefing delivered as both text and a voice memo; the same setup also pulled in Apple Health sleep data.

## How it went

The commenter presents it as part of their regular routine and says it works alongside other tracking tasks, but gives no business metrics, time saved, or failures. The post does not explain the exact workflow for approvals or corrections.

## Worth knowing

The only concrete constraint mentioned anywhere in the thread is that one user said they had not exceeded Muse’s daily free limit.
