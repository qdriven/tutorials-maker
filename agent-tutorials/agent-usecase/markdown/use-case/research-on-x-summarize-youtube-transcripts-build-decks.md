# Research on X, summarize YouTube transcripts, build decks

> @Tape_Vector uses Comet for X research, summarizing YouTube video transcripts and building presentations.

**Category:** Research & Knowledge  
**Agent:** Perplexity Comet  
**Source:** X · Oct 3, 2026

## What they did

User subscribed to Comet as the AI agent browser and gave it access to X and YouTube. The agent browsed X posts for research results on its own, and on YouTube it extracted video transcripts then generated summaries. Person directed the outputs into Google Workspace to build presentations in Drive and Sheets.

## How it went

Comet delivered reliable X research and YouTube summaries that the user incorporated into decks. No quantitative results or shortfalls are stated.

## Prompt

```text
Research [topic] on X by finding the most relevant recent posts and threads from [accounts or keywords], then summarize the main points. Next, open these YouTube videos [links] and summarize each transcript in five bullets. Finish by turning everything into an outline for a [number]-slide presentation with titles and key points per slide. Don't post or share anything.
```
