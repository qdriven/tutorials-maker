# Handle executive admin: email drafts, bookkeeper questions, insurance forms

> A Dots agent declined an invitation, drafted a lawyer email, answered bookkeeper questions, and filled an insurance broker form by finding the lease and budget documents, saving about two hours.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** Dots  
**Source:** platformer.news · Undated

## What they did

Casey Newton gave his Dot, named Kicker, access to his work accounts and ran it in the background as a running chat in ChatGPT while he wrote. It handled routine email and bookkeeper questions. For the insurance broker's form, it found the lease, searched city websites, checked budget documents, and asked him follow-up questions. He gave permission before it emailed his speaking agent.

## How it went

Kicker answered all but two of the broker's questions. Newton estimates it did about two hours of work for about 15 minutes of his effort. This was a limited test, only a couple of hours.

## Worth knowing

Dots is limited to paid ChatGPT users (he mentions about $100 a month), and it needs broad access to email and financial data, so he suggests starting with a lower-stakes connection such as Slack.

## Prompt

```text
Handle these admin tasks from my inbox and files: decline the invitation from [sender/event], draft an email to [lawyer name] about [topic], answer the bookkeeper's questions from [bookkeeper name] using [documents like lease or budget], and fill in the [insurance broker] form using the same documents. Show me every draft and the completed form before anything is sent or submitted, and list which documents you used for each answer. You're done when each task has a draft or filled form waiting for my approval.
```
