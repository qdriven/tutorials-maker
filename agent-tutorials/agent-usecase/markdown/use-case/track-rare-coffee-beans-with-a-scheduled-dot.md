# Track rare coffee beans with a scheduled Dot

> A user had a Dot monitor rare US coffee beans twice a day using context from old chats, and it returned Gesha options priced per cup.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** Dots  
**Source:** grokbot.sh · Undated

## What they did

A ChatGPT Pro subscriber named their Dot "豆哥" and asked it to track rare US coffee beans. The Dot drew on context from the user's old ChatGPT chats and set up its own check twice a day. Dots run on their own cloud computer and keep working after the laptop is closed. The source gives few other details about the setup.

## How it went

It came back with Gesha options priced per cup. The article counts it among the better early tests, because it reused old context, scheduled its own checks and did the per-cup math.

## Worth knowing

Dots are only available on Pro, Business Premium and Enterprise plans, and not in the EEA, Switzerland or the UK. Plus and Free users can't get one.

## Prompt

```text
Twice a day, at [times], check [list of roulette retailers or sites] for [type of product, e.g. rare coffee beans like Gesha] available in [country], within a budget of [price per unit or cup]. Send me a short message only when there is something new, listing name, seller, price and link. Don't buy anything; pause and show me the details before any purchase step.
```
