# Audit SEO and AI-search visibility with an agent

> The poster built MentionDesk, which includes an agent that audits a brand's SEO and GEO visibility to help it get recognized by ChatGPT and similar tools.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** ChatGPT  
**Source:** Reddit · Dec 1, 2025

## What they did

In the thread, the MentionDesk poster says they built the product after struggling to get recognized by ChatGPT and similar tools, and used it to optimize for AI discovery rather than just classic SEO. A commenter describing a similar setup says their agents split the work into separate audits for SEO and GEO visibility, mapping Reddit and subreddit discussions, generating structured content for AI engines, and monitoring reviews, mentions, and other credibility signals.

## How it went

The source claims the approach helped them get cited far more often than technical hacks, and another commenter says running the agents together produced a big lift in direct and organic search traffic. But the thread gives no workflow details, metrics, or independent results for MentionDesk itself.

## Worth knowing

The main catch is that this Reddit thread is high level: it names the audit areas and signals, but does not state pricing, runtime, or a step-by-step process.

## Prompt

```text
Audit how visible [brand name] at [website URL] is in traditional search and in AI answers. Check our rankings for [keywords], ask ChatGPT-style questions such as [example buyer questions] and note whether we are mentioned, and compare with [competitor 1, competitor 2]. Finish with a report of gaps and the top [number] fixes to improve SEO and AI-search visibility. Do not change anything on my site or submit forms without asking me first.
```
