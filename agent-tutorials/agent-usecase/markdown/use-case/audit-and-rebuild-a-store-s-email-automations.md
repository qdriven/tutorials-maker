# Audit and rebuild a store's email automations

> Muse audited a shoe store's Klaviyo flows, removed duplicates, rebuilt templates and enabled sales tracking, with approval needed to publish or spend.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** X · Oct 1, 2026

## What they did

April Polk gave Muse access to her JaeLuxe Shoetique Klaviyo account through its connectors. Muse audited the email automations, removed duplicate flows, rebuilt the templates, and turned on sales tracking. Nothing published or spent money until she approved.

## How it went

The source reports the listed tasks were completed but states no sales figures, time saved, or other results.

## Worth knowing

Nothing publishes, sends, or spends without approval.

## Prompt

```text
Audit the email automations in my [Klaviyo] account for [my store name]. Find duplicate or conflicting flows, rebuild the key templates (welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase) in my brand style, and make sure sales tracking is turned on. Pause and show me what you are about to do before publishing anything live or spending money. Finish with a summary of what you changed.
```
