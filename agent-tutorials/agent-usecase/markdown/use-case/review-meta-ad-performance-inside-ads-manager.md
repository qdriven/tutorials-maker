# Review Meta ad performance inside Ads Manager

> An agency tested Manus on a real ecommerce client's Meta ads account; it reported a 14.25x ROAS against an actual 1.76x and corrected itself only when challenged.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Manus  
**Source:** skaleit.agency · Undated

## What they did

Skaleit gave Manus, the agent built into Meta Ads Manager, access to a live client ecommerce account whose performance had dropped since January 2026. They asked it to compare the last 30 days with January, explain why ROAS fell, and propose a recovery plan. They then questioned its figures directly.

## How it went

Manus reported January ROAS of 14.25x and 30-day ROAS of 7.04x. When asked if it was sure, it rechecked and gave 1.76. Its advice was generic, and Skaleit rated strategic scaling 1 out of 5.

## Worth knowing

Check every number the agent reports against Ads Manager yourself. It gave wrong ROAS until challenged, and it kept recommending Meta's creative enhancement features, which Skaleit says most brands turn off.

## Prompt

```text
Review the last [time period, e.g. 30 days] of performance for my Meta ads account [account or client name] and write a short report on spend, ROAS, top and bottom ads, and what to change. Show the exact figures you read from Ads Manager next to each claim so I can check them. This is read-only: do not change any budgets, ads, or settings.
```
