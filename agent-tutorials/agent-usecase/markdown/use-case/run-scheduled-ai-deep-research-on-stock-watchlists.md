# Run scheduled AI deep research on stock watchlists

> A reply describes daily and weekly cron jobs running AI deep research on watchlists and monitoring company events and insider activity.

**Category:** Research & Knowledge  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** Reddit · Aug 9, 2026

## What they did

In a comment, the person said they wanted daily and weekly cron jobs to trigger a script that runs AI deep research across their watchlists, plus a few tools to pull quick baseline information on any stock. They were buying “skills” from a Substack and planned to adapt them for local AI on a MacBook, but described the setup as still a work in progress rather than a finished system.

## How it went

They did not report results, performance numbers, or a completed workflow. The main limitation they named was lack of time, and the implementation was still unfinished.

## Worth knowing

The only concrete cost mentioned was roughly $300 per year for the “Compound with AI” Substack, apparently after some discount.

## Prompt

```text
Every weekday at [time] and every [day] weekly, run deep research on my watchlist [tickers]. Summarize new company events, earnings dates, news, and insider filings, and flag anything that changed since the last run. Send me a concise digest at [channel], and do not place any trades or send anything to anyone else.
```
