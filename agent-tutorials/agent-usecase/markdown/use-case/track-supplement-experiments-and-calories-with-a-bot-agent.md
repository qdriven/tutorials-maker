# Track supplement experiments and calories with a bot agent

> A user runs Grok Bot for personal experiments with supplements and calorie tracking, alongside Codex and OpenCode for coding.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Grok Bot, Codex  
**Source:** HN · Nov 4, 2026

## What they did

The commenter uses Grok Bot as a personal agent for running supplement experiments and calorie tracking. For coding they use Codex and OpenCode as harnesses, and they use OpenRouter pay-as-you-go to try Chinese models like Deepseek and GLM. The source gives no detail on how the Grok Bot setup or workflow actually works.

## How it went

The source gives no results or numbers for the supplement and calorie tracking. They tried Hermes several times and disliked it each time, and a first try of Rakazo went no better.

## Worth knowing

Grok Bot came cheap: the commenter got three months at $30 when they tried to cancel the subscription.

## Prompt

```text
Act as my personal tracker for [supplement or habit experiment, e.g. creatine and sleep] and my daily calories. Each day I will message you what I took and ate; log it in a running spreadsheet with date, items, and estimated calories. Every [Sunday] send me a short summary of trends and anything that looks correlated, and ask me before changing how you track anything.
```
