# Plan and book a short-notice work trip

> Grok Bot researched flights and booked the one Vince picked, though he had to take over the computer to fill in data and found it harder than booking himself.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** X · Sep 30, 2026

## What they did

User handed short-notice Munich work trip planning to Grok Bot, giving it the destination and constraints of airport/Mynaric access without car rental. The agent researched flights, suggested departing a day early with options; after the user chose, it initiated booking (user had to intervene repeatedly to enter details). It then listed hotels, recommended one on the direct train line, and later produced a step-by-step city-center sightseeing plan for a 5-hour layover.

## How it went

Hotel proved convenient with restaurant and grocery nearby; $15 train to site and $5 back for sightseeing worked without issues. User reported saving hours of research and stress, avoided car rental, and found the trip seamless overall.

## Worth knowing

The only friction was that flight booking required the user to repeatedly take over the computer to fill forms.

## Prompt

```text
I need a work trip to [destination] on [dates] at short notice, arriving by [time]. Research flights on [booking site], shortlist three options with price and times, and wait for my pick. Then fill in the booking form using my details and stop at the payment step so I can review everything before paying.
```
