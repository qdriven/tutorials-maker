# Plan a group weekend trip and make reservations

> ChatGPT work mode planned a long weekend for 9 friends, built a coordination site, reached group agreement, made reservations, and drafted a Gmail email.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** ChatGPT Agent  
**Source:** X · Jul 26, 2026

## What they did

From his phone he prompted ChatGPT to use his full chat history for a long weekend with 8 friends, generate the three best options, build a full-stack site for the nine to coordinate preferences and reach agreement, then make reservations and draft the Gmail invite once decided. The agent handled the entire sequence on its own.

## How it went

It just worked.

## Prompt

```text
Plan a long weekend trip for [number] friends in [destination] from [dates], with a budget of about [amount] per person. Build a simple shared page with 2 or 3 itinerary options and a way for everyone to vote, then draft an email to the group in Gmail. Pause and show me the email and any reservation details before sending anything or booking or paying for anything.
```
