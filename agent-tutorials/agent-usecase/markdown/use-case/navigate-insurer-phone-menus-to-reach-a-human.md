# Navigate insurer phone menus to reach a human

> A Reddit user had Meta's Muse AI call their home insurer's toll-free number, get past the phone menus to a human rep, then call them to change the policy.

**Category:** Money, Bills & Benefits  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** tomsguide.com · Undated

## What they did

A Reddit user needed to change their home insurance policy but didn't want to work through the press-1, press-2 phone menus. They told Muse to call the insurer's toll-free number, keep going until it reached a human representative, then call the user's phone. Muse placed the call using a synthetic voice, which the user could choose as male or female.

## How it went

Muse reached a human rep and rang the user, who then made the policy change easily. The source is one user's own account, with no timings given.

## Worth knowing

The user says to tell Muse to call on your behalf, give it the task, and have it hand the call to you once a human answers.

## Prompt

```text
Please call my home insurer at [toll-free number], work through the phone menus, and get a live representative on the line. Then ask them to [policy change, e.g. raise my deductible or add a driver] on policy [policy number]. Before you confirm anything that changes the policy or costs money, stop and tell me exactly what was offered, and when the call is done send me a summary of what was said and any reference number.
```
