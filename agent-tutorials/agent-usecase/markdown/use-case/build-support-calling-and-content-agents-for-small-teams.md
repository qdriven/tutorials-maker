# Build support, calling and content agents for small teams

> A solo founder delivers Lindy-based chat support, inbound/outbound calling, content repurposing and negotiation agents to small-team clients.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Lindy  
**Source:** lindy.ai · Undated

## What they did

Rashi Gupta runs Seven Zero Ventures alone and builds Lindy agents for solo founders, teams under 10 and niche operators. The builds cover chat support, inbound and outbound calling, content repurposing and negotiation. She also built industry setups: influencer agencies (intake, reply handling, contract dispatch) and financial services. The source gives little detail on how the agents work.

## How it went

Reported figures: over $10K revenue from directory leads since January 2025, and every client came through Lindy's partner directory with no ads or outbound. Work has covered influencer marketing and financial services. The figures are self-reported.

## Worth knowing

The story is mostly about getting leads from Lindy's partner directory. It has no build details, costs or agent performance numbers.

## Prompt

```text
Build me a customer chat support agent for [my business type] that answers questions using the information at [link to FAQ, site, or document]. It should hand off to a human at [email or phone] when it isn't sure or when the customer is upset. Before turning it on for real customers, run five test conversations with me and show me the replies. It's finished when I've approved the test results.
```
