# Run web searches and browser tasks with Operator

> An early-access user tested Operator opening a browser and searching the web, and reported how well it handled web tasks.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** Operator  
**Source:** Reddit · Jan 1, 2025

## What they did

The poster gave Operator a concrete browser task on its website: find 50 popular finance YouTubers, collect LinkedIn details, emails, and short channel summaries, and put everything into a table. Operator opened a browser, searched Bing, visited pages, and tried to use online spreadsheet tools on its own; the user mainly watched, and only stepped in after about 20 minutes to stop it.

## How it went

It produced only 18 influencers after 20 minutes, and the author says the LinkedIn details and email addresses it returned were invented. The reviewer found the browser actions very slow and said it failed to ask for help when sign-in would have saved time.

## Worth knowing

The biggest catch was speed and reliability: the author says they could have done the same research themselves in about 15 minutes with fewer mistakes.

## Prompt

```text
Open a browser and research [topic or question] by searching the web and visiting the most relevant pages. Give me a short summary of what you found with the links to your sources, plus a note on anything you couldn't access or weren't sure about. Don't log in, submit forms, or buy anything without asking me first.
```
