# Auto-post scheduled images to Instagram and Pinterest

> A Make.com workflow watches a Google Sheet, pulls images from Drive, posts to Instagram and Pinterest on schedule for a supplements brand, and marks rows as posted.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Make  
**Source:** X · Oct 7, 2026

## What they did

A Make.com scenario watches a Google Sheet that the client populates with caption, image URL from Drive, and date. On the scheduled date it pulls the image and posts natively to Instagram (carousel or reel) plus Pinterest (rich pin). It then marks the row “Posted” and logs the action.

## How it went

After 30 days the brand reached 100% posting consistency (vs ~60% before), +34% Instagram reach, and +1.7k monthly Pinterest visitors while scaling from 30 to 90 posts per month with no added headcount.

## Prompt

```text
Build a scenario that watches my Google Sheet [sheet name] for rows where the post date has arrived and the status is not Posted. It should fetch the image named in the row from Google Drive folder [folder name], post it with the caption to Instagram and Pinterest, then mark the row Posted. Test it first with one row and show me the preview before turning on the schedule.
```
