# Organize saved recipes and research trip flights

> An Engadget writer had Muse pull recipes from saved Facebook and Instagram posts into a grouped list, then research nonstop flights and set up a price tracker.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** engadget.com · Undated

## What they did

The writer asked Muse, which Meta's account was already linked to, to go through all their saved Facebook and Instagram posts, pull out the recipes, and organize them. For travel, they gave it trip timing and a nonstop-only preference. Muse suggested flight options and route order, and set up a price tracker.

## How it went

Muse returned a recipe list grouped by type, with ingredients, steps and links to the original posts. It also built a restaurant guide sorted by city. The flight suggestions weren't revelatory, but the recurring price monitoring was useful.

## Worth knowing

Meta warns Muse can make mistakes, so check its permission settings (consider "always ask") and double-check what it does on your behalf.

## Prompt

```text
Go through my saved Facebook and Instagram posts, pull out every recipe, and group them into a list by [meal type or cuisine] with ingredients for each. Then research nonstop flights from [origin] to [destination] for [dates] and set up a price tracker. Show me the list and the flight options before you save or subscribe to anything, and do not book anything.
```
