# Find unclaimed money and check claim notarization rules

> A user searched for missing money, found over $5k, and used AI guidance on Oregon's notarization requirement for claims over $2,000.

**Category:** Money, Bills & Benefits  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** Reddit · Nov 5, 2026

## What they did

The poster searched a state unclaimed-property site, found a claim worth over $5,000, and then told others to check their names too. In comments, they said Oregon sent a document that had to be notarized, and before that they had asked AI about the process; the AI told them Oregon only requires that notarized paperwork for claims over $2,000, which they did not independently verify.

## How it went

The poster says the search turned up more than $5,000 for them, and a friend they prompted found $190. They also tallied about $1,623 other commenters said they had found after seeing the post, but the source does not say the poster had completed or been paid on the $5,000 claim yet.

## Worth knowing

Useful catch: the poster says Oregon required notarization paperwork for their claim, and the AI guidance they relied on was unverified.

Help me search for unclaimed money or property under the name [full name] and past addresses [cities/states], starting with the official state site for [state] and MissingMoney.com. List every match with the amount, holder, and the claim requirements, including whether notarization or ID documents are needed. Stop at the claim form and show me what you plan to enter before submitting anything or uploading documents.
