# Book flights with seat selection and traveler numbers

> Muse booked flights for Cyrus and his wife end to end on Amex Travel, including seat selection plus loyalty and TSA numbers.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** X · Sep 15, 2026

## What they did

Cyrus asked Muse to book round-trip flights for himself and his wife on Amex Travel. The agent completed the full process end-to-end on its own, entering seat selections plus loyalty and TSA numbers without further input.

## How it went

The booking succeeded. Cyrus called it the first time an agent had handled a task of this complexity autonomously.

```text
Book flights for [number] travelers from [origin] to [destination] on [dates] using [booking site, e.g. my credit card travel portal]. Show me the full itinerary, seats and total price before you pay, and send me the confirmation when it's booked.
```
