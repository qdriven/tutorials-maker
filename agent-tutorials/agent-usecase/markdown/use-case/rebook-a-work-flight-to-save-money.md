# Rebook a work flight to save money

> Arden texted a user that rebooking his Zurich flight would save $7,750; after he approved, it changed the flight and the refund hit his corporate card.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** X · May 6, 2026

## What they did

Arden monitored the user's booked Zurich work flight. It sent a text proposing a rebook that would save $7,750 and asking for approval. The user replied yes, after which the agent executed the change and triggered the refund to the corporate card.

## How it went

The rebooking went through and the $7,750 refund appeared on the card. The user took no other actions such as opening apps or setting alerts.

## Prompt

```text
Look at my booked flight [airline, confirmation code, route, date] and check for cheaper options on the same route and similar times, including rebooking fees and fare differences. Summarize the net savings. Pause and show me the exact change and cost before you rebook or pay anything.
```
