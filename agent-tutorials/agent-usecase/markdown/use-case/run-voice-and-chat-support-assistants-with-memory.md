# Run voice and chat support assistants with memory

> AT&T deploys Gemini-based voice and chat assistants with 60-day memory, reporting 20% more inquiries fully automated.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Gemini  
**Source:** cloud.google.com · Undated

## What they did

AT&T replaced rules-based, flow-chart call routing. BigQuery analyzes customer history to work out why someone is calling and sends them to the right place or a specialized human agent. Callers routed to a virtual assistant are handled by Gemini Enterprise for Customer Experience agents. These agents recall up to 60 days of history across web, retail and phone, and AT&T says it keeps them secure and closely monitored.

## How it went

AT&T reports about 20% better containment at the front of the call, 20% more inquiries fully automated, about $150 million in annual savings, and a better Net Promoter Score. The source gives no per-channel breakdown.

## Worth knowing

The figures come from a Google Cloud case study and an AT&T executive's quotes, and the page gives no implementation timeline or cost detail.
