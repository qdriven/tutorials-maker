# Collect shower glass supplier quotes by email

> OpenClaw emailed shower glass suppliers, collated responses, and handled the back-and-forth in a chat thread.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** OpenClaw  
**Source:** X · Sep 30, 2026

## What they did

User asked OpenClaw to research local shower glass suppliers then contact them. The agent sent emails on its own, gathered replies, and managed the back-and-forth conversation inside a single chat thread. Person gave ongoing oversight on domain-specific details.

## How it went

It produced supplier quotes through email. It required heavy oversight and could not be trusted to place an order.

## Worth knowing

Needed serious oversight on domain-specific details.

## Prompt

```text
Email these [type of supplier, e.g. glass] suppliers in [location]: [supplier list or 'find 5 near me'] and ask for a quote for [job details, dimensions, quantity, deadline]. Collect their replies into one comparison table with price, lead time, and notes. Show me each email before it is sent, and stop when you have quotes from at least [number] suppliers.
```
