# Summarize service calls and find local customers

> Grok Bot listens to phone calls and posts summaries and to-dos to Slack, and scans Reddit daily for local customers, creating Notion pages and calendar appointments for a home service business.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** X · Aug 28, 2026

## What they did

Connected Grokbot to the phone system so it listens to every call and posts summaries with tags and to-dos in Slack. Gave it Reddit access for daily scans of the local subreddit, tagging posts to engage. Stated weekly goals, prompting it to create a Notion page with to-list, calendar appointments, and rewritten follow-up scripts.

## How it went

Saved $250 on the phone subscription by downgrading. Describes the agent as already a real asset in the home service business.

## Prompt

```text
For my [type of service] business, summarize each customer phone call [from this source] and post the summary and to-dos in [Slack channel]. Also check [Reddit communities for my area] each day for people looking for my service, and list them with links in [Notion]. Show me any reply or calendar appointment you plan to create before you make it.
```
