# Design product labels and produce SDS sheets

> A seller used Manus to design product labels, turn customer testimonials into an updated file, and create SDS sheets.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** Manus  
**Source:** Reddit · May 1, 2026

## What they did

The seller used Manus across packaging and compliance work: it designed new co-branded product labels, read screenshots of new customer testimonials and added them into updated files, and generated three SDS sheets. A person still had to step in on label production, because when Manus offered to turn its label concepts into print-ready files, the output was not actually usable, so they traced from the rendering and made the final production file themselves.

## How it went

It saved enough labor that the user was comfortable paying $200-$300 per month, and they say the three SDS sheets alone avoided about $900 in software fees or $3,000 in hired writing costs. The main shortfall was that Manus could make strong label renderings but not dependable print-ready artwork.

## Worth knowing

The biggest catch was cost creep: the user said wasted credits were an expensive lesson, and upgrading plans was the only way to get instant new credits.

## Prompt

```text
I sell [product type] and need print-ready label designs for [product names], using my brand colors [colors] and logo [attach file]. Also fold in these customer testimonials [paste or attach] to produce an updated marketing file, and draft Safety Data Sheets for [products] using the ingredient details I attach. Deliver everything as downloadable files and flag any safety or regulatory information I should verify before use.
```
