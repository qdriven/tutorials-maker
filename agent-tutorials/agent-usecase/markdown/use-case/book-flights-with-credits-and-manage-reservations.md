# Book flights with credits and manage reservations

> A user had Muse use unused Southwest credits to book a flight, watch for better seats, handle OpenTable reservations and keep a trip dashboard for a visiting friend.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** applevis.com · Undated

## What they did

The user, a screen reader user, messaged Muse through WhatsApp. They said they had unused Southwest credits and wanted a Nashville flight. Muse found the credits and booked it, with no copying of confirmation numbers. It also rechecked for better seats every 30 minutes, and could change seats automatically or ask first. For a visiting friend, it booked and changed OpenTable reservations and kept a shareable trip dashboard updated.

## How it went

The user reports the credit booking and the reservation handling worked well, with most tasks smoother than on other agent tools. Requests can take a moderate to long time to finish.

## Worth knowing

The user used up 98% of the free tier by day 6 of heavy use and moved to the $20 plan.

## Prompt

```text
Check my [airline] account for unused credits and find a flight from [origin] to [destination] on [dates] that I can pay for with them. Also look for better seats on that booking and keep a simple trip dashboard with flights and dinner reservations at [restaurant/city] for [number] people. Pause and show me details before booking, paying or changing any reservation.
```
