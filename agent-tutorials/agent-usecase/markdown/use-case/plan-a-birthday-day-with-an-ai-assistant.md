# Plan a birthday day with an AI assistant

> A co-founder's AI assistant planned his birthday: gathered coworker stories, organized the day, booked dinner and ordered a cake.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** X · Oct 3, 2026

## What they did

The co-founder's AI assistant received planning access and gathered stories from coworkers. It then organized the full day schedule, booked dinner reservations, and ordered a cake, but every action required human approval.

## How it went

The birthday day was completely planned by the agent with no further details reported.

## Worth knowing

The agent requires approval unless the user sets a budget.

## Prompt

```text
Plan a birthday for [name] on [date] in [city]. Draft a message asking [coworkers or friends] to share short stories about them and compile the replies into a single document, then find a dinner reservation for [number] people around [time] and a cake from a local bakery within [budget]. Pause and show me the details before sending any message, booking, or payment.
```
