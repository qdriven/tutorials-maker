# Run a website business end to end with an agent

> A Grok Bot agent runs about 90% of a free AI guide site for over-50s: writing guides, daily deploys and link checks, newsletter drafts, directory submissions, with owner approval for public items. · Oct 16, 2026

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** exonexo.com · Oct 16, 2026 · Submitted by Robert Woolford @RFWoolford

## What they did

Exo, part of Grok Bot, works alongside Robert on a schedule. A daily job around 2am ET fetches news, rebuilds the site, checks every page and outside link, and replaces dead links. Twice-weekly jobs refresh a trending page, and a weekly job adds pages and drafts a newsletter. Exo also submitted the site to directories. Newsletters, social posts and emails wait for Robert's approval.

## How it went

Three days in, the site had 97 pages, 24 guides and 187 links checked, with 9 broken links replaced and 0 dead. All 6 routine runs succeeded. 12 items were waiting on Robert.

## Worth knowing

The source gives only three days of history, and the 90% figure isn't stated on the page. Public-facing actions like email and social posting stay human-gated.

## Prompt

```text
Help me run my website [site name and URL], which is a [short description] for [audience]. Each day, draft one new [guide or article] on [topic area], check all links, and prepare a deploy, and each week draft a newsletter. Anything public, like deploys, newsletter sends, or directory submissions, must be shown to me for approval first. Send me a short daily report of what you finished and what's waiting on me.
```
