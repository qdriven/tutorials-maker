# Take a video from thumbnail to published with promo posts

> Cowork proposes a Figma thumbnail, uploads the video to YouTube with metadata, and drafts and schedules X, Bluesky and LinkedIn promo posts.

**Category:** Content & Creative  
**Agent:** Claude Cowork  
**Source:** seancdavis.com · Undated

## What they did

Sean drops an exported video into an inbox folder in a Cowork project. Cowork transcribes it locally with Whisper and proposes titles and descriptions. It opens Figma, builds a thumbnail from his template, and after his tweaks uploads to YouTube. It then drafts X, Bluesky and LinkedIn posts in Markdown for his edits. It posts or schedules them, using a custom scheduled task for Bluesky.

## How it went

The process still takes more than the one-hour goal, because each run turns up small system tweaks. Sean says the foundation gets stronger each time but that he hasn't proven the hypothesis.

## Worth knowing

Bluesky has no native scheduling, so Cowork created a scheduled task to post at the right time. Expect to keep refining the setup over the first several runs.

## Prompt

```text
I have a finished video at [file path] about [topic]. Propose a thumbnail design in Figma, write a YouTube title, description and tags, and draft promo posts for [platforms such as X, Bluesky and LinkedIn]. Show me everything before uploading the video or scheduling any posts, and wait for my approval.
```
