# Hand off small business admin tasks to AI with approval

> A business owner uses AI for small admin tasks, approving anything involving money personally.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** Reddit · Sep 2, 2026

## What they did

The owner says they use AI as a first pass on routine finance admin: checking unpaid invoices, sorting expenses, and flagging upcoming subscriptions and what needs attention. In comments, they say they connect Meow on the banking side into the AI workflow so it can pull together finance context, while they personally approve anything important or involving money.

## How it went

They report it made the admin side much less annoying and helped surface what actually needed attention. The clear limit is that they do not let it make payment decisions on its own.

## Worth knowing

The main catch is the approval boundary: anything important, especially money movement, still stays with the owner.

## Prompt

```text
I run [type of business] and want help clearing small admin tasks. Start with these: [list tasks, e.g. sort invoices in my Downloads folder, reply to scheduling emails, update my expenses sheet]. Finished means each task is done or drafted, with a short summary of what you did. Anything involving money, sending messages, or deleting files must be shown to me for approval before you do it.
```
