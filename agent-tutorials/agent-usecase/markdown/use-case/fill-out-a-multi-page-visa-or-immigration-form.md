# Fill out a multi-page visa or immigration form

> A user had ChatGPT help complete a many-page visa form, with a voice-call flow in dots asking for needed details while filling it.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** ChatGPT, Dots  
**Source:** Reddit · Undated

## What they did

The poster used ChatGPT’s Dots/Astra voice mode while working through a long visa or immigration application. They had it fill the draft and, at the same time, ask follow-up questions by voice when details were missing; if something was hard to explain on the call, it sent the question in chat. The person says they only let it prepare the draft and then did a final review themselves.

## How it went

They call it a life-changing use case for many-page visa forms, especially because voice made the typing less tiring. The clearest time saver they mention was pulling travel history from the CBP website and email booking records, but they give no hard time or accuracy numbers.

## Worth knowing

Their main caution was to use it for a draft only and review everything yourself; they also mention privacy concerns unless model-improvement sharing is turned off.

## Prompt

```text
Help me complete this [visa/immigration] form: [paste link or upload the PDF]. Go through it section by section, and ask me for any detail you need, such as passport number, dates, addresses or travel history, rather than guessing. When done, show me a full summary of every answer and flag anything unclear; do not submit or pay for anything until I approve.
```
