# Post to all socials from Claude with approval

> The poster built a custom Claude connector linked to their social accounts that posts to multiple platforms in under two minutes, with their approval required before publishing.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Claude  
**Source:** Reddit · Mar 1, 2026

## What they did

The poster built a custom Claude connector tied to their social accounts. They give Claude a link or ask it to research a topic, then ask for a social post; Claude writes slide captions, calls the connector to pull and choose images from a large image database, and returns a preview link formatted for each network. The person reviews that external preview and manually approves publishing.

## How it went

It let them publish image posts, slideshows, and text to all their socials at once in under two minutes, and they say it helped them post educational content regularly and start growing an audience. It does not yet handle video or every platform they want.

## Worth knowing

The key safeguard is that Claude cannot publish on its own; only the user can approve the final post from an external URL.

## Prompt

```text
Connect to my social accounts through [connector/MCP name] and help me post to [platforms]. When I give you a topic, write a version for each platform in my voice: [voice notes]. Show me the final text and images for every platform and wait for my explicit approval before publishing, then confirm the post links when finished.
```
