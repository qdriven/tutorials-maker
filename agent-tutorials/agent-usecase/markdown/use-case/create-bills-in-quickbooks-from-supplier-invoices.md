# Create bills in QuickBooks from supplier invoices

> A commenter uses an AI agent connected to QuickBooks Online, email and Drive to check the books and turn supplier invoices into bills.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** Reddit · Undated

## What they did

A commenter described a hands-free setup where supplier invoices are sent to a dedicated inbox, then an AI agent parses each invoice and pushes the details straight into accounting software like QuickBooks, including taxes and net terms. They said they built variations of this for clients, with a person only reviewing the books quarterly.

## How it went

They said it reduced their own bookkeeping spend to about $200 a year. The thread does not give invoice volumes, error rates, or a step-by-step QuickBooks flow.

## Worth knowing

The clearest catch is that the human check was not removed entirely; they still have someone look things over once a quarter.

## Prompt

```text
Check my email [label or folder] and Drive folder [folder name] for supplier invoices from the last [time period]. For each one, prepare a bill for QuickBooks Online with supplier, invoice number, date, amount, tax, and the suggested account. Show me the full list for approval before creating anything in QuickBooks, and flag any possible duplicates.
```
