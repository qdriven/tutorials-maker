# Compile recent LLM research papers into a Word doc

> Comet found major LLM/agent training papers from six months with links and analysis, then saved them to a Word doc via Outlook.

**Category:** Research & Knowledge  
**Agent:** Perplexity Comet  
**Source:** analyticsvidhya.com · Undated

## What they did

Nitika Sharma gave Comet one prompt asking for every major LLM/agent training paper from the past six months. She wanted each paper's link, publication date, an analysis, how the approaches differ, and one unique factor. She then asked it to put the results in a Word doc. For that step she connected Comet to her Outlook account. She could watch the browser agent work in the main window.

## How it went

Comet returned a large body of reading material and produced the Word doc, which the author links. The post gives no paper count and doesn't say how accurate the analyses were.

## Worth knowing

Saving the doc required linking an Outlook or Google account to Comet. Comet is free on Windows and macOS as of October 2025.

## Prompt

```text
Find the most important papers on [research topic] from the last [number] months. For each, give the title, authors, link and a two-sentence summary of why it matters. Compile everything into a Word document titled [title] and tell me where it was saved.
```
