# Run brand marketing end to end and publish to social

> A tool lets Claude Code scrape competitors, generate and edit images and videos, and publish to Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X and Reddit; one video reportedly hit 5.3M views.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Claude Code  
**Source:** Reddit · May 5, 2026

## What they did

The poster says they built a CLI called Wonda that gives Claude Code a stable interface for end-to-end marketing work: scraping competitors, generating images and videos with tools including Seedance, Sora, and Kling, editing with captions and music, and publishing to Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, and Reddit. In the comments, they say the team creates the core skills itself from patterns that worked across real campaigns, while human taste still matters.

## How it went

They report using 20+ AI models across campaigns and say one Instagram video reached 5.3 million views. They also point readers to a fuller writeup on what fails, saying bad generations stay bad and platform nuance still matters.

## Worth knowing

The main catch they mention is quality control: even with the workflow automated, taste and platform-specific judgment still need a person in the loop.

## Prompt

```text
Research [3-5 competitors] in [my industry] by looking at their public social content, then propose a content plan for my brand [brand name and style]. Draft [5] posts with image or video ideas for [platforms]. Don't publish anything until you've shown me each post and its destination and I've approved it.
```
