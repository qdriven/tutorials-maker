# Plan a road trip with Claude Cowork

> The poster used Claude for the entire planning of a trip to National Harbor, MD, with it acting like a knowledgeable companion on routes.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Claude Cowork  
**Source:** Reddit · Sep 21, 2026

## What they did

The poster used Claude Cowork to plan a 764-mile drive to National Harbor, Maryland end to end. Claude mapped rest stops and meal stops, flagged traffic areas where the car’s lane-follow assist might need more manual takeover, and suggested where to buy gas, including filling up before crossing into Virginia; the driver still chose to follow the plan and handle the car.

## How it went

They said it felt like having a knowledgeable travel companion in the car and they drove the full 764 miles in one day without feeling worn out on arrival. The only limitation mentioned was Claude pushing some rest stops even when they did not yet feel tired.

## Worth knowing

The main catch was to trust the fatigue planning: Claude urged breaks before they subjectively felt tired because fatigue can creep up slowly.

## Prompt

```text
I'm planning a road trip from [starting city] to [destination] on [dates]. Compare two or three driving routes and give me drive times, toll costs, good stops for gas and food roughly every [number] hours, and parking options near [hotel or destination]. Finish with a one-page day-by-day plan saved as a document, and don't book or pay for anything.
```
