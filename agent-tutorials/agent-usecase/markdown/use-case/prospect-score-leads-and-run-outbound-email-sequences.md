# Prospect, score leads and run outbound email sequences

> An agent scans Apollo.io and job boards twice daily for ICP-matching prospects, scores leads, writes 3-email sequences with Claude, sends follow-ups, and pauses on replies with human approval.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Claude  
**Source:** g2.com · Undated

## What they did

getSalesClaw is a product that scans Apollo.io and job boards twice daily for prospects matching an ideal customer profile. It scores each lead 0–100 and drops low scorers. For qualified leads, Claude writes a unique 3-email sequence, with follow-ups on Day 3 and Day 7. A person can approve emails via Telegram, Slack, or a dashboard before sending.

## How it went

The source is a product description with no results, numbers, or user reports. It has no reviews or discussions yet, so real-world performance is not shown.

## Worth knowing

Sending volume ramps from 5 to 150 emails a day over 60 days for domain warmup, so full-scale outreach is not immediate.

```text
Twice a day, search [Apollo.io and job boards] for prospects matching my ideal customer profile: [titles, industries, company size, region]. Score each lead from 1 to 10 with a one-line reason, write a 3-email sequence for those scoring [threshold] or higher, and save them to [CRM or sheet]. Pause and show me every email for approval before sending anything, and stop an outreach thread as soon as the prospect replies.
```
