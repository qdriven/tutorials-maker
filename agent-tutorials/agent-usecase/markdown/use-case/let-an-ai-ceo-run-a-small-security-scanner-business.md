# Let an AI CEO run a small security scanner business

> The poster gave an AI agent a security scanner business idea with three AI employees; at day 2 there was no revenue and a homepage outage.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** Reddit · Oct 18, 2026

## What they did

The poster gave a Grok Bot agent called Angie one brief: build a security scanner for AI-built apps, with the poster acting as a “board” that approved spending, posts under his name, and logins only he could do. In about four hours, the agent researched competitors, chose a name, checked domain and trademark availability, built the site and scanner, set up Stripe, created branding and social accounts, then added three AI roles for growth, critique, and security research; the poster also spot-checked scan results and suggested using Google dorks for nightly prospecting scans.

## How it went

By day 2, it had launched, repaired a 10-minute homepage outage, and changed the monetization so scans were free but fixes cost $29; revenue was still $0 and almost all scans were their own. Reported cash costs were about $10 for the domain and $5/month hosting, though the poster said it also ran on his existing $300/month Grok Bot plan.

## Worth knowing

The biggest non-passive part was still human oversight and distribution; the poster said he spent more time editing videos about it than the AI spent building the product.

## Prompt

```text
Act as the CEO of a small [business type, e.g. website security scanner] business, with three AI employees for [roles, e.g. development, marketing, support]. Write a 30-day plan with weekly goals and a daily checklist. Set up the project folder with a role brief for each employee and a status file that tracks revenue, uptime and open issues. Finish with today's task list for each role. Before any step that spends money, posts publicly or emails someone, pause and show me what you're about to do.
```
