# Run a daily SEO article campaign

> An agent researches keywords, publishes one SEO article daily and refreshes articles every two weeks using Search Console and Ahrefs data.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** X · Apr 15, 2026

## What they did

The agent received access to keyword and article research plus a transcript of the user's views on the topic. It researched keywords and articles independently, wrote one article daily for the target keyword, and published it. A separate agent pulled live data from Google Search Console and Ahrefs every two weeks to rewrite and improve the articles.

## How it went

Article 1 reached page one of search results.

## Worth knowing

Offered via free trial at graphed.com.

## Prompt

```text
Act as my SEO content agent for [my website URL]. Using my Search Console and Ahrefs exports in [folder path], pick one keyword with good potential, write a draft article of about [word count] words in my brand voice, and save it as a file in [content folder]. Also list the two oldest articles that most need a refresh. Stop once the draft and the refresh list are ready, and show me both before anything is published.
```
