# Research a blog post with Claude computer use

> The author used Claude computer use with Claude in Chrome to research the post itself, suggesting starting with a logged-in site and a specific lookup.

**Category:** Research & Knowledge  
**Agent:** Claude computer use, Claude in Chrome  
**Source:** prosperinai.substack.com · Undated

## What they did

From the Claude app's Dispatch feature, the author sent one prompt asking Claude to open X, where he was already logged in, and find 2-4 posts worth writing about. Claude opened Chrome, scrolled his feed, and read the engagement numbers. It picked four posts, wrote an angle for each, then stopped and asked what to do next.

## How it went

It returned four post ideas with view and like counts in about 90 seconds. The author says he still has to choose the story and judge the angle. Anthropic calls the feature a research preview, so complex tasks may need a second try.

## Worth knowing

Needs a Claude Pro or Max plan, macOS, the desktop app awake and running, and the Claude in Chrome extension. Screen control is slower than a direct integration.

## Prompt

```text
I'm already logged into [site, e.g. a research database or analytics dashboard] in this browser. Look up [specific thing, e.g. a stat, filing or past article on topic X] there and collect the 5 most relevant findings with links. Give me a short summary with sources for a blog post about [topic], and don't submit any forms or change anything on the site.
```
