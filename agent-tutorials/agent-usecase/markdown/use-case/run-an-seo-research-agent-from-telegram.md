# Run an SEO research agent from Telegram

> The author fed Manus business-context, content-strategy and competitor-watchlist files via Telegram to run an SEO research agent; it cited 9 real sources and was rated 4/5. · Oct 14, 2026

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support

**Source:** aimaker.substack.com · Oct 14, 2026 · Submitted by from search gap: 营销增长=

## What they did

Dheeraj Sharma linked Manus to Telegram by scanning a QR code. He uploaded the three context files he already used in Claude Code, then sent one prompt asking for an SEO knowledge base and a keyword and content brief for a road-trip topic. Manus read the files and returned two PDFs. A follow-up request in the same thread reused the files without re-uploading.

## How it went

The brief took about 5 minutes and 148 credits, was rated 4/5, and cited 9 sources. Keyword volumes were only LLM-estimated High/Medium/Low ranges, not real data. The follow-up Lahaul brief cost 125 credits and scored 4.5/5.

## Worth knowing

Manus shows no cost preview. Even a request for a brief answer cost 123 credits, so a free-tier daily refresh of 300 covers only about two runs.

## Prompt

```text
I'm giving you my business context: [paste or attach a description of my business, audience and offers], my content strategy: [attach it], and a competitor watchlist: [list competitor sites]. Research current SEO opportunities for [topic or niche], using real sources and citing each one with a link. Finish with a ranked list of 5 to 10 content ideas, each with a target keyword and why it's worth pursuing.
```
