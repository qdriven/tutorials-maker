# Run family logistics and kids' education agents

> A founder runs OpenClaw agents including a household logistics assistant and a kids' education agent; her first attempt wiped the family calendar.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** OpenClaw  
**Source:** aol.com · Undated

## What they did

Claire Vo began with one OpenClaw agent as an executive assistant for scheduling, email and coordination. She grew that to nine agents running across several computers. Two cover home life: a family assistant for household logistics and a kids' education agent. The rest cover business work such as sales and operations. She widens each agent's access in stages (calendar, then email visibility, then drafting and sending email, then more autonomous tasks).

## How it went

One agent now does CRM management and customer email drafting that she used to pay someone about 10 hours a week to do. Her first OpenClaw attempt wiped the family calendar.

## Worth knowing

Risks she names are file deletion and the agent knowing where her children go to school, so she grants access gradually. The article gives no setup details for the education agent.

## Prompt

```text
Set up a household logistics assistant for my family that reads my [Google Calendar / shared calendar] and helps with [school pickups, appointments, meal planning]. Start in read-only mode: summarize this week's schedule and flag conflicts. Before you add, change or delete any calendar event, show me exactly what you're about to do and wait for my OK.
```
