# Take over an inherited online store with an agent

> An AI agent was asked to understand an inherited online store's setup, turn priorities into tasks, prepare changes, and keep a record, starting with research on the tea business.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** Reddit · Undated

## What they did

Omni first found the tea business through its Signal Hunter workflow, which reviews public business information, then researched the company, checked its own opportunity records, and sent outreach emails until the website-management gap surfaced. After a human handled the proposal and access, the agent reconstructed the Shopify store’s inherited setup, split requests into bounded tasks, prepared changes in preview, read back configuration and rendered pages to verify them, and kept the request, review notes, and results together.

## How it went

The post says this produced a usable handover: the team could inspect changes, review preview work before release, and continue from one project record. Some scoped updates went live, while a later product-page change stayed unpublished for team review.

## Worth knowing

The source’s main practical catch is governance: live changes were only released when the team explicitly approved them; visually sensitive work stayed in preview first.

## Prompt

```text
I inherited an online store selling [product type, e.g. tea] on [platform]. Review the files and exports in [folder or links] to explain how the store is set up, then research the market for [product type]. Turn what you find into a prioritized task list, draft any proposed changes without applying them, and keep a running log of decisions in a document. Pause and show me before changing any live listing, price, or setting.
```
