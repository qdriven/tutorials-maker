# Generate websites, a course and a 318-page ebook

> A newsletter author used Manus over three days to produce websites, interactive courses and a 318-page ebook from simple prompts.

**Category:** Content & Creative  
**Agent:** Manus  
**Source:** aiblewmymind.substack.com · Undated

## What they did

Daria Cupareanu gave Manus a one-line prompt to build an AI-business course from scratch. Two follow-ups had it deploy a live website and add quizzes, games and YouTube videos. For the ebook, she attached her 100-prompt ebook, prompting guidelines, other prompt collections and a logo, and asked for expanded prompts. The task hit context limits, so she split it into three tasks.

## How it went

She got a deployed interactive course site and a 318-page formatted ebook, though design and branding needed polish and prompts needed review. A third test, an infographic, came out illegible even after retries.

## Worth knowing

She paid for Manus Plus at $39/month. Failed or restarted runs, like the ebook's context-limit restarts, used extra credits, and final formatting polish would have cost more.

## Prompt

```text
Create a [website, interactive course or ebook] about [topic] for [audience]. Plan the structure first and show it to me, then produce the full thing with [length or number of sections] and a consistent style. Finish with the files or a live link and a short list of anything you were unsure about or had to assume.
```
