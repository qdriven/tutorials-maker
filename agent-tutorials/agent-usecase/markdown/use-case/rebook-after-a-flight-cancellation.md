# Rebook after a flight cancellation

> ChatGPT Work mode found replacement flights, arranged a rental car, and consolidated the new travel arrangements after a cancellation.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** ChatGPT Agent  
**Source:** X · Sep 29, 2026

## What they did

After the cancellation the user activated ChatGPT Work mode and directed it to locate replacement flights, arrange the rental car, and consolidate the updated itinerary details into one location. No further access or hand-offs are described.

## How it went

The task took about 30 minutes instead of over an hour and made the situation simpler, though it remained a pain.

## Worth knowing

Cut time from over an hour to roughly 30 minutes.

## Prompt

```text
My flight [flight number] on [date] from [origin] to [destination] was cancelled. Find replacement flight options that get me there by [deadline], and a rental car at [destination] if needed. Put everything into one summary with times, prices, and confirmation steps. Pause and show me the choices before booking or paying for anything.
```
