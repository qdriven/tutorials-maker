# Compile genealogy research into a spreadsheet and plan

> A commenter had ChatGPT build a spreadsheet for her genealogy research, and agent mode was recommended for compiling and formatting findings into a usable document.

**Category:** Research & Knowledge  
**Agent:** ChatGPT Agent  
**Source:** denyseallen.substack.com · Undated

## What they did

A commenter said ChatGPT had earlier created a spreadsheet for her genealogy research. Looking back at it, she noted that ChatGPT had recommended Agent mode for compiling, organizing and formatting research findings into a usable plan or document. She suggested Agent or Deep Research mode as an alternative to the Thinking mode used in the article's comparison.

## How it went

The source gives no results or numbers. It is a brief comment, and she did not report running Agent mode herself. The author said she uses Deep Research to find and compile information, not to analyze files.

## Worth knowing

The thread is thin on detail. Agent mode was ChatGPT's own suggestion for this kind of task, and nobody in the thread reports having tested it.

## Prompt

```text
Take my genealogy notes from [file, link or pasted notes] on [family name or branch] and compile them into a spreadsheet with columns for name, birth, death, location, relationships and source. Then write a short research plan listing gaps and the next records to look for. Finish when the spreadsheet and plan are ready to download.
```
