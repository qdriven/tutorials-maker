# Phone local stores to check product stock

> A Grok-based personal assistant used Bland.ai to call local stores, hold conversations about stock, and return full transcripts with availability.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** X · Sep 5, 2026

## What they did

The Grok-based agent was wired to Bland.ai for phone calls. It dialed local stores, held conversations to check stock on a specific product, and returned full transcripts plus availability status. The user tested it by having the agent call his own phone, role-play a chat, then end the call.

## How it went

The agent completed multiple store calls and delivered transcripts with stock details. No quantitative results are stated.

## Prompt

```text
I need to find out which stores near [my city or zip code] have [product name or model] in stock. Look up the stores that carry it, check their websites for availability and the phone number, and give me a table with store, stock status, price, and phone number. Don't place any orders or reserve anything without showing me first.
```
