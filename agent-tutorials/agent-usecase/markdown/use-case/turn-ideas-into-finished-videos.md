# Turn ideas into finished videos

> Grok Bot acts as a video production assistant that generates and edits video, adds motion and voiceovers, and finishes content.

**Category:** Content & Creative  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** X · Oct 7, 2026

## What they did

User built custom agent "dimul" that connects Grok via CLI using SuperGrok subscription. Grok acted as orchestrator for Imagine to generate clips from its prompts. Grok 4.7 orchestrator then assembled the clips into video and added music and effects.

## How it went

Produced finished videos shown in the post and follow-up example. No specific metrics or shortfalls stated.

## Worth knowing

Custom agent release to users planned in a couple of days.

## Prompt

```text
Turn this idea into a finished video: [describe idea, audience, and length]. Generate the footage, add motion, write and record a voiceover in [tone of voice], and edit everything together. Finish with one exported video and a short note on what you'd change in a second version.
```
