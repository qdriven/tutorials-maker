# Reserve a restaurant table through OpenTable

> In a demo, Operator booked a 7 p.m. table at Beretta in San Francisco via OpenTable, first searching the wrong region before correcting and asking for approval.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Operator  
**Source:** gulfnews.com · Jan 23, 2025

## What they did

Yash Kumar of OpenAI asked Operator to book Beretta in San Francisco around 7 p.m. through OpenTable. Operator opened a remote browser, went to OpenTable and searched, but OpenTable was set to Iowa. Using a San Francisco zip code Kumar had given it earlier, Operator switched regions by itself and sent the reservation for his approval.

## How it went

Operator found the right setting without help and stopped for approval before booking. It did not find the restaurant on the first search. This was a staged demo, not a real-world test.

## Worth knowing

Operator is a research preview for US ChatGPT Pro subscribers at $200 a month. It leaves logins, payment details and CAPTCHAs to the user.

## Prompt

```text
Book a table for [number] people at [restaurant name] in [city] on [date] at [time] using OpenTable. Double-check the city and location before searching, and if that time is unavailable, show me the closest options. Pause and show me the reservation details for approval before you confirm.
```
