# File pet insurance reimbursements for prescription dog food

> Muse gathered the Chewy invoice and prescription card and uploaded them to the insurer's app, handling multiple reimbursements at once.

**Category:** Money, Bills & Benefits  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** Reddit · Oct 6, 2026

## What they did

The poster used Muse for a recurring pet-insurance chore tied to prescription dog food bought from Chewy. They say the normal process is pulling the Chewy invoice, getting the prescription card, and uploading both in the insurer’s app; Muse handled that paperwork flow itself and submitted multiple reimbursement claims at once after four months had piled up.

## How it went

It cleared a backlog the poster had been avoiding and the screenshot shows multiple successful Ivy claim reimbursement notifications. The thread does not give payout amounts, timing, or any failed steps.

## Prompt

```text
I need to file pet insurance reimbursement claims for [pet's name]'s prescription [food/medication]. Find the matching invoices in [Chewy/retailer account or my email], gather the prescription document, and fill out a claim for each in [insurer's website] with the details. Stop before submitting each claim and show me the amount, dates, and attachments so I can confirm.
```
