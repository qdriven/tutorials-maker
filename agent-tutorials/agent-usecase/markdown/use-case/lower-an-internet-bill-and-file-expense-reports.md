# Lower an internet bill and file expense reports

> Commenters say Grok Bot negotiated a Spectrum rate down by $20 a month via chat and handles expense reports from photos sent to a Clerk Bot.

**Category:** Money, Bills & Benefits  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** Reddit · Oct 4, 2026

## What they did

One commenter said they used GrokBot to negotiate with Spectrum in a chat session. Their only stated step was logging in; after that, the bot handled the conversation by itself and got a lower rate. Another commenter said they taught a Clerk Bot to do expense reports so they now just send it a photo.

## How it went

The Spectrum commenter said the bill ended up $20 lower per month. The expense-report commenter framed it as time saved, not direct income, and gave no numbers.

## Worth knowing

A useful catch from a bookkeeping-related comment in the same thread: one user said they now require screenshot proof after the bot initially claimed tasks were done when they were not.

## Prompt

```text
Check whether I'm overpaying for [provider, e.g. internet service] at [current monthly price]. Look up current promotions and competitor rates for my address [address], then open a chat with the provider's support and negotiate a lower rate, using the best offer you found as leverage. Before you accept any new terms, show me the new price, contract length, and any fees and wait for my approval.
```
