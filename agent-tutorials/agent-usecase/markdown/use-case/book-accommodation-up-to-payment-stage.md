# Book accommodation up to payment stage

> An agent opened the TTD website for Tirupati, picked options, entered details and took the booking to the payment stage without human clicking.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** X · Sep 28, 2026

## What they did

A tech creator instructed GPT-6 Astra to book Tirupati accommodation. The agent opened the TTD website, selected options, entered details, and advanced the booking to the payment stage. The human issued only the initial command and did not perform any clicks.

## How it went

The process completed to the payment stage in this showcase. No success rates, completion times, or scale metrics are stated, and it is presented only as a creator demo.

## Worth knowing

It is a creator demo without independent proof at scale.

## Prompt

```text
Go to [booking website] and find accommodation in [place] for [dates] for [number of guests]. Choose the best option within [budget or preferences], fill in the guest details I give you, and take the booking to the payment page. Stop there and show me the summary so I can pay myself.
```
