# Draft and send an email through Gmail

> Comet wrote an SEO email and sent it to the author without asking for review; it arrived with markdown formatting problems.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** Perplexity Comet  
**Source:** juliangoldie.com · Undated

## What they did

Julian Goldie connected his Gmail account to the Comet browser. He then told it to write an email about SEO strategies and send it to his own address. Comet wrote the subject line and body in about 5 seconds and sent the email from his Gmail account. He did not click send or review the draft.

## How it went

The email arrived in his inbox. Comet had used markdown instead of HTML, so the formatting was off. He says telling it to use HTML formatting fixes this.

## Worth knowing

Comet sent the email with no review step. Even Goldie says to check AI output before it goes out, especially client emails, so ask for HTML formatting and a draft first.

## Prompt

```text
Draft an email in Gmail to [recipient] about [topic], in plain text with no markdown symbols, in a [tone] tone. Save it as a draft and show me the full text. Do not send it until I tell you to.
```
