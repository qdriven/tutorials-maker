# Block spam and deliver morning briefings

> Grok Bot blocks spam in email, LinkedIn and calendar invites, sends morning updates on critical items, and keeps working on a business launch while the user is away.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** X · Oct 7, 2026

## What they did

The user gave their Grok bot access to all email accounts, LinkedIn, calendar, and an ongoing business launch. The agent blocks and removes spam from email and LinkedIn, blocks spam calendar invites, and produces a daily morning update covering critical items plus any unfinished personal or business to-dos from the prior day. It also continues work on the business launch independently while the user is away from their desk.

## How it went

No results, metrics, or limitations are stated.

## Prompt

```text
Every morning at [time], review my email, LinkedIn messages and calendar invites. Block or flag obvious spam and cold pitches using these rules: [rules]. Send me a short briefing of only the critical items needing my attention today, and ask me before deleting anything or replying on my behalf.
```
