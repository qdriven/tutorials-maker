# File roofing permits and inspection forms online

> A roofing contractor had Grok Bot fill out and pull a permit, book an inspection through a captcha-protected form, and email two job-notification forms to his insurer.

**Category:** Work & Operations

## What they did

Price Foulger, a roofing contractor, gave Grok Bot his office paperwork over two days. It filled out and pulled an online roofing permit. It also booked a roof inspection through an online form with a captcha, and emailed two job-notification forms to his insurance company. The post doesn't describe the setup or any human steps in between.
