# Build lead lists and multichannel outbound sequences

> Grok Bot built a lead list from the author's website and ICP, then drafted a Salesforge sequence for review before launch; author reports a 38% reply rate.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** salesforge.ai · Undated

## What they did

The author set up one Grok Bot agent with the Leadsforge API for prospect data and the Salesforge API for campaigns. Grok read the author's website, proposed ICPs, and built a first list of 100 leads. It sorted them into high fit, possible fit and poor fit, then drafted a short email and LinkedIn sequence. The author reviewed the sequence and approved it before launch.

## How it went

The author reports a 38% reply rate on the campaign and rates the setup 4.8/5. Lead selection still included some leads the author wouldn't target, and the email copy needed edits.

## Worth knowing

Keep a human review of lead fit, copy and any ICP changes. The author tells the agent not to invent leads or guess emails, and not to launch without approval.

## Prompt

```text
Read [my website URL] and build a lead list of [number] companies matching this ICP: [ICP description], with contact names and emails. Then draft a multichannel outbound sequence of [number] steps in [sequencing tool]. Do not launch anything; stop at the draft and show me the list and sequence for review.
```
