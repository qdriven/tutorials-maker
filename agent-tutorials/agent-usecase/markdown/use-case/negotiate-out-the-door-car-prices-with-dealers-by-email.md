# Negotiate out-the-door car prices with dealers by email

> An AI buying agent emails dealers, asks for written prices, and pushes back when quotes don't match listings, for car buyers.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** caredge.com · Undated

## What they did

CarEdge's AI buying agent emails dealers on the buyer's behalf, so the buyer's phone number stays private. In the example shown, it asked a Culver City Chevrolet dealer for a written out-the-door breakdown on a used Blazer EV. When the quote didn't match the $23,637 listing, it challenged the gap. The dealer answered with trade-in and financing discounts.

## How it went

The quote came back at $41,038, including $11,270 of add-ons that weren't on the listing. The dealer was graded F. Customer testimonials report savings, but the page's own caveat is that results vary.

## Worth knowing

The AI Buying Agent costs $49.99 in beta. First dealer replies usually take about 24 hours, and most buyers finish in 3 to 5 days.

## Prompt

```text
I want to buy a [year make model trim] near [city or zip]. Find [3-5] dealers with matching cars in stock, then draft an email to each asking for a written out-the-door price that itemizes taxes, fees, and add-ons. If a quote doesn't match the listing price, draft a polite reply asking them to explain the difference and match it in writing. Pause and show me every email before it is sent, and stop once I have a side-by-side table of all the quotes.
```
