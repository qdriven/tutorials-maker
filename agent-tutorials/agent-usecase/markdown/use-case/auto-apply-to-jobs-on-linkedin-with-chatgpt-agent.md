# Auto-apply to jobs on LinkedIn with ChatGPT Agent

> A commenter said ChatGPT Agent applied to 40 LinkedIn jobs and led to 2 interviews, though they hit a monthly usage limit quickly.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** ChatGPT Agent  
**Source:** Reddit · Apr 1, 2026

## What they did

The thread is mostly opinions, but one commenter says ChatGPT Agent handled LinkedIn auto-applying for them. The source gives no workflow details beyond that it applied to 40 LinkedIn jobs on its own; there is also no description of what access it had or what the person had to check manually.

## How it went

That commenter says the run led to 2 interviews without outreach messages. The main limit they mention is that they quickly hit a monthly usage cap.

## Worth knowing

The only concrete catch in the source is the monthly limit: after 40 applications, the commenter says they were quickly capped.

## Prompt

```text
Using my logged-in LinkedIn, find [number] [job title] roles in [location] that match my resume and these criteria: [salary, remote, industry]. For each, tailor my application answers from the resume I've attached. Pause and show me each application summary before you submit anything, and finish with a list of what was applied to and what was skipped.
```
