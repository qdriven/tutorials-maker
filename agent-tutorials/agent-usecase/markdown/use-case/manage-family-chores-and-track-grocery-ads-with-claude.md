# Manage family chores and track grocery ads with Claude

> A user had Claude help with Home Assistant devices, family calendars and chores, and a daily scheduled check of grocery ads plus inventory tracking from plain-language messages.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Claude  
**Source:** Reddit · Aug 28, 2026

## What they did

In the thread, one user says they use Claude with their Home Assistant setup and smart devices via MCP, and another describes using plain-language household inputs to organize family life: checking calendars, generating meal plans from weekly grocery flyers, and routing shopping across up to three stores. For grocery tracking, a user says Claude in Chrome downloads store receipts from their Coles account, reviews specials, and can even pre-populate an online cart; the human still chooses whether to use that output.

## How it went

The clearest reported result is grocery optimization saving one user over $2,500 per year. The setup appears highly customized and the meal-planning builder was still not fully finished in the comment describing it.

## Worth knowing

One practical catch raised in the comments is privacy: a commenter warns that mixing sensitive personal data with browsing and tool access increases prompt-injection and data-exposure risk.

## Prompt

```text
Help me run my household: track chores for [family members], keep a family calendar, and keep a running inventory of [pantry/household items] that I update by telling you in plain language. Every day at [time], check the grocery ads from [store names or ad links] and tell me which sales match what I'm low on. Before changing any calendar event or controlling any smart-home device, show me what you plan to do and wait for my OK.
```
