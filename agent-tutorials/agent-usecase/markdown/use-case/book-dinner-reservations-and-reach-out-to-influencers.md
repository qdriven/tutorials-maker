# Book dinner reservations and reach out to influencers

> A commenter says the agent booked date-night dinner reservations and contacted about 20 social media influencers for another company.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** ChatGPT Agent  
**Source:** Reddit · Aug 19, 2025

## What they did

A commenter said ChatGPT’s agent handled a few live tasks for them: it made a date-night dinner reservation for the commenter and their wife, and for another company it reached out to about 20 social media influencers. They also used it to manage Base44 AI and fix a feature, though that required about 10 Base44 credits, and a later Cloudflare integration attempt stalled at the login step.

## How it went

They estimated it worked about 75% of the time and said they were overall pretty happy with it. The main miss they named was failing to get past the Cloudflare login during an API-layer setup between two frontend apps.

## Worth knowing

One concrete cost mentioned was burning about 10 Base44 credits to get it to fix a feature.

Find [number] restaurants in [city] for a [cuisine or vibe] dinner on [date] at [time] for [party size], and check availability on [booking site]. Then list the best options with price, rating, and available times. Pause and show me the exact reservation details before booking anything.
