# Book restaurants, movie tickets and haircuts with an agent

> A poster says they've used Muse for a couple of weeks to book restaurants, movie tickets, and haircut appointments.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** Reddit · Oct 17, 2026

## What they did

The poster says they used Meta Muse for a couple of weeks to handle everyday bookings by giving it about the same information they would give a human assistant. They say it navigated unfamiliar websites on its own to book restaurants, movie tickets, and haircut appointments, and when a booking was not open yet it set itself a reminder to follow up later.

## How it went

The reported result is that it completed those bookings and handled a simple delay case without step-by-step instructions. The thread gives no hard metrics, and commenters say this kind of success is still limited to basic, familiar tasks.

## Worth knowing

The main catch mentioned is reliability once circumstances change after a delayed task is queued; one commenter specifically questioned whether it would cancel an outdated goal before following up later.

## Prompt

```text
Help me book [restaurant / movie / haircut] in [city] for [date and time] for [number] people, with a backup time of [alternative]. Find available options on the booking site, fill in my details ([name, phone]), and stop before confirming or paying so I can review the final details. Once I approve, complete the booking and tell me the confirmation.
```
