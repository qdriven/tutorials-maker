# Shop hotel sites for a New York stay

> In a Skift test, Muse opened a browser and searched hotels for Nov 1-2 at $350-450 a night, suggesting the Marlton Hotel first.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** deeparrival.com · Sep 1, 2026

## What they did

This account relays Skift's test of Meta's Muse agent. Hotels have no unified booking rail, so Muse opened a browser and shopped consumer hotel sites directly, comparing published prices. The request was a New York City room for Nov 1-2 at $350-450 a night. Muse checks with the person before purchases and can pay through Stripe Link.

## How it went

Muse put the Marlton Hotel first, based on published prices. The source treats it as a shortlist rather than a confirmed booking, since a browser-shopped price is only as good as the page it came from.

## Worth knowing

Before approving, recheck dates, room type, taxes and the total on the hotel's own site. Hotel results reflect whatever the open web showed the agent that day.

## Prompt

```text
Search hotel sites for a stay in [city] from [check-in date] to [check-out date], at [min price] to [max price] per night, near [neighborhood or landmark]. Compare at least three sites and give me a table of the top five options with price, location, rating, cancellation terms and total cost with fees. Stop before booking and ask me which one I want.
```
