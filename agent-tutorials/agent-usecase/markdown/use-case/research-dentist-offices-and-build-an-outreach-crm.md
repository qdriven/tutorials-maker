# Research dentist offices and build an outreach CRM

> Manus found 40+ Los Angeles dental offices with contact details across directories, then built a CRM app with dashboard, profiles and notes.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Manus  
**Source:** nocode.mba · Undated

## What they did

The author started a second Manus agent while a game was building in another. They asked it to research Los Angeles dentist offices (name, phone, email) and build a CRM for outreach. The agent opened a browser, searched Yelp and other directories, worked around captcha blocks, then built the app on its own.

## How it went

It found 40+ offices with contact details and produced a CRM with a metrics dashboard, office profiles, activity logging and notes. The author doubts it could replace HubSpot for complex sales teams.

## Worth knowing

Credit use climbs on research-heavy tasks like this, and the generated app may need refinement before real use. The post is sponsored by Manus.

## Prompt

```text
Find [40] [type of business, e.g. dental offices] in [city] using online directories and Google Maps, and collect each one's name, address, phone, email, and website. Then build a simple CRM web app with a dashboard, a profile page per business, and a notes field for outreach. Stop when the app runs with the data loaded, and don't contact any of the businesses.
```
