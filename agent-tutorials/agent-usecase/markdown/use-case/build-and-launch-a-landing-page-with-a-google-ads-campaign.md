# Build and launch a landing page with a Google Ads campaign

> OpenClaw built and shipped a live landing page, wired lead capture with duplicate protection, set up a geo-targeted Google Ads campaign and a consent-mode tag.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** OpenClaw  
**Source:** X · Oct 10, 2026

## What they did

OpenClaw received the task to create an AI consultant landing page for South Florida. It built and shipped the page, added lead capture with duplicate protection, configured a search-only Google Ads campaign with geo-targeting, and installed a consent-mode tag. The user then published it live.

## How it went

The page is live at theonegroup.info/ai-consultant-south-florida. No further results or shortfalls are stated.
