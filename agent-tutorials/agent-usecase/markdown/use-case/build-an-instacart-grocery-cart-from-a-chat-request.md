# Build an Instacart grocery cart from a chat request

> A reporter connected Instacart to ChatGPT, which assembled the cart for formula, ice cream and fruit and opened a checkout confirmation in chat.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** ChatGPT  
**Source:** techradar.com · Undated

## What they did

The reporter connected an Instacart account through ChatGPT's app list, then asked for infant formula, a small container of vanilla ice cream and some fruit for delivery. ChatGPT suggested products and put options in the cart without much discussion. When the reporter said to check out, a window opened in the chat confirming the order, payment method and delivery address.

## How it went

ChatGPT built the cart quickly and kept checkout inside the chat. The same list in Claude produced very similar products, but Claude only gave a link to finish the purchase in Instacart's site.

## Worth knowing

The test used a deliberately simple three-item list and did not compare prices or delivery times. Specific requests would probably matter more for getting the items you want.

## Prompt

```text
Using my connected Instacart account, build a cart at [store name] for: [list of items, with brands or sizes if it matters]. Choose reasonable matches and tell me about any substitutions. Stop when the cart is ready and show me the full cart and total, and do not place the order until I confirm.
```
