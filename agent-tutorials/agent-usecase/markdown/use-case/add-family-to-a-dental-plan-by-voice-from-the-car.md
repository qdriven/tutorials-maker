# Add family to a dental plan by voice from the car

> A user relayed instructions through their car to a bot that searched email for documents and added family members to their dental plan while driving.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** X · Oct 14, 2026

## What they did

The user spoke instructions to their car while driving, which relayed them to the bot. The bot searched the user's email for documents and sent them to add family members to the dental plan. The user only provided the initial voice instructions.

## How it went

Family members were added to the dental plan. The full task took a total of 2 minutes.

## Worth knowing

The entire process took 2 minutes.

## Prompt

```text
Search my email for documents about my [dental plan/provider] and find the enrollment requirements for adding [family members' names]. Collect the details you need, like dates of birth and the plan number, and tell me what's missing. Before submitting anything or agreeing to any cost change, read me the full summary and wait for my yes.
```
