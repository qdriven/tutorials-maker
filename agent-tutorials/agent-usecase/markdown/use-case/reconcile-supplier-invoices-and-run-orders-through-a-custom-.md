# Reconcile supplier invoices and run orders through a custom CRM

> A business owner uses AI to auto-reconcile supplier invoices and report on pricing and freight costs, plus a custom CRM that orders from suppliers and invoices clients via Zoho Books API.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** Reddit · Undated

## What they did

In a comment, the business owner says AI helped them build several internal tools rather than run them end to end. They describe bookkeeping that auto-reconciles supplier invoices and reports pricing and freight-cost changes, plus a custom CRM that generates mailers from call notes, lets them place supplier orders, and invoices clients through the Zoho Books API.

## How it went

They say it became a one-stop shop for orders and invoicing and saved them a ton of time. The post gives no metrics, process detail, or specific limits.

## Worth knowing

The main catch is that the source is very thin: it names the automations and Zoho Books API, but gives no build time, cost, or error-rate details.

## Prompt

```text
Go through the supplier invoices in [folder path] and reconcile them against my purchase orders in [file or spreadsheet]. Produce a report showing price changes by item, freight costs per supplier, and any mismatches or duplicate charges. You're done when I have one summary spreadsheet with flagged discrepancies. Do not send, edit, or delete anything in my accounting system without showing me first.
```
