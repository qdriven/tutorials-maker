# Run outbound sales from research to booked demos

> A solo SaaS founder gave an agent his ICP, calendar and inbox; it researched 300 companies, qualified 61, wrote personalized emails and booked 14 demos.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Dots  
**Source:** X · Oct 12, 2026

## What they did

Solo founder gave OpenAI Dots his ICP, calendar and inbox plus one prompt. The agent ran recurring jobs: scholar researched news/hiring/funding/tech for targets, watcher spotted signals, bouncer disqualified before outreach, builder wrote one fact-based first line per email, manager handled replies with calendar slots and produced one-page briefs. It acted autonomously after the first 20 emails, max 30/day, stopping on no-interest replies.

## How it went

Over four days it researched 300 companies, qualified 61, sent emails, got 18 replies and booked 14 demos for $186k pipeline; nine were disqualified for size or budget.

## Worth knowing

Founder reviewed and approved only the first 20 emails before full autonomy.

## Prompt

```text
Run outbound sales for my product, [what you sell]. My ideal customer is [industry, company size, job titles, other criteria]. Research [number] companies that fit, qualify them against those criteria, and give me a list showing why each qualified. For each qualified company, draft a personalized email to [contact role] that mentions something specific you found, and propose times from my calendar for a demo. Pause and show me the full list and all drafts before sending any email or booking anything on my calendar, and stop once I have approved the batch.
```
