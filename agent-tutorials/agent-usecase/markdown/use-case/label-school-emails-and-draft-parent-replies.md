# Label school emails and draft parent replies

> A parent's agent labels school and parent emails, drafts replies she edits and sends, and asks before adding dates to her calendar.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** chiefhouseholdofficer.substack.com · Undated

## What they did

She set up a Gmail label, "School + Parent", that automatically collects mail from teachers, school admin and PTA contacts. A GPT-based agent, connected through Gmail and ChatGPT, scans that folder once a day and writes a morning summary of key points, deadlines and needed replies. It drafts replies to routine requests, which she edits and sends. It asks before adding events to her calendar.

## How it went

She says she no longer misses events or answers late, and her inbox feels manageable. The source gives no measured results, such as time saved.

## Worth knowing

The agent is built from a Gmail filter plus a ChatGPT-based agent. Replies are drafted from her stated preferences, and she edits each one before sending.

## Prompt

```text
Set up an assistant that watches my inbox for emails from [school domains or sender names] and from other parents, and labels them [labels, e.g. School, Activities, Permission slips]. For each one that needs a response, write a draft reply in my voice and leave it for me to edit and send. If an email mentions a date or event, ask me before adding it to my calendar. Stop when the labeling and drafting are working on my last [number] emails.
```
