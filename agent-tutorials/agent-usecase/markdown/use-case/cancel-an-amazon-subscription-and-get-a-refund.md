# Cancel an Amazon subscription and get a refund

> Codex navigated Amazon settings to cancel a subscription, then chatted with customer service and secured an immediate $15 refund.

**Category:** Money, Bills & Benefits  
**Agent:** Codex  
**Source:** X · Apr 29, 2026

## What they did

The user asked Codex to obtain a refund for an Amazon subscription. Codex navigated the account settings to cancel it, then moved to the help page and opened a live chat with an associate. It explained that the subscription showed active only due to the billing period but requested immediate stop and refund, handled the full exchange, and closed the chat.

## How it went

The associate approved the refund. Codex obtained an immediate $15 and the user reported $400 total across multiple subscriptions that night, all completed in about five minutes.

## Worth knowing

The entire process took five minutes and nearly paid for the agent itself.
