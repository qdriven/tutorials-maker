# Turn videos and company research into slide decks

> The author describes Manus watching a YouTube video to make a slide deck and researching companies for investment potential into a presentation.

**Category:** Content & Creative  
**Agent:** Manus  
**Source:** ainutpam.substack.com · Undated

## What they did

The author gives Manus a goal and says it plans the steps on its own. In one task it watches a YouTube video, works out what it covers, and turns it into a slide deck. In another it researches companies for investment potential and builds a presentation. The post gives no step-by-step detail. Side-by-side videos against ChatGPT are on a linked page.

## How it went

The post gives no figures or concrete results for these two tasks, only the claim that Manus delivers finished decks. The comparison videos sit on a separate blog, so the outputs can't be judged here.

## Worth knowing

Manus can hallucinate, so check the research. It also uses credits, though new users get some free ones to test.

## Prompt

```text
Watch this video [YouTube URL] and turn it into a [number, e.g. 10]-slide deck that captures the key points, with a slide for any data or quotes worth keeping. Finish with an exported presentation and a short list of timestamps for the main claims.
```
