# Negotiate a car purchase with ChatGPT

> A parent says ChatGPT built a purchase checklist and guided negotiation strategy, helping their daughter save over $3,000 on a car.

**Category:** Money, Bills & Benefits  
**Agent:** ChatGPT  
**Source:** Reddit · Jun 1, 2025

## What they did

The parent had their daughter paste a detailed ChatGPT prompt while she was at a dealership, feeding it the exact car, location, VIN, and credit score and asking for incentives, nearby comparable listings, negotiation guidance, warranty distinctions, and dealer add-on warnings. ChatGPT surfaced missed factory incentives, cheaper matching cars nearby, and a walk-away script; after she nearly signed, she left, then returned the next day with her parent and an out-the-door quote to buy elsewhere.

## How it went

The daughter bought the same trim at another dealership, in her preferred color, with a better warranty, and the parent says she saved over $3,000. The poster also says VIN-specific lookups only worked in some cases, so the tool was not reliable for every VIN.

## Worth knowing

The parent says this was not a one-shot prompt: they co-refined it with ChatGPT almost daily for more than a month before using the final version.

## Prompt

```text
I'm buying a [new/used] [make and model] with a budget of [amount] and a financing plan of [cash/loan details]. Build me a checklist for inspecting and buying it, then coach me through negotiating: give me an opening offer, a walk-away price, and scripts for common dealer tactics. When I paste the dealer's replies, help me answer each one. Finish with a summary of the final deal terms to verify before I sign.
```
