# Make a music video from a reference

> Given a song, lyric timing data, images and a reference video, Grok Bot produced a music video with transitions and text effects in minutes of hands-on time.

**Category:** Content & Creative  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** note.com · Undated

## What they did

Taku loaded a song and its lyrics into an app he is building, which produced analysis data such as song structure and lyric timing. He gave Grok Bot that data, the audio, his earlier illustrations and a reference YouTube video, then asked it to make an MV like the reference. The Bot chose images, transitions, lyric text effects and motion, and also handled the YouTube upload.

## How it went

A finished MV came out of that one request, and Taku says his hands-on time was probably just a few minutes. Anything he disliked could be changed by telling the Bot. The source gives no quality critique.

## Worth knowing

The few minutes assumes you already have the images, the song and the analysis data. Making the original MV by hand took far more effort.

## Prompt

```text
Make a music video using my song [audio file], the lyric timing data [file], these images [folder], and this reference video for style [link or file]. Add transitions and text effects that follow the lyric timing. Finish with a rendered video file and tell me what you'd tweak if I want a second version.
```
