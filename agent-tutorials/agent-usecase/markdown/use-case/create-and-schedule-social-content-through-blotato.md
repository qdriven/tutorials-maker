# Create and schedule social content through Blotato

> Cowork with the Blotato connector produces a LinkedIn post with infographic, turns a YouTube video into a carousel, and schedules posts across platforms.

**Category:** Content & Creative  
**Agent:** Claude Cowork  
**Source:** blotato.com · Undated

## What they did

The author, Blotato's founder, added Blotato to Cowork as a custom connector by pasting its MCP URL and approving OAuth. She keeps a content folder with brand voice and platform-rule files that Cowork reads on every task. Cowork then drafted a LinkedIn post with a generated infographic, turned a YouTube video into a carousel, and scheduled posts. She starts on manual approval and keeps final approval on every piece.

## How it went

The source shows Cowork listing the next five scheduled posts across Twitter, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook and TikTok. It gives no measured results for these specific tasks. The demo itself is in a video.

## Worth knowing

You need a paid Claude plan and a paid Blotato plan with a social account connected. Cowork uses up usage limits faster than chat, and automatic approval uses more still.

## Prompt

```text
Connect to my Blotato account and create a LinkedIn post with an infographic from [topic or data], plus turn this YouTube video [link] into a carousel. Show me drafts and scheduled times across platforms, and pause for my approval before anything is actually scheduled or published.
```
