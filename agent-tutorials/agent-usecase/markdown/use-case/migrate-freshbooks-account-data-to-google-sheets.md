# Migrate FreshBooks account data to Google Sheets

> A commenter used Comet to move five years of FreshBooks account data into Google Sheets.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** Perplexity Comet  
**Source:** Reddit · Mar 1, 2026

## What they did

A commenter said they used Comet to move five years of account data from FreshBooks into Google Sheets because FreshBooks’ exports were inadequate. They said Comet’s AI assistant copied the data and verified it on its own while they worked on other tasks, and they also used it to navigate support on complex sites they did not know well.

## How it went

They described it as very helpful for people who live and work online. The only concrete limitation they gave was that FreshBooks itself did not provide decent exports.

## Worth knowing

The practical catch was needing the browser agent because FreshBooks’ built-in export options were not good enough.

## Prompt

```text
Log in to my FreshBooks account (I'll handle any login or 2FA prompts) and move my [invoices, expenses, clients] from [start year] to [end year] into a Google Sheet called [sheet name], with one tab per data type and clear column headers. When finished, tell me the row counts for each tab and compare them against FreshBooks so I can confirm nothing is missing. Don't change or delete anything in FreshBooks.
```
