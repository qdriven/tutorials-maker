# Shop for Raspberry Pi parts on Amazon with Claude

> Claude searched Amazon for a Raspberry Pi, asked about mic and speaker options, and added items to the basket in about 15 minutes, slower than manual.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** Claude computer use  
**Source:** tech.yahoo.com · Undated

## What they did

The author turned on Computer Use in the Claude desktop app on a Mac and granted accessibility and screen-recording permissions. They asked Claude to find parts for a local Home Assistant voice assistant on Amazon, checking review scores. Claude searched, paused to ask whether they wanted a basic USB mic and speaker or a ReSpeaker HAT, then clicked to add items to the basket. The author stayed signed out of Amazon.

## How it went

The basket held suitable, well-reviewed parts after about 15 minutes, but the author could have done it faster by hand. Session tool-call limits kept stopping it until they clicked Continue.

## Worth knowing

Computer Use is a macOS-only research preview for Pro and Max users. Leave Amazon signed out, since the author wouldn't let it near the buy button.

## Prompt

```text
Search Amazon for the parts I need to build [project, e.g. a Raspberry Pi voice assistant], including [main board] and accessories like [microphone, speaker, power supply, case]. Ask me about options and compatibility where it matters, then add your picks to my basket. Pause before checkout and show me the list with prices; do not buy anything.
```
