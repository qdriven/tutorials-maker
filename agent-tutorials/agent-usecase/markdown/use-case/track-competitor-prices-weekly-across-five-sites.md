# Track competitor prices weekly across five sites

> An agent opens five brand sites every Monday, compares against last week's snapshot, captures timestamped screenshots of changes, and sends a report.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** X · Sep 21, 2026

## What they did

User gives QwenWork the one-time instruction to check five brand sites for price changes every Monday at 9 AM and send a report. The agent opens each site on schedule, compares current prices to the previous week's snapshot, extracts changes such as higher prices, markdowns or promotions, and attaches a timestamped screenshot of each.

## How it went

The completed report arrives automatically each Monday at 9 AM with every detected change listed.

## Prompt

```text
Every Monday at [time], visit these sites: [list of five URLs] and record the prices for [products or categories]. Compare them to last week's snapshot stored in [spreadsheet or doc], and take timestamped screenshots of anything that changed. You are done when a report of the changes is sent to [my email or Slack channel].
```
