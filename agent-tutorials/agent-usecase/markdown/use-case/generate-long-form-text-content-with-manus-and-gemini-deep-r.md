# Generate long-form text content with Manus and Gemini Deep Research

> A self-taught creator used Manus together with Gemini 2.5 Pro Deep Research for long-form text content generation work.

**Category:** Content & Creative  
**Agent:** Manus, Gemini  
**Source:** Reddit · Mar 1, 2025

## What they did

The poster says they paired Manus with Gemini 2.5 Pro Deep Research and a prompting method they call “fractal iteration,” using hierarchical decomposition, style guides, planning structure, and .md/PDF modules. They used that setup to generate and assemble long-form works, including a 300+ page nonfiction book, a 255-page AI ethics project with chapters assembled after generation, and a 100-story collection, while the person directed the methodology and kept extensive notes.

## How it went

They report producing a 300+ page nonfiction book, half of its first chapter, and 100 short stories totaling over 225,000 words, and say later-produced sections still matched style and voice. In comments, they frame the result as strong for cohesive long output, not for hiding AI-written tells.

## Worth knowing

The main catch is that the poster attributes the results to the methodology plus collaboration with Manus, and says they kept extensive notes while assembling chapters after generation.

## Prompt

```text
Research [topic] in depth using multiple reputable sources, then write a long-form [article, report, or guide] of about [word count] words for [audience] in a [tone] style. Include a source list and flag any claims you could not verify. Finished means a complete draft I can edit, with an outline up front.
```
