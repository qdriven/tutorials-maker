# Run a side hustle with three agents for content, email and books

> A working parent uses three agents: one researches and drafts content, one handles client emails and books calls, one categorizes Etsy income and expenses and writes monthly summaries. · Oct 18, 2026

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** aimomlab.com · Oct 18, 2026 · Submitted by from search gap: side hustle ceo

## What they did

Nova gave each of three agents a narrow job with fixed boundaries. The researcher gets a niche, audience and approved sources, then returns an outline and draft. The ops agent drafts client emails in her voice and books calls around her calendar, with no calls before 9am or after 3pm. The finance agent categorizes Etsy income and expenses, flags oddities with a one-sentence reason, and writes a monthly summary. She reviews and edits the output.

## How it went

She reports about 6-8 hours reclaimed per week. Content drops from 3-4 hours per piece to about 40 minutes of editing. No accuracy figures are given for the finance agent.

## Worth knowing

The post doesn't name the tools or models used, so you'd have to choose your own. Her advice is to keep each agent's scope narrow, with no overlap between them.

## Prompt

```text
Set up three agents for my [type of side business]. Agent one researches [topics] and drafts [content type] into [doc or folder]. Agent two reads client emails in [inbox], drafts replies and suggests call times from my calendar. Agent three categorizes income and expenses from [Etsy export or sheet] and writes a monthly summary. Pause and show me every email reply and calendar booking before it is sent.
```
