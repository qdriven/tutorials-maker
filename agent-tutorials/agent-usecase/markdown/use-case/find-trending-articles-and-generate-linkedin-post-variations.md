# Find trending articles and generate LinkedIn post variations

> A tutorial shows Operator searching Google for trending eCommerce articles, picking one, and using EasyGen to generate 10 LinkedIn post variations, pausing for the user to log in.

**Category:** Content & Creative  
**Agent:** Operator  
**Source:** gptcentral.substack.com · Undated

## What they did

In the tutorial, Operator was asked to create 10 LinkedIn post variations from eCommerce news. It searched Google for trending eCommerce articles and picked one about digital shopping trends. It then asked the user to log into EasyGen, copied the article text into the tool, and generated the posts.

## How it went

Operator produced 10 different LinkedIn posts from the chosen article. The source gives no quality assessment, timing, or cost.

## Worth knowing

Operator stops for you to log into EasyGen, so be ready to sign in. The tutorial says other content or scheduling tools could work the same way.

## Prompt

```text
Search Google for trending articles about [topic, e.g. eCommerce] from the past [week], pick the most relevant one and tell me why. Then open [post generator tool, e.g. EasyGen], pause so I can log in, and generate 10 LinkedIn post variations based on that article. Finish by listing all 10 in one place, and do not publish anything to LinkedIn.
```
