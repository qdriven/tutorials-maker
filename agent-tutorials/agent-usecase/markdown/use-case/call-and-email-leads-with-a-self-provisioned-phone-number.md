# Call and email leads with a self-provisioned phone number

> An AI agent gave itself a phone number, found fitting leads, called and emailed them, followed up and reported results.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** X · Oct 2, 2026

## What they did

The agent assigns itself a phone number and business email. It finds leads that fit the business, then handles calls, emails, and follow-ups while qualifying interest, all without user checks or instructions.

## How it went

It runs continuously year-round and reports who is interested.

## Prompt

```text
Build an outreach assistant that finds [number] leads matching [ideal customer description] in [region], gets them a phone number and email, and contacts them about [my offer]. It should follow up after [number] days if there's no reply and send me a summary of who replied and what happened. Before it places any call or sends any email, have it show me the script and message list for approval.
```
