# Claim refunds for delayed or cancelled flights with Claude

> Claude gathers the facts, researches the rules and drafts the refund request email after a delayed or cancelled flight.

**Category:** Money, Bills & Benefits  
**Agent:** Claude  
**Source:** Reddit · Oct 12, 2026

## What they did

The post describes a cousin who had booked a flight through Claude using a custom MCP connector, so Claude already had the flight details in context. When the flight had a major delay, he told Claude about the disruption and asked it to check U.S. DOT rules and draft the claim request; Claude researched the delay and refund rules and said he was entitled to a refund.

## How it went

The claimed result was a full ticket refund, framed as avoiding claims companies that take a large commission. In the comments, others note this was just a refund rather than extra compensation, and that some airlines may let passengers cancel in the app without waiting in line.

## Worth knowing

The main catch in the comments is that refund rules were changing again on October 19, 2026, so anyone copying this would need Claude to use the latest rules.

## Prompt

```text
My flight [airline, flight number] on [date] from [origin] to [destination] was [delayed by X hours/cancelled]. Ask me any questions you need, then research which refund or compensation rules apply to this route. Finish by drafting a refund request email with the facts, the rule, the amount I'm claiming, and a reply deadline, and list the documents to attach. I'll send it myself.
```
