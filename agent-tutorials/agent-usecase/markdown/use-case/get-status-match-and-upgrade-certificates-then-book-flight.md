# Get status match and upgrade certificates, then book flight

> John's Dot found a status match, got four move-to-lie-flat seat certificates, filled the forms, confirmed by email, read the terms and booked the flight.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Dots  
**Source:** X · Oct 4, 2026

## What they did

John's dot located a status match and obtained four move-to-lie-flat seat certificates. It completed the required forms, received the email confirmations, reviewed the terms, and booked the flight on its own.

## How it went

John secured the four certificates and the upgraded booking. He noted he would have skipped the process and missed the opportunity entirely.

## Prompt

```text
I want to fly [route] around [dates] with [airline]. Look into status match options from my [current airline status], any upgrade or seat certificates I can get, and fill out the needed forms using my details. Pause and show me the terms, cost and final itinerary before you submit anything or book, then confirm by email once done.
```
