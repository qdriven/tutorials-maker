# Email a daily AI news brief and build slide decks from tabs

> Comet searches the day's AI news, picks the top 5 stories and emails a brief, and merges tagged tabs and reports into a cited Google Slides deck.

**Category:** Research & Knowledge  
**Agent:** Perplexity Comet  
**Source:** thecreatorsai.com · Undated

## What they did

In Comet, Perplexity's AI browser, the author asked it to search the day's AI news across major outlets, pick the top 5 stories and email a brief. This can be saved as a shortcut or a recurring task. For decks, she tagged open tabs, articles and reports and asked Comet to merge them into a Google Slides presentation with headings, citations and an executive summary.

## How it went

The email brief was actually sent, which plain chatbots can't do. The slide deck was not great design-wise, but it covered part of the work. The source gives no timings or counts.

## Worth knowing

Keep prompts short and focused. Long multi-step requests often stop partway through, and Comet can be slower than doing the task by hand.

## Prompt

```text
Every morning, search for the latest news on [topic, e.g. AI], pick the top 5 stories, and write a short brief with a two-sentence summary and source link for each. Show me the draft email before sending it to [my email address]. Then, once I confirm, send it.
```
