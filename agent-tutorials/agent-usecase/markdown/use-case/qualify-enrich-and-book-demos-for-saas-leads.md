# Qualify, enrich and book demos for SaaS leads

> A Lindy agent qualifies, enriches and books demos around the clock for SaaS lead gen, with mock conversion reported rising from 40% to 78%.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Lindy  
**Source:** X · May 11, 2026

## What they did

A Lindy agent was set up with three prompts to qualify, enrich and book demos 24/7 for SaaS leads. It replaced hours of manual work on incoming leads.

## How it went

Mock conversion rose from 40% to 78%.

## Prompt

```text
Build an agent for my [product name] that responds to new demo requests from [form or inbox]. It should qualify each lead on [criteria, e.g. company size, role, use case], enrich it with public company info, and offer demo slots from [calendar]. Test it on three sample leads and show me the messages it would send before it goes live.
```
