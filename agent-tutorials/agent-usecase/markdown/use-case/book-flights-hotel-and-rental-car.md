# Book flights, hotel and rental car

> Grok Bot booked flights, a hotel and a rental car as part of handling the user's life admin, alongside email management.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** X · Oct 10, 2026

## What they did

The user gave Grok bot access to email and booking services for life admin tasks. The agent booked flights, hotel and rental car. It also trashed 1,000+ junk emails, tracked 7 Amazon returns, merged 17 PRs, and set up its own email account.

## How it went

All listed tasks were completed. No shortfalls stated.

## Prompt

```text
I'm traveling from [origin] to [destination] from [start date] to [end date], with a budget of [amount]. Find a flight, a hotel near [area or landmark], and a rental car that fit my preferences: [preferences]. Put the best option for each in a short comparison, and pause for my approval before booking or paying for anything.
```
