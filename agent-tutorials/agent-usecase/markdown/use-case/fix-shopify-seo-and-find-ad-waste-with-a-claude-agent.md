# Fix Shopify SEO and find ad waste with a Claude agent

> A Claude-based agent analyzed a Shopify store, fixed SEO issues raising the score to 95, found ad waste, tested ideas and checked AI visibility.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Claude  
**Source:** X · Sep 28, 2026

## What they did

The user built an agent in Claude, gave it access to their Shopify store, and prompted it to analyze. The agent produced a full SEO report identifying issues, applied fixes directly, scanned ad spend for waste, ran tests on new ad concepts, and generated performance charts. It also checked the store’s mentions across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity.

## How it went

The store’s SEO score rose to 95. The agent revealed ad waste and an AI-visibility score of 23 out of 100 on the demo store. All work completed in minutes for the report and one evening total, with no agency involvement.

## Worth knowing

The process cost almost nothing and required only one evening instead of weeks.

## Prompt

```text
Audit my Shopify store at [store URL] for SEO problems such as titles, meta descriptions, alt text and broken links, and show me the list of proposed fixes. Also review [ad account or export] for wasted ad spend and check whether AI assistants mention the store. Pause and show me each change before you save it in the admin.
```
