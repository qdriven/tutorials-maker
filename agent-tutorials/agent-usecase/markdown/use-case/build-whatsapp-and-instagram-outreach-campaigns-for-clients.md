# Build WhatsApp and Instagram outreach campaigns for clients

> An agency uses Grok Bot to check WhatsApp and Instagram accounts, build a campaign and enroll 900 people in a DM sequence; it doesn't send messages itself.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** roasbeast.com · Undated

## What they did

The agency attaches WarmySender to Grok Bot as a custom connector, using a workspace API key, and withholds the bot's browser. In one weekly conversation it checks the health of numbers and handles across four client workspaces. It builds a WhatsApp campaign for a number that has finished its ramp. It enrolls 900 people in an Instagram DM sequence (follow, view, like, comment, message). Humans connect accounts and read replies.

## How it went

The 900 enrollment drains over weeks at the scheduler's safe pace, not in a burst. The agent can explain a quiet account, for example a daily ceiling reached. It can't send messages, raise limits, or reply.

## Worth knowing

Expect slow delivery. Pacing limits and new-account ramps are fixed, and an agent can't raise or shorten them.

## Prompt

```text
Check that the [WhatsApp and Instagram] accounts for [client name] are connected and working in [outreach tool]. Build a campaign with a [number]-step DM sequence for [audience list], using this tone and offer: [description]. Before enrolling anyone, pause and show me the messages, the audience, and the schedule, and stop once I've approved and the campaign is set up.
```
