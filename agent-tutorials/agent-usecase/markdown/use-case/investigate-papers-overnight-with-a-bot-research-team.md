# Investigate papers overnight with a bot research team

> Grok Bot roles read new papers and extract claims, rerun analyses, and scan sources, working overnight to produce briefs.

**Category:** Research & Knowledge  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** X · Oct 4, 2026

## What they did

A shared brief set the objective, sources, deliverable, and deadline for five specialized Grok Bots. One bot per stage read new papers and extracted claims with sources and confidence notes, traced failed experiments and tested fixes, reran analyses and updated figures, scanned repositories for evidence, and compiled verified findings into a brief; each handoff listed completed and pending items. Sending, publishing, or spending required human approval.

## How it went

The bots investigated, reproduced, analyzed, and drafted overnight while the user slept, with final authority held until morning approval.

## Prompt

```text
Overnight, find new papers published this week on [research topic] from [sources such as arXiv or journals]. For each of the top [number], extract the main claims and note whether the methods look sound. Have my brief waiting for me by [time], with links to every source and a note on anything you could not verify.
```
