# Automate invoice organizing and monthly invoice emails

> The author demos Cursor sorting inconsistently named invoice files and handling monthly invoicing by editing a PDF, attaching it, checking the recipient and sending the email.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** Cursor  
**Source:** note.com · Undated

## What they did

The author opened a folder of invoices with inconsistent names and formats in Cursor and gave instructions. The agent read the files, renamed them and moved them into folders. For monthly invoicing, a second demo had Cursor edit a PDF invoice, attach it, check the recipient and send the email. Recipients are listed in a CSV.

## How it went

The author says the tasks, about 10 minutes a month for filing and 10 to 20 minutes for invoice emails, can be compressed. The source gives no measured results; both are video demos.

## Worth knowing

To add more invoices, the author says you only need to add rows to the CSV. The demos are only in embedded videos, so check them for the exact prompts.

## Prompt

```text
Look through my invoices folder [path], rename the files to a consistent pattern like [YYYY-MM-vendor-amount], and sort them into [folders by year or client]. Show me the planned renames before applying them. Then prepare this month's invoice for [client] from my template [file], attach it to an email draft, and pause for me to review the recipient and contents before sending.
```
