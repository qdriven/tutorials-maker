# Scan thrift store items and flag good deals

> An agent processes video frames from a thrift store, looks up online prices for items, and alerts the user to good deals.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** X · Sep 23, 2026

## What they did

Wes Bos built an agent that processes video frames captured while scanning thrift store items, identifies objects in the frames, queries online prices for each, and alerts on good deals.

## How it went

The builder reported being shocked at how well the agent worked.

## Prompt

```text
I'll share photos or video frames from a thrift store. For each item you can identify, look up typical resale prices on [eBay, Etsy, or other sites] and tell me which ones are priced well below resale value. You are done when I have a ranked list of the best deals with the asking price, expected resale price, and your confidence for each.
```
