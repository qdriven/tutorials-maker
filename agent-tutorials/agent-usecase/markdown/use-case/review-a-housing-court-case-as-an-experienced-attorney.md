# Review a housing court case as an experienced attorney

> After Grok led to a missed filing, a commenter had Claude review their housing court case in a new chat as an experienced housing attorney.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Claude  
**Source:** Reddit · Feb 14, 2026

## What they did

The poster said Grok led them to miss an important housing-court filing, then they switched to Claude to draft an opposition. In a fresh chat they asked Claude to review it as an experienced housing attorney, which changed the brief’s tone to admit the plaintiff’s mistakes; in another separate chat they asked for a seasoned-attorney review and Claude swung back toward blaming the defendant, leaving the poster unsure what to file.

## How it went

The concrete result was two conflicting rewritten versions of the same filing rather than a clear answer. The thread also surfaced that new Claude chats do not carry over prior chat context unless the user re-uploads material or keeps working in the same thread/project.

## Worth knowing

The main catch was context management: starting a new chat reset the case history, so separate sessions produced inconsistent advice.

## Prompt

```text
Act as an experienced [housing / area of law] attorney in [state or jurisdiction] reviewing my case. Here are the facts, documents and timeline: [paste them]. Tell me the strengths and weaknesses, any filing deadlines or procedural steps I may have missed or still need to meet, and what to do next, flagging where I should verify with the court's rules or a licensed lawyer.
```
