# Delegate medical paperwork, finances and lease renewal to a personal agent

> The author gave a persistent OpenClaw agent limited access to email, calendar, files and messaging; in week one it processed medical paperwork, analyzed finances, worked on a lease renewal, and spawned five specialized agents.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** OpenClaw  
**Source:** rettig.substack.com · Undated

## What they did

Lane Rettig set up a persistent agent, Baz, with OpenClaw on a dedicated machine. It had limited access to email, calendar, files and chat apps, plus a memory file it reads each session. It scanned 34 pages of hospital forms, drafted an insurance pre-approval, and ran lease comps and cash-flow models. He reviewed everything before anything went out.

## How it went

The paperwork took one afternoon, where he estimated a weekend. The lease analysis improved after he pushed back on a thin first pass. Delegation stayed partial: every external action needed his approval, and booking flights and spending money weren't possible.

## Worth knowing

Each service integration (email, calendar, Slack, Telegram) took 30-60 minutes to set up by hand, repeated for every extra agent, and the author says a non-technical person couldn't do it today.

## Prompt

```text
Act as my personal admin assistant with read access to my [email account], [calendar], and the folder [path to documents]. In the first week, sort my [medical paperwork/bills], summarize what needs action and by when, and draft a plan for [lease renewal or other task]. Do not send any messages, make payments, or delete files without showing me exactly what you are about to do and getting my approval.
```
