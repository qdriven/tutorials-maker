# Compile and filter apartment listings

> A renter used Claude and ChatGPT to compile property-management sites and filter listings; results were decent but included some out-of-parameter listings, and he found a place through a friend.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Claude, ChatGPT  
**Source:** almanacnews.com · Oct 6, 2026

## What they did

Renno Mihalis, 23, had one week to find an apartment in San Francisco after getting a job offer. Rather than using a single rental platform, he used Claude and ChatGPT to compile a list of property management websites. He set search parameters for price, location and apartment size.

## How it went

The AI did a pretty good job listing apartments within his parameters, but sometimes included ones outside them. He ended up finding a place through a family friend's vacancy.

## Worth knowing

Mihalis said AI might have helped more if he'd had more than a week, so check every result against your own criteria.

## Prompt

```text
Visit the property-management sites for [list of buildings or companies] in [city] and compile every available listing that fits my criteria: [bedrooms], under [rent] per month, [pets or parking needs], available by [date]. Put them in a table with address, rent, availability, and link, and flag any listing that doesn't exactly meet my criteria. Don't submit applications or contact forms without showing me first.
```
