# Auto-post blog summaries to Bluesky from RSS

> A Zapier agent watches an RSS feed and posts content summaries to Bluesky, helping manage blogging across Medium, Buy Me a Coffee and Bluesky.

**Category:** Content & Creative  
**Agent:** Zapier Agents  
**Source:** medium.com · Undated

## What they did

The author built a Zapier Central agent from scratch with an RSS by Zapier trigger on their Buy Me a Coffee feed. They connected Bluesky's Create Post action, uploaded a media style guide as a knowledge source, and told the agent to draft a punchy hook pointing readers to Medium. The tool fields were left for the agent to generate. They tested in Chat Preview before publishing.

## How it went

The agent is live and keeps Bluesky stocked with fresh hooks for Medium articles while the author is away. The article gives no post counts or engagement results.

## Worth knowing

The free plan allows 400 activities a month. The Require Approval toggle lets you review each drafted post before it is sent.

## Prompt

```text
Watch this RSS feed: [your blog RSS URL]. When a new post appears, write a short summary of under [300] characters in my voice with a link to the post, and draft it for [Bluesky account]. Show me the draft and wait for my approval before posting anything; the job is done when approved posts go live once per new article.
```
