# Scan Marketplace and handle errands with Meta Muse

> Commenters say they use Muse to build expense trackers, schedule meetings, send follow-up emails, and scan Facebook Marketplace for toys, negotiate, and check condition.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** Reddit · Sep 17, 2026

## What they did

Commenters describe using Muse as a personal agent through the app to handle small tasks end to end. One user said they tell it to scan Facebook Marketplace for toys, negotiate, check that items are in good condition, and alert them when something is a good deal or free to pick up; another said they used it to create expense trackers, schedule meetings, send follow-up emails, and find items to buy.

## How it went

The clearest reported result was one user saying it had gotten two toys for their daughter so far. Another commenter said using it for subscription cancellations and Macy’s price-adjustment chats saved about 5–10 minutes per task.

## Worth knowing

Several commenters mention signing up with a referral code that gave them 1 billion starting tokens, but the thread gives no clearer pricing or usage limits.

## Prompt

```text
Scan Facebook Marketplace near [city] for [item type] under [price], and shortlist the five best listings based on price, photos, and described condition. For each, note any red flags and draft a polite offer message at about [percent] below asking. Show me the shortlist and drafts and wait for my approval before sending any message.
```
