# Produce a full video with an agent assistant

> Grok Imagine's agent mode suggested ideas, organized and assisted through making a full video in one hour.

**Category:** Content & Creative  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** X · May 2, 2026

## What they did

The user chatted with Grok Imagine in their native language. The agent suggested ideas, organized the project, and assisted throughout production as a full assistant rather than only generating images or video clips. A person directed the overall workflow and final assembly.

## How it went

One complete video was finished. The source states the process took one hour.

## Worth knowing

The full video was made in one hour.

## Prompt

```text
Help me make a [length]-long video about [topic or story] in Grok Imagine. Suggest three concept ideas first, then once I pick one, organize the shot list, generate the clips, and assemble them into a finished video. Stop when you have one complete video file and tell me what you would tweak next.
```
