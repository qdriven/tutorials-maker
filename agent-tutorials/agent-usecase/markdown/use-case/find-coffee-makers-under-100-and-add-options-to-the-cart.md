# Find coffee makers under $100 and add options to the cart

> Perplexity Comet searched Amazon for coffee makers with a grinder, built a comparison table and added two options to the cart, stopping before purchase.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** Perplexity Comet  
**Source:** dreamhost.com · Undated

## What they did

DreamHost's testers gave Comet one plain-language prompt. It was to go to Amazon, research the best coffee makers under $100 that grind beans, build a table of the top 5, and add two to the cart with a New York address selected. Comet searched and parsed the results page, built the comparison table with prices, and added two options to the cart.

## How it went

The task completed successfully, and Comet stopped at the cart page without trying to buy, which the testers called correct. The article gives no timing for Comet on this task.

## Worth knowing

The article says multi-step checkout, CAPTCHAs and security verification routinely break browser agents, so expect to finish the purchase yourself.

## Prompt

```text
Search Amazon for [product type] with [must-have features] under $[budget]. Build a comparison table of the top options with price, ratings and key differences, then add the best [number] to my cart. Stop before checkout and do not buy anything.
```
