# Publish ready social posts using Claude Cowork

> A commenter pointed Claude Cowork at a folder or Canva containing finished posts to handle social posting.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Claude Cowork  
**Source:** Reddit · Oct 1, 2026

## What they did

The commenter used the Planoly Claude Connector from Claude’s directory and started by pointing Claude Cowork at a local folder or Canva containing posts that were already ready to go. Claude could read brand docs, suggest platform-specific captions, schedule the posts through Planoly, and for recurring weekly posts use a Claude skill to turn 3–4 raw photos into the commenter’s preferred carousel format before checking the caption with them.

## How it went

It helped them get consistent again on platforms that had gone stale, especially LinkedIn, and the repeatable weekly-post setup became a bigger time saver. They still kept a human check on the caption before publishing.

## Worth knowing

Their more advanced setup depended on a custom Claude skill for a repeatable post format, not just the connector alone.

## Prompt

```text
Open the folder [folder path] (or my Canva project [name]) containing my finished social posts. For each post, match it to the right platform from [platforms] and prepare it for publishing with the caption in the file or [caption rules]. Pause and show me each post and its destination before you publish, and when finished list what went out and what was skipped.
```
