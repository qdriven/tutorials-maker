# Plan a birthday evening with dinner and a comedy show

> Muse agent planned a wife's birthday evening, booking dinner and buying two tickets with permission after refining location details.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** X · Sep 30, 2026

## What they did

User instructed Muse to plan a wife's birthday evening of dinner plus comedy show. Muse first proposed dinner in Palo Alto then a show back in SF; after user added location details it booked the dinner reservation and purchased two tickets, requesting permission first. It sent progress buzzes while the user handled patient rounds.

## How it went

Dinner and two tickets were booked successfully. It also located cheapest flights for a separate trip.

## Worth knowing

Required explicit permission before completing any bookings or purchases.

## Prompt

```text
Plan a birthday evening for my [partner's name] on [date] in [city]: dinner at a [cuisine] restaurant near [area] followed by a [comedy show or other event]. Ask me about any location details you need, then find options that fit [budget] and a sensible timeline between dinner and the show. Before you book the table or buy two tickets, show me exactly what you're about to purchase and wait for my approval.
```
