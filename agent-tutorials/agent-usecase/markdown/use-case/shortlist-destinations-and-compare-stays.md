# Shortlist destinations and compare stays

> Comet shortlisted sunny destinations by flight time from Amsterdam, then pulled Airbnb and Booking.com listings into a comparison table.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Perplexity Comet  
**Source:** rentalscaleup.com · Undated

## What they did

In a live session, Thibault Masson of RSU by PriceLabs opened Perplexity's Comet browser and gave it one prompt. The prompt asked for five apartments for four nights in April, under €250 a night, sunny, good for remote work, and within a 3-hour flight of Amsterdam. He named no destination. He took his hands off the keyboard while the agent worked out flight times, picked cities, opened Airbnb and Booking.com, and extracted listing details.

## How it went

Comet returned a table of five apartments with Wi-Fi speeds, flight durations and nightly prices, and named Lisbon, Malaga and Valencia as candidate cities. The article gives no accuracy check and no booking step.

## Worth knowing

With a broad prompt like this, the agent defaulted to Airbnb and Booking.com. Masson says more specific asks, such as design-led stays, pulled from niche sites instead.

## Prompt

```text
Shortlist [number] sunny destinations in [month] within [number] hours flight of [home city]. For my top [2-3] picks, pull listings from Airbnb and Booking.com for [dates] and [number of guests] under [budget], and put them in a comparison table with price, rating, and location. Do not book anything; stop once the table is ready.
```
