# Coordinate a cancer patient's records and care with an agent

> A family gave a dying relative's medical records to an agent as coordinator; it flagged a wrong pain medication schedule and a transcription error in his genetic markers, which changed his treatment plan.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** HN · Oct 15, 2026

## What they did

After the father-in-law was passed between many doctors and NPs and ended up on so many conflicting medications that he passed out, the commenter gave all his records to an agent and made it coordinator. Doctors and tests became inputs to it. It reviewed everything together and presented evidence for actions to his doctors.

## How it went

It found a wrong pain medication and schedule, and a voice-transcription error in his genetic markers that changed his treatment plan. He felt better, but he had already lost too much weight and died of pancreatic cancer.

## Worth knowing

The commenter says they wish they had been far more aggressive sooner; the earlier the agent coordinates, the more it can help, since his reserves were already drained.

## Prompt

```text
I'm helping coordinate care for [relative's name], who has [condition]. I'll paste in their medication schedule, lab results, and notes from [doctors/clinics]. Build a single timeline, then flag anything that looks inconsistent: dosing or schedule conflicts, values that differ between documents, or likely transcription errors. Finish with a list of specific questions to bring to the care team, and remind me you're not a substitute for their clinicians.
```
