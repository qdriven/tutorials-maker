# Claim unclaimed property using ChatGPT guidance

> A user photographed an unclaimed property letter and ChatGPT pointed them to the Florida treasury site to claim it; another says a fiancé claimed and received the money.

**Category:** Money, Bills & Benefits  
**Agent:** ChatGPT  
**Source:** Reddit · May 8, 2026

## What they did

In a comment on the removed Reddit thread, someone said they got a mailed notice from a company offering to file an unclaimed-property claim for a 10% fee. They photographed the letter, sent it to ChatGPT, and ChatGPT told them to check Florida’s treasury site and file the claim themselves; they then submitted the claim without using the company.

## How it went

They said the claim worked: the fiancé received the money by deposit, it took only a few weeks, and they did not have to jump through any hoops. The thread gives no amount for that specific claim.

## Worth knowing

The key catch was avoiding the third-party filer’s 10% fee by going directly through Florida’s official unclaimed-property process.

## Prompt

```text
I have a letter about possible unclaimed property in [state]. Here is the text or photo: [paste/attach]. Find the official state treasury or unclaimed property site, check the claim steps and documents I need, and walk me through them. Stop before submitting any claim or entering my SSN and show me what you plan to enter.
```
