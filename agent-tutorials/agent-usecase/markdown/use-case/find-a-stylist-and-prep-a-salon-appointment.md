# Find a stylist and prep a salon appointment

> A CNET reporter had ChatGPT's agent research stylists and request booking details, but stopped it before it booked.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** ChatGPT Agent  
**Source:** tech.yahoo.com · Undated

## What they did

The reporter used a paid ChatGPT account, switched on Agent mode, and asked for a balayage hairdresser in Montclair NJ or NYC with a price cap. She watched the agent browse live and added a correction mid-run. She then had it sort options by price and rank stylists at one salon. It offered to book, but she held back.

## How it went

The search took 9 minutes and returned prices with notes and source screenshots. She was impressed, but she wouldn't hand over sensitive details and would take over at booking.

## Worth knowing

The agent pauses for payment details and wants your confirmation before submitting. The reporter still wouldn't give it personal details, and it's in beta.

## Prompt

```text
Research hair stylists near [neighborhood or city] who specialize in [service, e.g. balayage, curly cuts] and have strong reviews and prices under [budget]. Shortlist three with their rates, availability and booking links, and find out what details each needs to book. Do not book anything or submit any forms; stop with the shortlist and a draft of what you would enter.
```
