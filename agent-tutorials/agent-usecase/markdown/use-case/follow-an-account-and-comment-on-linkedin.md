# Follow an account and comment on LinkedIn

> Comet followed the Perplexity CEO on LinkedIn, found a specific post and commented on it in one attempt.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Perplexity Comet  
**Source:** X · Nov 7, 2025

## What they did

The agent received access to the user's LinkedIn account. It followed the Perplexity CEO, located a specific post, and added a comment underneath, completing the sequence autonomously in one attempt while displaying its reasoning before each action.

## How it went

It succeeded on the first try after the upgrade that improved reliability and raised internal test scores by 23%.

## Prompt

```text
On LinkedIn, find [person's name] and follow them. Then find their recent post about [topic] and draft a thoughtful comment of one or two sentences about [your angle]. Show me the draft comment and the post before you follow or post anything, and finish once I've approved and it's live.
```
