# Clean inbox, chase refund, and book HVAC tune-up

> Meta Muse deleted 20k+ old Hotmail emails, chased a missing refund via ISP support chat, and scheduled an HVAC tune-up from maintenance history.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** X · Sep 26, 2026

## What they did

The Muse agent had access to the user's Hotmail inbox and prior maintenance records. It reviewed 80K emails, flagged 20k+ old promos and newsletters for deletion with explicit user approval, and logged every action. It then opened an ISP support chat, retrieved refund details from the emails, and booked an HVAC tune-up from the history.

## How it went

Inbox dropped from 96% to 60% full. The refund date was confirmed and the tune-up was scheduled with no further user input.

## Prompt

```text
Help me clean up my [email provider] inbox by finding old promotional and newsletter emails older than [date], and show me the count and a sample before deleting anything. Then contact [company] support chat about my missing refund of [amount, order number]. Finally, check my home maintenance history and propose times to book an HVAC tune-up with [provider], pausing for my OK before the booking.
```
