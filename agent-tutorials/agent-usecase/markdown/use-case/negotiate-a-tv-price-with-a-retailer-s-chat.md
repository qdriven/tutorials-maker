# Negotiate a TV price with a retailer's chat

> ChatGPT Agent negotiated in Dutch with a retailer's chat, handled upsells, and landed an LG OLED TV at €939 including shipping.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** ChatGPT Agent  
**Source:** Reddit · Aug 7, 2025

## What they did

The poster told ChatGPT Agent Mode to go to the retailer’s site, open live chat, and negotiate the LG OLED55C46LA down to €935 or less including shipping. According to the post, the agent handled the conversation in Dutch, waited through delays, dealt with multiple reps, cited an earlier offer and competitor pricing, declined upsells, and got an order link emailed to the user.

## How it went

The reported result was a price of €939 including shipping, down from the stated usual price of €1,129. In comments, other Redditors said comparable or lower prices were already available elsewhere, so the claimed savings may not have been the best market deal.

## Worth knowing

The catch is that it missed the user’s stated ceiling by €4, even though it still closed the purchase link.

## Prompt

```text
Find the best price for [product and model] from retailers in [country], then use the retailer's chat to negotiate a lower price including shipping, aiming for [target price]. Decline upsells like extended warranties unless I approve them. Pause and show me the final price and order details before paying or confirming anything.
```
