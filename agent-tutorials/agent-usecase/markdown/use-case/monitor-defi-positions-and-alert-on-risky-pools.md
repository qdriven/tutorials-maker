# Monitor DeFi positions and alert on risky pools

> AI agents watched a user's DeFi and traditional accounts across 37 chains and flagged a risky liquidity pool, preventing potential losses.

**Category:** Money, Bills & Benefits  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** X · Dec 27, 2025

## What they did

Agents received access to the user's full financial data across 37 chains and traditional accounts. They monitored spending and positions 24/7, with one agent dedicated to tracking DeFi risks and flagging issues proactively. The user received an alert on a risky liquidity pool but took no further described steps.

## How it went

The alert identified a forgotten risky liquidity pool and prevented potential losses.

## Worth knowing

## Prompt

```text
Check my DeFi positions and accounts at [wallet addresses or platforms] on [chains] every [interval], and compare each pool or protocol against risk signals like sudden TVL drops, low liquidity, unaudited contracts, or large price divergence. Send me an alert at [my email or chat app] with the pool name, the risk found, and the amount I have exposed. Never move funds or sign transactions; only alert me.
```
