# Find unpaid bills and suggest recipes from inbox

> A WSJ reviewer connected her inbox and calendar to Muse, which found unpaid bills and suggested a recipe based on a produce delivery.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** thecurrency.news · Undated

## What they did

Nicole Nguyen of the WSJ tried Meta's Muse, an agent built to work like an executive assistant. She connected her inbox and calendar. From that access, Muse found unpaid bills and suggested a recipe based on an incoming produce delivery. The visible text is only the opening of a paywalled piece.

## How it went

Muse turned up unpaid bills and a recipe idea tied to the produce delivery. The visible excerpt gives no figures, and the headline calls the tool both helpful and scary.

## Worth knowing

The more data you give Muse, the more useful it gets, which is the privacy tradeoff the reviewer flags. The full article is paywalled.

## Prompt

```text
Go through my email and calendar from the last [number] weeks and list every unpaid bill or payment due, with the sender, amount, due date and a link if there is one. Also check my emails for [grocery or produce delivery] and suggest [number] recipes using what is arriving. Give me the results in a single summary and do not pay, reply to or delete anything.
```
