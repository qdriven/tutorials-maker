# List a Quest 3 and negotiate with buyers

> Meta Muse listed a Meta Quest 3 on Facebook Marketplace and was instructed to negotiate with buyers using a floor price and push strategy.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** X · Sep 21, 2026

## What they did

Muse had native access to the user's Facebook account and Messenger. It listed the Meta Quest 3 on Marketplace. The user then supplied the floor price, negotiation push strategy, and permission to handle buyer messages on its own.

No results or numbers are stated.

```text

Show me any offer you want to accept before confirming, and do not share my address or phone number.
