# Scrape AI news three times a day with a custom bot

> A paying user built three bots in week one, including a news scraper collecting AI news three times daily; one other bot flopped.

**Category:** Research & Knowledge  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** christopheralarcon.com · Undated

## What they did

Chris Alarcon paid for Grok Bot, xAI's beta product where each bot gets its own cloud computer. He set up a news bot that collects the latest AI news three times a day. It runs on its own, and he says it also tidied some of his lists. The source gives no setup detail beyond that.

## How it went

He calls the news scraper simple and boring, which is what he wants from an assistant. His lead-scout bot, built the same week, produced nothing. He hit his usage limit once on the $100 plan.

## Worth knowing

Bot access is listed from the $30/month SuperGrok tier, but docs and pricing have disagreed during beta, so check xAI's pricing page before you buy.

## Prompt

```text
Set up a recurring job that runs [three times a day, at 8am, 1pm, 6pm] to collect the latest news on [topic] from [sources or sites]. Summarize each item in one or two sentences with a link, remove duplicates from earlier runs, and send the digest to [email or chat]. Run it once now so I can check the format, then ask me what to adjust before scheduling it.
```
