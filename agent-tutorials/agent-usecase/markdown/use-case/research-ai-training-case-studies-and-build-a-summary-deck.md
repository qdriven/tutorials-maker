# Research AI training case studies and build a summary deck

> A team had Agent Mode research case studies from McKinsey, PwC and similar firms and make a slide deck in 32 minutes, with some image and overlapping text issues.

**Category:** Research & Knowledge  
**Agent:** ChatGPT Agent  
**Source:** aioperator.com · Undated

## What they did

The team gave ChatGPT Agent Mode one prompt: research AI training case studies from McKinsey, PwC and similar firms, then build a summary deck. In its sandboxed browser it read each site, retried or searched around URLs that failed to load, and outlined the findings. It then generated images and tables and assembled the slides. The team downloaded the file and edited it.

## How it went

The agent finished in 32 minutes, and the team's edits brought the total to under 45 minutes. The deck's structure and content were solid, but some images had formatting problems and a few text boxes overlapped.

## Worth knowing

Plan 10 to 15 minutes of manual cleanup for layout and branding. Agent Mode is on Plus (about 30 to 40 uses a month) and Pro plans.

## Prompt

```text
Research case studies on [topic, e.g. AI training programs] from major consulting firms and reputable sources like [firm names]. Build a slide deck summarizing [number] case studies with results, lessons and source links. Finish when the deck is ready, and check that images and text don't overlap before you hand it over.
```
