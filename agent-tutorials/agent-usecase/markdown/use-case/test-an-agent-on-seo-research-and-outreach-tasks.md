# Test an agent on SEO research and outreach tasks

> SE Ranking tested Operator on pulling SERP results, backlink checks and outreach email management; it was slow and error-prone, so they did not recommend it for complex SEO work.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Operator  
**Source:** seranking.com · Undated

## What they did

SE Ranking gave ChatGPT Operator prompts in four areas: competitor analysis, SERP monitoring, keyword research, and link building. It had to pull top-10 Google and Bing results into a Google Sheet, check a sheet of referring pages for backlinks to seranking.com, and draft replies to unread link-building emails in Gmail. For the SE Ranking tool tasks, the team supplied their own login credentials.

## How it went

Simple SERP pulls and outreach drafting worked best. Competitor research was abandoned partway, keyword research covered one keyword at a time, and the backlink check wrongly marked some pages as having links.

## Worth knowing

It needed constant babysitting, couldn't get into Reddit, and ran on a $200/month subscription, so check every output by hand.

## Prompt

```text
For the keyword [keyword], open Google and record the top [10] results with URL and title in a table. Then check backlinks for [my domain] using [free backlink tool] and list the top [10] referring domains. Stop when both lists are complete, and tell me anything you could not verify. Do not send any outreach emails; if you draft any, show them to me first.
```
