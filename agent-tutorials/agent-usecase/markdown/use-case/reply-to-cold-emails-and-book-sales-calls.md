# Reply to cold emails and book sales calls

> Grok Bot answered cold email replies, qualified leads, checked calendar slots, booked 20 sales calls in 8 days, and updated the CRM within guardrails.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** X · Oct 6, 2026

## What they did

Specialized Grok Bot triggered by webhook on positive cold-email replies reads a charter listing the offer, $600K qualification, calendar rules, and when to ask vs act alone. It proposes one time slot with the booking link, checks availability, books the call with notes, confirms from the same inbox, and updates the CRM in one run. Human approval needed only for odd cases; reschedules and follow-ups run alone.

## How it went

Booked 20 sales calls in 8 days for the agency at 60% conversion of interested replies. Speed fell short, with only 16% of first replies under 3 minutes.

## Prompt

```text
Go through the replies to my cold emails in [inbox or tool], qualify each lead against [my criteria], and check my calendar for open slots. Draft replies proposing times and update [CRM] with each lead's status. Follow these guardrails: [list what not to promise or discuss], and show me each reply before sending until I say you can go ahead.
```
