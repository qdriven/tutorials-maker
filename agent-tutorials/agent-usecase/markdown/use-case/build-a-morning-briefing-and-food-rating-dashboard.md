# Build a morning briefing and food rating dashboard

> A commenter says Muse crawls credit card, email, and calendars to track restaurants visited and rate food, and gives a daily briefing with calendar, weather, news, and follow-ups.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** Reddit · Oct 10, 2026

## What they did

The commenter connected Muse to their credit card, email, and calendars so it could infer restaurant visits and assemble them in a dashboard. Muse automatically pulled in details like location and cuisine type, and the couple reviewed it once a week to add their own ratings and notes about the food.

## How it went

It gave them a centralized way to track where they had eaten and keep ratings. The post does not give any numbers, accuracy rates, or specific problems beyond the fact that ratings and notes were still added manually in weekly reviews.

## Worth knowing

The main catch is that this was not fully automatic: they still went in weekly to update ratings and food notes.

## Prompt

```text
Each morning at [time], give me a briefing from my calendar [calendar], the weather in [city], news on [topics], and emails that still need a follow-up. Also keep a log of restaurants I visited, based on [receipts or card statement export], with a field for my rating. Don't change or send anything without asking, and finish by showing me today's briefing and the first version of the log.
```
