# Summarize meetings and triage support tickets with Lindy

> Lindy agents summarized meeting notes, sent action items to teammates, suggested knowledge-base replies on support tickets, and handled follow-up emails and calendar updates.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** Lindy  
**Source:** cybernews.com · Undated

## What they did

A Cybernews reviewer built Lindy agents in its no-code drag-and-drop editor, connecting Gmail, Slack and CRM tools and setting triggers, conditions and actions. One agent summarized meeting notes, pulled out action items and sent them to teammates. Another reviewed support tickets and suggested knowledge-base replies before escalation. Others handled email follow-ups and calendar updates.

## How it went

The reviewer found the agents did well on routine, repetitive workflows and set up easily. The review gives no time-saved figures. Complex multi-step automations, custom integrations and debugging were weaker.

## Worth knowing

The free tier (400 credits) is too limited to test properly; the reviewer recommends a paid plan, which starts at $49.99/month for 5000 credits.

## Prompt

```text
Create a Lindy agent that joins my [Zoom/Meet/Teams] meetings, writes a summary, and emails each person their own action items. Add a second agent that reads new tickets in [help desk tool], suggests a reply drawn from our [knowledge base] and saves it as a draft without sending. Show me the first few summaries and drafts for approval before anything goes to teammates or customers.
```
