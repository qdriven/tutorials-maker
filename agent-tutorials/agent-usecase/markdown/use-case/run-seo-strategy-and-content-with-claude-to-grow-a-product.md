# Run SEO strategy and content with Claude to grow a product

> A founder used Claude for SEO and content strategy, including keyword-gap analysis from Search Console data, and reports growing to 10,000 active users in six weeks without ad spend.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Claude  
**Source:** Reddit · Undated

## What they did

The founder says they exported Google Search Console, Ahrefs, and Google Analytics data into Claude each week so it could spot keyword gaps, zero-click queries, cannibalization, indexing misses, and weak titles. Claude then helped shape articles around those gaps, with question-style H2s, quick-answer intros, tables, internal links, FAQ/schema markup, and technical fixes the founder implemented through Lovable prompts and checks like curl and GSC lists.

## How it went

They report publishing 96 articles and growing from 5 clicks a week to 1,000+ weekly clicks, 300K monthly impressions, 878+ page-one rankings, 348 AI-referred sessions a month, and 10,000 active users in six weeks with no ads. In comments, many readers challenged the traffic claims using third-party SEO tools, and the founder defended them with first-party GSC and GA4 numbers.

## Worth knowing

Their key input was first-party data exports; they specifically say Claude became useful when fed GSC, analytics, and competitor data rather than asked for generic posts.

## Prompt

```text
I run [product name], which helps [target customer] with [main problem]. I'm attaching an export of my Search Console data for the last [number] months. Find keyword gaps where I get impressions but few clicks or rank on page two, then give me a prioritized list of 10 content ideas with a target keyword, search intent and suggested title for each. Stop once you have the ranked list and a short rationale for the top three.
```
