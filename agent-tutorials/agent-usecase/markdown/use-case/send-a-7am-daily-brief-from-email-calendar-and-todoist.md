# Send a 7am daily brief from email, calendar and Todoist

> A personal agent runs at 7am, checks email, calendar and Todoist, pulls likely follow-ups and delivers one daily brief instead of the full inbox.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** Reddit · Jun 5, 2026

## What they did

The poster built a personal agent that runs at 7am, reads their email, calendar, and Todoist, then sends one email with a daily summary, schedule, and likely follow-ups instead of making them scan everything themselves. In comments, they said each person gets their own assistant agent, and when they mark something as junk or correct a mislabeled email, that feedback is used to help train the agent for future runs.

## How it went

They say it saves “way way too much time,” but give no hard metrics in the thread. It still mis-surfaces or mislabels items sometimes, and they fix those cases as part of improving it.

## Worth knowing

The clearest catch is that it needs ongoing correction: when it surfaces junk or labels something wrong, they manually fix it so the agent learns.

## Prompt

```text
Every day at [7am, my time zone], check my email, my calendar, and my [Todoist or task app]. Send me one short brief with today's meetings, tasks due, and emails that likely need a follow-up from me, instead of the full inbox. Don't reply to, archive, or delete anything; just deliver the brief to [where I want it, e.g. email or chat].
```
