# Use AI as an internal recruiting assistant

> Recruiting teams use AI to summarize profiles, prepare submissions, draft client updates and structure candidate information before meetings.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** Reddit · Undated

## What they did

Recruiters in the thread describe using AI as back-office help rather than an autonomous sourcer: summarizing resumes and candidate profiles, matching them to job descriptions, drafting client or internal update emails, writing interview summaries, preparing interview guides, organizing candidate information before debriefs, and helping with scheduling or notes. People still do the judgment-heavy work themselves, especially conversations, submissions with account context, and any direct communication with candidates.

## How it went

The reported benefit is mainly time saved on repetitive prep and admin work, freeing recruiters for calls and relationship work. The main shortfalls mentioned are hallucinations in sourcing or screening and lack of institutional or hiring-manager context.

## Worth knowing

One recruiter explicitly said they do not want AI contacting candidates, treating that part as human work even when other admin tasks are automated.

## Prompt

```text
I'm a recruiter working on a [role] search for [client type]. Here are the candidate's resume and my notes: [paste]. Summarize the candidate in a structured profile (experience, key skills, achievements, gaps, compensation and availability if known), draft a concise client submission, and write a short client update email on the search's progress. Finish with 5 questions I should ask in the next meeting; don't send anything, just give me drafts to review.
```
