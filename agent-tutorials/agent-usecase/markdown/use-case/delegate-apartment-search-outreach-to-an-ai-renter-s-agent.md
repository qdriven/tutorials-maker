# Delegate apartment search outreach to an AI renter's agent

> Kaleno's AI, with the renter's permission, contacts properties, chases missing details and manages follow-up; the vendor says half of beta contacts were handled this way.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** prnewswire.com · Oct 21, 2026

## What they did

A renter describes what they want in their own words. Once they opt in to AI Contacting, Kaleno emails properties, asks about missing details, requests tours and handles follow-up. The renter can review the exchanges, take over or switch it off at any time. Kaleno draws on structured data for more than 7 million U.S. rental units.

## How it went

In the June–Oct 2026 beta, about 150,000 renters tried it. AI Contacting sent half of emails to properties and averaged eight messages per conversation. These are vendor figures, and independent research is still to come.

## Worth knowing

The 50% figure counts only emails to properties, not phone calls or direct tour bookings. It is Kaleno's own beta data, and the product is free in beta.

## Prompt

```text
I'm looking for an apartment in [city or neighborhood] with a budget of [rent range], [beds] bedrooms, move-in by [date] and these must-haves: [pets, parking, laundry, etc.]. Go through the listings on [site] and, for each good match, find any missing details such as fees, lease length, availability and pet policy. Pause and show me each inquiry message before sending it, and stop once you have a comparison table of the [number] best options with follow-ups drafted.
```
