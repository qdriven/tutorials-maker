# Book dentist, physical, and haircut appointments

> The poster asked Muse to schedule three appointments; it found the booking page but for the barber drafted a long email instead of finishing the booking, so only partly worked.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** Reddit · Oct 19, 2026

## What they did

The poster tried using Muse to schedule a dentist appointment, an annual physical, and a haircut. On the dentist task, Muse missed the website’s new-patient request form, asked roughly 20 follow-up questions, and tried to send an email; for the physical, it recognized there was a patient portal but did not navigate it; for the haircut, it found the booking page but drafted a long email to the barber instead of completing the booking.

## How it went

It did not complete any of the three appointments. The poster says they manually booked the dentist in about 30 seconds, the physical in under a minute, and rebooked the haircut themselves from the booking page.

## Worth knowing

Comments suggest better results may require granting calendar, email, or messaging access and explicitly telling Muse to act autonomously, but the post itself shows a lot of question-asking and little follow-through.

## Prompt

```text
Book these appointments for me: [dentist, physical, haircut] in [city], within [date range], preferably [days and times]. Use each provider's online booking page, and if there isn't one, tell me and give me the phone number instead of drafting long emails. Before confirming any booking or entering insurance or payment details, pause and show me the slot, and finish with a list of what's booked and what still needs a call.
```
