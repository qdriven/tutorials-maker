# Find cheaper car insurance

> A startup founder said Muse found car insurance costing $3,500 less per year than his original plan.

**Category:** Money, Bills & Benefits  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** aol.com · Undated

## What they did

Startup founder Joseph Devoy used Meta's Muse agent, launched September 8, for car insurance. Meta's pitch is to prompt Muse to review your finances, then have it carry out savings tasks in the background, such as haggling with insurers by phone. The article shows only a screenshot of Devoy's chat. It doesn't describe his exact prompt or steps.

## How it went

Devoy said the insurance Muse found cost $3,500 less per year than his original plan, and he called it a killer product. The figure is his own claim, and many other posted wins came from Meta staff.

## Worth knowing

The result is a self-reported social media post, not independently checked. Amazon has also blocked Muse from placing orders on its site, so purchasing tasks may hit limits.

## Prompt

```text
Help me shop for car insurance for [vehicle, year and model] in [ZIP code]. My current plan is [coverage levels and annual price]. Get quotes from at least three insurers that match the same coverage and show them in a comparison table with the annual savings. Don't submit personal details beyond what's needed for a quote, and pause for my approval before buying or switching anything.
```
