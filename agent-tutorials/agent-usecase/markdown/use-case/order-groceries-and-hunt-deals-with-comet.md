# Order groceries and hunt deals with Comet

> A user gave Comet a grocery list and signed in to their grocery account in another tab, and it searched for deals; another has it email daily job leads.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** Perplexity Comet  
**Source:** Reddit · Oct 1, 2025

## What they did

One user used Comet for grocery ordering by giving it a shopping list, then signing into their grocery account in another tab and telling it to look for deals. Comet searched the hard-to-navigate store site and surfaced item options; the user handled the account login and saw it as a step toward eventually placing a weekly order from one prompt. Another user said they set a daily task for Comet to email them job leads.

## How it went

The grocery run was described as a decent job and faster than doing it manually because the site was difficult to navigate. The only concrete result on the job-leads setup was that it worked smoothly; no numbers or misses were given.

## Worth knowing

The grocery example was done on a regular Pro subscription, and the user had to be signed into the grocery account in another tab.

## Prompt

```text
I'm signed in to [grocery store] in another tab. Take this list: [items with quantities] and search for the best deals or sales on each, preferring [brands or dietary limits]. Add the best options to my cart and stop when the cart is complete. Pause and show me the full cart with the total before checkout, and do not pay until I confirm.
```
