# Complete an online survey end to end

> The poster says Muse navigated a survey, solved a captcha and submitted a response with no human intervention.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** Reddit · Oct 2, 2026

## What they did

The poster says they gave Meta’s Muse a link to one of their surveys and told it to complete the whole thing without human help. According to the post, Muse handled the survey from start to finish on its own, including navigating pages, answering questions, solving the captcha, and submitting the response; the poster says they never clicked, answered, or took over.

## How it went

The reported result was a fully submitted survey response with no human intervention. In comments, the poster says Muse took only a little longer than humans overall, and its open-ended answers could be hard to distinguish, though they were longer than typical human responses.

## Worth knowing

The practical catch the poster highlights is that Muse was free at the time, with a usage limit, which they argue lowers the cost of survey fraud.

## Prompt

```text
Open this survey at [survey URL] and fill it out using these answers and preferences: [my answers or persona details]. If you hit a captcha or login, pause and let me handle it. Show me my completed answers before you press the final Submit button, and stop once I confirm it has been submitted.
```
