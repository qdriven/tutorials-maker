# Qualify leads and handle onboarding with Make and AI

> A commenter built a calendar voice agent and uses Make with AI for lead qualification, customer onboarding, and basic customer service responses.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Make  
**Source:** Reddit · Jan 1, 2025

## What they did

The commenter said they pair Make.com with AI so the system can qualify leads, run customer onboarding, and answer basic customer-service questions without constant manual handling. They did not describe a full build, tools beyond Make and AI, or where a human reviews or intervenes; in the same thread they advised starting with one repetitive task before adding multi-step workflows.

## How it went

They called this the biggest game-changer for building systems that can make decisions and take actions on their own. But they gave no metrics, examples, or results for these specific workflows.

## Worth knowing

Their main practical warning was the learning curve: start with a single task that saves 30+ minutes a day before attempting complex chained automations.
