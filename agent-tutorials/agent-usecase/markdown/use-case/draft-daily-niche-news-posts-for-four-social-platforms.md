# Draft daily niche-news posts for four social platforms

> An agent scans niche news, picks a story, writes posts for LinkedIn, X, Instagram and Facebook, makes images, and emails drafts each morning.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** X · Mar 26, 2026

## What they did

Built from one prompt in 15 minutes. The agent scans daily niche news, selects the most relevant story, writes tailored posts for LinkedIn, X, Instagram and Facebook, generates platform-cropped images, then emails the four drafts at 7am each morning. It runs on autopilot after that single setup.

## How it went

No performance numbers or results are stated. The build was completed in 15 minutes and can be turned into a one-click web app for others to use.

## Worth knowing

Built in 15 minutes with one prompt.

## Prompt

```text
Every morning at [time], scan news in [niche] and pick the most relevant story for [audience]. Write a post for each of LinkedIn, X, Instagram and Facebook in my voice [describe voice], suggest an image for each, and email all the drafts to [my email]. Do not publish anything; the job is done once the morning email is sent.
```
