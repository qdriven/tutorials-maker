# Phone a restaurant in Thai to book a table

> Grok Bot phoned a Michelin restaurant in Thailand, spoke Thai, navigated the phone menu, and asked them to hold the seat while a deposit was arranged. · Oct 11, 2026

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** x.com · Oct 11, 2026 · Submitted by @thebowlboy

## What they did

Grok bot was integrated with JooVoice.com to make outbound calls. It spoke Thai to book a restaurant table for the user. The integration required no browser step and was described as the most seamless among bots tested.

## How it went

The agent completed the booking successfully.

## Worth knowing

Recording details redacted; free welcome call available via JooVoice.com.

## Prompt

```text
Please phone [restaurant name] in [city, country] and book a table for [number] people on [date] at [time]. Speak [local language] and work through any phone menu. If they want a deposit, ask them to hold the table until [deadline] and get the payment method and amount. Pause and tell me what you plan to say before you agree to any payment, then send me a summary of the call when you are done.
```
