# Build and order a grocery cart from an idea

> Through the Instacart connector, Muse turns an idea like Taco Tuesday into a cart at the preferred store, checks out and arranges delivery.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** X · Undated

## What they did

Muse received an Instacart connector and user access to a preferred store. The user gave a phrase such as "Taco Tuesday"; Muse then selected matching items, populated the cart, completed checkout, and scheduled delivery through the same Instacart flow.

## How it went

No performance numbers or completion rates are stated. The feature is presented as a direct re-skin of the existing Instacart experience.

## Worth knowing

Available only in the U.S. as of the September 2026 launch.

## Prompt

```text
Build an Instacart cart for [meal idea, e.g. Taco Tuesday] for [number] people from [preferred store], avoiding [dietary restrictions] and staying under [budget]. Show me the full cart with quantities and total cost, and wait for my approval before checking out. Then use delivery time [window] to schedule it.
```
