# Schedule prospect meetings and log them in the CRM

> A Lindy agent replied to prospect emails with time options, booked the chosen slot, logged it in HubSpot and sent a pre-call brief.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** Lindy  
**Source:** lindy.ai · Undated

## What they did

The author connected Lindy to Gmail, Google Calendar and HubSpot. When a prospect emailed asking to connect, Lindy replied with three time options before the author saw the email. After the prospect chose 2 p.m., it booked the slot, added it to the calendar and logged her in HubSpot. Ten minutes before the call it sent a brief built from her last three emails.

## How it went

The author went into the call already knowing the context. The source gives no timing or volume figures for this workflow; it describes a single prospect example.

## Worth knowing

Lindy's Plus plan starts at $49.99/month with usage limits, and Lindy pauses to check in once you hit them. The post says you can review and approve before anything goes out.

## Prompt

```text
When a prospect emails [inbox or address] asking to meet, reply with three time options from my [calendar] in [time zone]. When they choose one, book it, log the meeting in [HubSpot or CRM] and send me a short pre-call brief on the person and company. Show me the first reply draft before sending, and stop after the brief is delivered.
```
