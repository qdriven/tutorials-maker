# Log new English words into a Notion vocabulary database

> The author sends new English words to OpenClaw, which adds them to a Notion vocabulary database and fills in phonetics, part of speech, definition and mnemonic. · Oct 31, 2026

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** OpenClaw  
**Source:** sspai.com · Oct 31, 2026 · Submitted by from search gap: 英语

## What they did

The author uses OpenClaw's built-in Notion Skill. A free Notion plan works if you link it through a Connection. When the author meets an unfamiliar word, they send it to OpenClaw. It adds the word to the Notion vocabulary database and fills in phonetics, part of speech, definition, root-based mnemonic and mnemonic method. A daily cron task also picks five words to review.

## How it went

The article presents this as a working part of the author's routine, with no figures or accuracy checks. The author says it gives them a steady supply of words to study.

## Worth knowing

Free Notion is enough, because OpenClaw reaches it through a Connection. The author cancelled their paid Notion plan after switching to this setup.

## Prompt

```text
Whenever I message you an English word, add it to my Notion database [database name] with these fields filled in: phonetics, part of speech, a clear definition in [my language or English], and a short memorable mnemonic. Skip it and tell me if the word is already in the database. Reply with a one-line confirmation of what you saved.
```
