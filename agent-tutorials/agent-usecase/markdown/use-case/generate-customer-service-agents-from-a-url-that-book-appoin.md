# Generate customer-service agents from a URL that book appointments

> A platform builds website and WhatsApp support agents that answer questions and book Google Calendar appointments, plus an autopilot that scrapes Google Maps and preps outreach with demo bots.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** Reddit · Jul 1, 2026

## What they did

The founder says the platform takes a business URL, scrapes the site, and auto-configures a customer-service agent in about 60 seconds. That agent answers questions around the clock, can book appointments directly into Google Calendar, and runs both as a website widget and on WhatsApp; in comments, the founder also says an “autopilot” can pull businesses from Google Maps, draft outreach, and spin up a personalized demo bot for each lead, while people can submit their URL for a free build.

## How it went

The stated result is a working support agent generated from a pasted URL, with multilingual use implied by the founder saying it was built Spanish-first but works fine in English too. The post shares no usage, revenue, or accuracy metrics, so the outcome is described more as a live capability than a measured business result.

## Worth knowing

The clearest catch is that this was built solo in about two months and the post offers free custom demos by having users drop in their business URL.

## Prompt

```text
Build me a customer-support assistant for my business at [website URL]. It should answer common questions using the content on my site and book appointments into my Google Calendar [calendar name] during [available hours]. Test it with three sample questions and one sample booking and show me the results. Before connecting it to my live calendar or any customer-facing channel, pause and ask for my approval.
```
