# Research and benchmark competitors into a deck and spreadsheet

> ChatGPT Agent mode researched the top 5 UK coffee subscription companies, benchmarked their USPs, and produced a deck and spreadsheet from one prompt in 18 minutes.

**Category:** Research & Knowledge  
**Agent:** ChatGPT Agent  
**Source:** marketsmarterwithai.substack.com · Undated

## What they did

With Agent mode on in ChatGPT Pro, the author pasted one prompt. It asked for a competitor deck on the top 5 UK coffee subscription companies, exported as a PDF, plus a table of each company's top 5 USPs as a spreadsheet. The agent found the competitors, pulled their positioning, pricing and USPs, and built the files. They were downloaded from the chat.

## How it went

The run took 18 minutes and returned a PDF deck and a spreadsheet. The author calls them good initial information, not finished work, and says follow-up prompts can build on them.

## Worth knowing

You can leave the window while it runs and ChatGPT will notify you. Agent mode was still rolling out to Pro users, and the deck may need a tool like Gamma to look presentable.

## Prompt

```text
Research the top [number] [industry/market] companies in [country] and benchmark them on pricing, unique selling points, positioning, and customer reviews. Produce a short slide deck summarizing the findings and a spreadsheet with the comparison data and source links. Finish when both files are ready to download, and flag anything you couldn't verify.
```
