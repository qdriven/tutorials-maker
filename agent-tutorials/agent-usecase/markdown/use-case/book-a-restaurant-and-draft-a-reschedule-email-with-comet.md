# Book a restaurant and draft a reschedule email with Comet

> A reviewer had Comet find and reserve a Friday 7pm table and fill in a Gmail reschedule email, leaving the send to the user.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Perplexity Comet  
**Source:** tomsguide.com · Undated

## What they did

The reviewer gave Comet two separate prompts. For the booking, it went off on its own to check the highest-rated restaurants and their availability. It then asked whether to proceed before completing the reservation. For the email, Comet asked for the time, recipient and reason, then opened Gmail, asked the reviewer to enter their email details, and filled in the draft.

## How it went

The reservation went through after Comet asked for confirmation, and the Gmail draft was filled in so the reviewer only had to hit send. A plain draft request, without Gmail access, produced a generic template.

## Worth knowing

Comet needed answers to its follow-up questions and a Gmail sign-in, so the email draft wasn't fully hands-off.

## Prompt

```text
Find a [cuisine] restaurant in [city] with a table for [number] on [day] at [time], and go through the booking up to the final confirm step. Then open my Gmail and fill in a draft email to [person] about rescheduling to [new time]. Pause before confirming the reservation and before sending the email, and show me what each says.
```
