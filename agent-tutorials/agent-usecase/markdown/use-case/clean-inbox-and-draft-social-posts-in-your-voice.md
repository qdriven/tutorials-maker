# Clean inbox and draft social posts in your voice

> Grok Bot logged into apps, cleaned and analyzed an inbox, drafted X and LinkedIn posts in the user's voice, and ran workflows overnight, pinging for decisions.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** X · Aug 20, 2026

## What they did

The Grok bot was given access to the user's computer and apps. It logged into sites, clicked through them independently, cleaned and analyzed the inbox, then drafted X and LinkedIn posts in the user's voice. It watched one workflow, ran it overnight on its own, and pinged only when needing decisions or news updates; multiple bots coordinated with each other.

## How it went

The bots managed and improved daily ops with ongoing coordination. On day 3 the user was still discovering additional capabilities.

## Prompt

```text
Log into my email at [email provider] and clean my inbox: archive newsletters and promotions, and flag anything that needs a reply from me. Then read my recent posts on [X and/or LinkedIn] and draft [number] new posts in my voice about [topics]. Show me the drafts and a summary of the inbox changes, and do not publish or delete anything until I approve.
```
