# Turn a large quarterly PDF into short documents and requisitions

> A commenter uses Claude every three months to convert a large PDF into many short documents, Excel requisitions and summary tables.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** Claude  
**Source:** Reddit · Undated

## What they did

The poster described a recurring work task they handle every three months: they receive a large PDF and use Claude in a regular chat window to turn it into many short documents, Excel-based requisitions, and summary tables. They said the key step was learning how to build a reusable skill for that workflow, while keeping the process manual because Claude was not given access to work drives.

## How it went

They said the setup worked well enough to be mind-blowing for that quarterly job, but gave no counts, time savings, or error rates. They also said they had not yet gotten Claude to help with much else.

## Worth knowing

The catch they mention is access: they could not give Claude access to their work drives, and they did not describe any Google Drive or OneDrive setup.

## Prompt

```text
I'm attaching a large PDF, [description, e.g. quarterly report]. Split it into short documents by [section or topic], pull the data into [Excel requisition format] and create summary tables of [key figures]. Finish by listing everything you produced and flagging any data you were unsure about.
```
