# Answer Shopify store customer questions with an AI sales agent

> A commenter uses Rep.AI on a Shopify store to handle routine shipping, promo and product questions, reporting 97% handled without handoff.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** Reddit · Undated

## What they did

A Shopify store owner in gardening and hydroponics said they use Rep.AI’s AI live chat on their store. They described it answering customer and prospect questions, filling product-knowledge gaps, giving product advice, and taking over Shopify messages, emails, and webform queries instead of handing them to a human.

## How it went

They said it pays for itself in sales and reduced Shopify messages to zero. In their reported last 30 days, it handled 28 conversations and resolved 97% of queries without handoff.

## Worth knowing

The biggest catch from the thread is scale: the only metric shared was 28 conversations in 30 days, so the reported results came from a fairly small volume.

## Prompt

```text
Build me a customer chat assistant for my [Shopify] store that answers routine questions about shipping times, current promos, and product details using [FAQ doc or store policies]. When a question involves refunds, complaints, or anything it is unsure about, hand it off to me at [email] with the conversation summary. Draft the setup and show me sample replies to five typical questions before turning it on.
```
