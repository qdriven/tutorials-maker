# Hand a one-person business to AI agents for a week

> A solo owner handed their inbox and scheduler to a network of AI agents for seven days; the result was profitable but uncomfortable, with few details given.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** Claude  
**Source:** medium.com · Undated

## What they did

The owner closed their inbox and schedulers and built three narrow agents on Claude with automation workflows. A research agent gathered industry signals, a creation agent drafted posts and emails in their past style, and a support agent handled messages, FAQs and call booking. They stepped in only if money was at risk or someone felt emotionally uncomfortable.

## How it went

Over seven days the agents published 14 pieces, booked 4 discovery calls, and produced $1,200 in digital product sales, saving about 22 hours. Problems: hollow-sounding content, a published statistic that didn't exist, and a vulnerable message that got a canned checklist.

## Worth knowing

Supervision is still needed: a made-up statistic was published within minutes, so review factual claims before they go out.

## Prompt

```text
Act as my assistant for my [type of business] for the next week. Triage my inbox, sort messages into reply now, reply later and ignore, and draft replies for the first group in my usual tone. Also propose how to schedule my calendar for the week around [my priorities]. Do not send any email or accept any meeting until I have reviewed what you plan to send, and finish with a short summary of what you handled and what needs me.
```
