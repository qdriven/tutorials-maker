# Monitor investing news and produce morning macro summaries

> SouthernValue95's Meta Muse agent Alfred monitors X investing topics, transcribes podcasts, tracks news and regulation for watchlist names, and writes morning macro summaries.

**Category:** Money, Bills & Benefits  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** X · Sep 11, 2026

## What they did

User configured Meta Muse agent Alfred with ongoing tasks to monitor and summarize X posts on specific investing topics and watchlist names, transcribe and summarize podcasts, scan news and regulatory developments for those names, and compile a general morning macro and business news summary.

## How it went

The agent serves as an investment assistant for these functions; user calls it the start of the setup.

## Prompt

```text
Every morning at [time], check X for posts on [investing topics or accounts], plus news and regulatory updates for my watchlist: [tickers]. Transcribe any new episodes from [podcasts] and pull out the key points. Send me one macro summary of the most important items with source links; this is finished when I have a single readable summary, and do not make any trades or post anything.
```
