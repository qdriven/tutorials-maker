# Play a mobile game round on Android by screen control

> The poster says Gemini computer use on Android played a round of Fire Emblem Heroes by reading the screen and moving characters to fight.

**Category:** Games & Simulations  
**Agent:** Gemini  
**Source:** Reddit · Aug 20, 2026

## What they did

The poster built an Android app in AI Studio to give Gemini tool use on the phone: it could open apps, tap and swipe the screen, and interact with XML UI elements. With that setup, Gemini 3.7 Flash read the Fire Emblem Heroes screen, recognized the turn-based state, and moved the poster's strong characters through a round on its own.

## How it went

It completed a round, which the poster says earlier Gemini 3.6 could not do and instead tapped around menus before giving up. The poster says the play was not optimal, simple tasks still took a minute or two, and the behavior was inconsistent.

## Worth knowing

The clearest catch is speed and reliability: even simple tasks took a minute or two, and the poster says the computer-use behavior was still inconsistent.
