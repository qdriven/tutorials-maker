# Follow up with real-estate leads via a text bot

> A realtor runs an AI agent text bot on their IDX site that follows up with leads and has brought in a couple of deals.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** Reddit · Undated

## What they did

One realtor said they use the AI agent/text bot built into a Lofty IDX website. The bot handles the repetitive follow-up on leads that come through the site, while the agent stays hands-on elsewhere, noting they are still very specific about their own listing copy.

## How it went

They said it brought in a couple of deals. The post does not give deal value, conversion rates, setup details, or where the handoff from bot to human happened.

## Worth knowing

The only concrete catch in the thread is that the source is thin: it names Lofty and says the bot follows up with site leads, but gives no workflow, cost, or timing details.
