# Plan trips and social calendars with Claude

> A commenter uses Claude as a travel agent and trip planner, and for birthday lists, gift suggestions and adjusting day-to-day plans.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Claude  
**Source:** Reddit · May 1, 2026

## What they did

In the thread, one commenter says they use Claude as a travel agent and trip planner, plus for a birthday list with reminders and gift suggestions, local events, meals, workouts, and a social calendar. Their setup was a phone-friendly web app Claude suggested; they saved the site to their home screen, run it on Supabase and Vercel, and were testing it with minimal manual input while the agent keeps track of tasks and plans.

## How it went

The commenter says the app was cool and was already usable on phone and computer, but gives no hard results. They were still testing and explicitly looking for better ways to reduce manual input and increase agent support.

## Worth knowing

The main catch they mention is that they had zero clue what they were doing technically, even though they got a working app running on Supabase and Vercel.

## Prompt

```text
Act as my travel agent and personal planner. I'm planning [a trip to destination] from [dates] with [who's traveling] and a budget of about [amount]. We like [interests], so suggest a day-by-day itinerary, recommended places to stay, and a short list of things to book in advance. Then help me with my social calendar: keep a list of upcoming birthdays [names and dates], suggest gift ideas for each within [budget], and tell me what to adjust if my plans change. You're done when I have a finished itinerary and a birthday and gift list I can save.
```
