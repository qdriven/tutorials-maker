# Run grocery, email and photo tasks through Comet browser

> A reviewer gave Comet access to his Intermarché account and tried it on email cleanup, sorting 8,000 Dropbox photos and calendar planning, with failures and security risks.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Perplexity Comet  
**Source:** julienrio.com · Nov 25, 2025

## What they did

Julien Rio paid $20 a month for Comet Pro and gave its assistant logged-in access to Intermarché, his 15-year-old inbox, YouTube Studio, Dropbox and Google Calendar. He asked it to analyse purchases, clean email, sort 8,000 photos into daily folders, and turn a vacation spreadsheet into events. He restarted it repeatedly.

## How it went

Mostly failed. It couldn't read Intermarché's PDF receipts and found only a handful of emails. Only 300–500 of the 8,000 photos moved in days, and the calendar took 40+ restarts. He uninstalled it.

## Worth knowing

The invoice-filing task moved his whole inbox into a oddly named folder; he recovered it, so back up or test on a small sample first.

## Prompt

```text
I'm logged into [grocery store or service]. Please [specific task, e.g. build a basket for these items: list], and show me the final basket with prices. Do not check out, pay, delete or send anything without pausing and showing me exactly what you are about to do first.
```
