# Grow an email list with agent-run cold outreach

> Drew Carr used Hermes agents for daily cold outreach and community comments, with email verification and LLM fit checks, reaching 1,000 subscribers in about 80 days.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Hermes Agent  
**Source:** ryandoser.com · Undated

## What they did

Drew pulls business lists from AI Ark or Apollo, cleans them with Million Verifier, and has Claude keep only companies that fit his offer. Contacts go into a Turso database by campaign. A second Hermes agent, run from Telegram on a spare laptop, finds relevant Facebook group posts and drafts helpful comments. Drew writes his own emails and approves each comment.

## How it went

He reached about 1,000 real subscribers in roughly 80 days with no ad spend. The source gives no per-channel breakdown or reply rates.

## Worth knowing

Hermes ran on a $20 a month OpenAI Codex plan, because Anthropic blocked Claude subscriptions on such agents. Drew also says to run the workflow by hand first.

## Prompt

```text
Help me grow my email list for [topic or product]. Each day, find [number] people who fit [my audience description], verify their email addresses, check fit with my criteria, and draft a personalized message with a link to [signup page]. Pause and show me the drafts before sending anything, and report daily on sends, replies and signups.
```
