# Chase unquoted leads and book callbacks

> The agent watches the inbox or CRM, follows up on unquoted leads after 24 hours, and books callbacks.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** Reddit · Oct 6, 2026

## What they did

A commenter described this as a service-business pattern they see people run: the agent watches the inbox or CRM, checks for leads that have gone 24 hours without a quote, and follows up to book callbacks. The same comment frames it as a recurring outreach/admin workflow rather than a one-off chat task, with the person choosing and driving the setup.

## How it went

The commenter says this is the biggest-ROI use case they see because service leads go cold fast. They also warn Grok Bot is weaker at always-on scheduled jobs like nightly or morning sweeps, which is where users get burned.

## Worth knowing

Main catch: this depends on reliable scheduled automation, and the source specifically says Grok Bot is weaker for that kind of always-on job.

## Prompt

```text
Watch my [inbox or CRM] for leads who asked for a quote and haven't received one in 24 hours. Draft a friendly follow-up for each and propose callback times from my calendar at [availability]. Show me the drafts and times first, and send or book only after I approve.
```
