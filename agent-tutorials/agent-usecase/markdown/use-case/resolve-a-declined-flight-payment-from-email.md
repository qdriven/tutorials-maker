# Resolve a declined flight payment from email

> With email access, Meta Muse spotted a declined flight payment, found the new debit card email, confirmed payment, and added the flight to the calendar.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** X · Sep 28, 2026

## What they did

User granted Muse read access to email. The agent spotted the declined flight payment email, connected it to a separate notice about a new debit card, told the user exactly how to activate the card on the bank site, confirmed the payment succeeded, and added the booking to the calendar including PNR, seat numbers, and check-in time.

## How it went

Payment cleared and flight was added to calendar with full details. No other metrics reported.

## Worth knowing

Zero setup or prompting was required.

## Prompt

```text
Search my email for a declined payment on my [flight/booking] and find any recent email about my new [debit or credit card]. Tell me what failed and what you plan to do, and pause for my approval before entering any payment details or confirming payment. Once it's paid, add the trip to my calendar with the dates and times and show me the final result.
```
