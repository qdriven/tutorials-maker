# Review Slack channels for business issues

> Lindy reviewed Slack activity across sales, support, inventory and reviews for a clothing business and returned a report on demand, support issues, low stock and feedback.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** Lindy  
**Source:** X · Oct 3, 2026

## What they did

The owner of Lumen Clothing connected Lindy to the company's Slack workspace and asked it to review activity in sales, customer support, inventory and reviews channels. Lindy scanned the messages on its own, connected related items across channels, and produced one report plus a list of next steps.

## How it went

The report listed customer demand signals, five unresolved support issues, low-stock and out-of-stock items, and recent review feedback, then converted the findings into four concrete actions such as restocking high-demand products.

## Prompt

```text
Create an agent that reviews my Slack channels [channel names for sales, support, inventory, and reviews] when I ask. It should return one report covering sales, open support issues, low stock items, and customer feedback. Keep it read-only so it posts nothing in Slack, and stop once I have a report I can skim in two minutes.
```
