# Book tables and watch for openings or price drops

> The OdessiaTravel agent books tables and keeps monitoring sold-out openings or price drops on travel bookings rather than just listing options.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** X · Oct 14, 2026

## What they did

The agent receives a booking request for tables or travel. It completes the reservation on its own and continues monitoring afterward for newly available slots or lower fares.

## How it went

It acts ahead of the user checking, unlike list-only chatbots. No results or numbers are stated.

## Prompt

```text
I want [restaurant or travel booking, with details] on [dates]. If it's sold out or too pricey, keep monitoring [booking site] every [interval] for openings or prices below [amount]. When one appears, show me the details and pause for my approval before booking or paying. Stop once I've confirmed a booking or after [end date], and send me an update whenever something changes.
```
