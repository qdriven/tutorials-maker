# Automate home tasks like emails and ticket price checks

> Microsoft's Omar Shahine used OpenClaw at home to draft emails and investigate concert-ticket prices.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** OpenClaw  
**Source:** geekwire.com · Undated

## What they did

Omar Shahine started using OpenClaw earlier in the year to automate tasks at home, such as drafting an email or investigating concert-ticket prices. He called his assistant Lobster. The article gives no setup detail on the home use. It covers his later move into a Microsoft team building OpenClaw-based agents that suggest actions and wait for user approval.

## How it went

The article doesn't report results for the home tasks. Shahine demoed Lobster internally on Feb. 26. By March 31 he had a new role, and by May 1 more than 3,000 Microsoft employees were testing the related ClawPilot.

## Worth knowing

Microsoft's Defender team says OpenClaw is untrusted code execution with persistent credentials and shouldn't run on a standard personal or enterprise workstation.
