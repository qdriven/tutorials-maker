# Win a flight refund dispute with ChatGPT

> A traveler used ChatGPT to frame a dispute over a cancelled IndiGo flight and says they won the ₹43,992 refund.

**Category:** Money, Bills & Benefits  
**Agent:** ChatGPT  
**Source:** Reddit · Mar 1, 2026

## What they did

The traveler used ChatGPT throughout an IndiGo refund fight after a January 22 flight-cancellation email, a failed attempt to accept an alternate flight because the website errored, and a later refund denial. They say ChatGPT helped them research aviation rules, draft each escalation, and point out contradictions as they moved from airline support to AirSewa, then a DGCA grievance, and finally a CPGRAMS appeal.

## How it went

They say IndiGo eventually gave a full ₹43,992 refund. The post does not share the actual prompts, filings, or response times, so the process details are high level.

## Worth knowing

The poster’s main caution is that AI helped with research and wording, but persistence and careful documentation were still the decisive parts.

## Prompt

```text
My [airline] flight [flight number] on [date] was cancelled, and I was offered [what they offered] instead of the refund of [amount]. Here's the timeline and what the airline said: [paste details]. Explain which rules or passenger rights apply in [country], then draft a firm, polite dispute message asking for a full refund by a set deadline, and list what evidence I should attach. I'll send it myself.
```
