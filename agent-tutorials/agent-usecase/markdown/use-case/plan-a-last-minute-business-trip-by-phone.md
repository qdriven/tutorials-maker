# Plan a last-minute business trip by phone

> In OpenAI's demo, a Dot found a refundable policy-compliant flight and hotel options and prepared a booking, though the flight was still pending at the end.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Dots  
**Source:** cmarix.com · Undated

## What they did

The presenter phoned a Dot and asked for a refundable, policy-compliant flight to LA plus East LA hotel options, needing to be in Hollywood by 3 p.m. The Dot weighed airport and drive times, suggested an earlier Southwest flight, showed hotel photos and map views in chat, then prepared a one-night booking for the Silver Lake hotel the presenter chose.

## How it went

The Dot gave a flight and hotel plan and prepared the booking. At the end of the demo, about 8:16, the flight was still being booked, and the Dot still had to verify the full price and cancellation terms.

## Worth knowing

The launch-day demo stalled on stage, and this account describes a replay the next day. Texting a Dot is a limited beta for some US Pro users only.

## Prompt

```text
I need to travel to [city] for [purpose] on [date], returning [date]. Find refundable flights and hotels near [location] that fit this policy: [budget, cabin class, rules]. Give me your top two options with prices, and prepare the booking but pause for my approval before paying.
```
