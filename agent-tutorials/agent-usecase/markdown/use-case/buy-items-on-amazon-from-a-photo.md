# Buy items on Amazon from a photo

> Author used ChatGPT Agent to reorder an item from a photo of its wrapper, handling the whole Amazon purchase until Amazon blocked agents.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** ChatGPT Agent  
**Source:** HN · Nov 4, 2025

## What they did

The author photographed a wrapper from an item they wanted again and told ChatGPT Agent to buy a new one on Amazon. The agent then handled the whole purchase process from that photo and instruction. The source gives no further detail on the steps or on where the author stepped in.

## How it went

It worked well until Amazon blocked ChatGPT Agent, which ended the workflow. The author then began shopping at other retailers instead.

## Worth knowing

Amazon blocked the agent, so this may no longer work there; the author's own guess is that agents cheaply compare listings and pick the cheapest.

## Prompt

```text
I'm attaching a photo of [item or wrapper]. Identify the product, find the best match on [Amazon or retailer], and add it to the cart in the size and quantity I want: [quantity]. Stop at the checkout page and show me the item, price, and delivery address before buying anything.
```
