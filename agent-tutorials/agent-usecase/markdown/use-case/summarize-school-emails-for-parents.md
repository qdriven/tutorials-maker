# Summarize school emails for parents

> A Grok bot reads kids' school emails nightly and reports what to pack, wear, sign or pay for, flagging deadlines and sign-ups.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** X · Oct 3, 2026

## What they did

A Grok bot template was created for busy parents. It reads kids' school emails and reports nightly on items to pack, wear, sign or pay for while flagging sign-ups, deadlines and blanks.

## How it went

No specific results or shortfalls are stated.

## Worth knowing

A link to the Grok Bot template is provided.

## Prompt

```text
Every evening at [time], read the new emails from [kids' school email addresses or sender names] and send me a summary of what to pack, wear, sign, or pay for tomorrow and this week. Flag any deadlines and sign-ups with the date in bold-free plain text at the top. If nothing needs action, say so in one line. Don't reply to or pay for anything without showing me first.
```
