# Review vendor contracts and draft redlines with AI

> In-house counsel uses AI for vendor and DPA review, redline responses, contract drafting and first-pass research, checking its output like a paralegal's.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** Reddit · Mar 2, 2025

## What they did

One in-house commenter said they use enterprise Claude and Gemini, chosen for data-privacy protections, on transactional work rather than final judgment calls. They use them for vendor contract and DPA review, drafting customer-contract redline responses in a specified tone, generating contract language, and doing first-pass legal research, then check the output themselves like a strong paralegal’s work.

## How it went

The reported outcome was practical day-to-day help across contract workflows, but not hands-off automation. The catch was that every output still needed lawyer review and verification.

## Worth knowing

The most concrete limitation in the thread is that this only made sense with enterprise versions that purportedly protect company data.

## Prompt

```text
Act as a first-pass reviewer for this vendor contract: [paste text or attach]. We are the [customer/vendor] and our key concerns are [e.g. liability cap, data protection, termination, indemnity]. Finish with a table of risky clauses, why each is a concern, and proposed redline language for each. Flag anything you are unsure about so I can verify it myself, since I'll check everything like a paralegal's work.
```
