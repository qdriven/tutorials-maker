# Find fitting job postings and tailor resumes

> People use Claude to search for job postings that fit and tailor the resume for the ones the user picks.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Claude  
**Source:** Reddit · Jul 13, 2026

## What they did

Commenters describe using Claude mainly before the application itself: one person has it search for relevant postings, then tailors resumes only for jobs they choose. Others keep a canonical multi-page resume or templates in a Claude project, feed in a job description, and let it pick matching experience, draft a targeted resume or cover letter, and sometimes summarize the listing or score the fit before a person reviews and submits manually.

## How it went

The reported benefit was speed: one user said AI-assisted job hunting finally started getting them interviews, and another said it saves a lot of time. But multiple commenters said full auto-apply is unreliable, especially on Workday-style forms, and manual cleanup or submission is still needed.

## Worth knowing

The clearest number was from one commenter who said a custom setup could generate about 200 tailored resumes in roughly 3 minutes.

## Prompt

```text
I'm looking for [job title] roles in [location or remote] and my resume is below. Find [5-10] current postings that fit my skills and salary range of [range], and give me a short list with why each fits. After I choose which ones I want, tailor my resume to each one without inventing experience, and show me each version for review before I send anything. [paste resume]
```
