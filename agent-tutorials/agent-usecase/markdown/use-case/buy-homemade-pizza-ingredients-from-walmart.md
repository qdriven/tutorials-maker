# Buy homemade pizza ingredients from Walmart

> A reviewer had ChatGPT Agent shop at Walmart for pizza ingredients, handing control back briefly for an anti-bot check.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** ChatGPT Agent  
**Source:** androidauthority.com · Undated

## What they did

The reviewer asked ChatGPT Agent to buy everything for a homemade pizza from Walmart, with no ingredients or price guidance. The agent opened a virtual browser and hit an anti-bot check, so he took control for about ten seconds. It then searched for dough, sauce, mozzarella and pepperoni, and chose items by value instead of taking the first result.

## How it went

The cart was full within four minutes, and the agent skipped a premade frozen pizza when shopping for pepperoni. It stopped at checkout. The reviewer didn't enter login or payment details, so no order was placed.

## Worth knowing

Agent mode needs ChatGPT Plus at $20 a month and was capped at 40 messages a month. Expect to handle CAPTCHAs, login and payment yourself.

## Prompt

```text
Go to [store website, e.g. Walmart] and add the ingredients for homemade [dish, e.g. pizza] for [number] people to my cart, choosing items under [budget] total. Tell me if you need me to take over for a login or a verification check. Stop at the checkout page and show me the cart and total, and do not place the order without my confirmation.
```
