# Find outperforming YouTube videos and newsletter advertisers

> Manus browsed YouTube for videos outperforming their channel size and identified 20 companies advertising in AI newsletters with contact details, then was set up for scheduled research runs.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Manus  
**Source:** nocode.mba · Undated

## What they did

The author ran two research tasks in Manus at the same time. One had it browse YouTube for AI-tool videos from the last month that beat their channel size on views. The other asked for 20 companies that advertised in AI and no-code newsletters in the last month. He spot-checked the results, then set the advertiser task to run daily at 11 AM and the YouTube task weekly.

## How it went

Manus returned a trends summary and a spreadsheet of advertisers with ad copy and contact details. Spot checks confirmed sponsors like 11 Labs and IBM, but some contact details were missing and some newsletters were misattributed.

## Worth knowing

Verify the data before acting on it. The author says specific prompts matter, and the post is sponsored by Manus.

## Prompt

```text
Browse YouTube and find [number] videos in [niche] that are getting far more views than their channel size would suggest, noting the title, channel, subscriber count, views, and why it might be working. Then give me a spreadsheet of companies advertising in [newsletters or niche] with their contact details from public sources. Don't contact anyone; just deliver the lists and stop when done.
```
