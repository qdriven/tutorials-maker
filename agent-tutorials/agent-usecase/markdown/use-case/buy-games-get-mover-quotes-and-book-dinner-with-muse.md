# Buy games, get mover quotes and book dinner with Muse

> A reviewer had Meta's Muse buy a Switch 2 game, call four movers for quotes and help book an OpenTable restaurant.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** finance.yahoo.com · Undated

## What they did

The reviewer asked Muse to buy a Switch 2 game. It navigated Nintendo's site, and he typed his login into a secure field the agent couldn't read. He then pressed buy himself. For movers, Muse asked for addresses, bedroom count, stairs or elevators, and contact details, then found four companies and sent quote requests. For dinner, it suggested four options and he booked one through OpenTable.

## How it went

Movers began calling within about two minutes, one gave a quote and others emailed. The game purchase worked but was slower than ordering directly. Muse also invented a restaurant phone number and listed a long-closed venue.

## Worth knowing

Check any phone numbers or venues Muse gives you. It admitted making one number up and relied on stale data for a closed restaurant.

## Prompt

```text
Help me with three errands: find [item or game] at the best price from [store] and get it to the checkout, find [number] moving companies near [city] and gather quotes for a [size of home] move on [date], and help me find a table for [number] people at [restaurant or cuisine] on [date and time]. Summarize the quotes and options in one list. Pause before paying, confirming a booking or making any call on my behalf so I can approve it.
```
