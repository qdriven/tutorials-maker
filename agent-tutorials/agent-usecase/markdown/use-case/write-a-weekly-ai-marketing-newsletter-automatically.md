# Write a weekly AI marketing newsletter automatically

> A scheduled Lindy agent researches the week's news, drafts the newsletter plus social posts and scripts, stores them in Airtable and emails the result.

**Category:** Content & Creative  
**Agent:** Lindy  
**Source:** annikahelendi.substack.com · Undated

## What they did

Annika Helendi built the agent in Lindy with a Monday 9am schedule trigger. Her prompt tells it to research the past week's AI marketing news, write content for marketers, store it in Airtable and email her the newsletter. She gave it web search, Airtable, email and AI writing skills, plus her style guide for context. The agent chooses its own tools and steps.

## How it went

She says setup took under 10 minutes and produces a newsletter draft plus LinkedIn posts, tweets and video scripts. The post gives no sample output or quality assessment.

## Worth knowing

The full prompts and Airtable base template sit in her AI Marketing Masters community, not in the post. She also still uses Make.com for workflows needing precise control.

## Prompt

```text
Every [day and time], research the past week's top news in [my topic or industry] and draft a newsletter in my voice, using [link to a past issue] as a style example. Also create [three] social posts to go with it. Save everything to [Airtable or Google Docs] and email me the drafts. Don't publish or send to subscribers; stop once the drafts are in my inbox.
```
