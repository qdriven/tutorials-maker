# Send daily AI news research reports

> Grok Bot reads X and AI news every few hours, checks sourced figures, and sends five written research reports a day.

**Category:** Research & Knowledge  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** X · Oct 10, 2026

## What they did

A Grok bot was configured to scan X and AI news every few hours. It generated five written research reports each day, with the user's system verifying every sourced figure before any number appeared in the output. The user then reviewed and decided on the finished reports.

## How it went

Research arrived pre-finished, moving the user's role from searching to deciding.

## Prompt

```text
Every [number] hours, scan [X accounts, news sites or feeds] for news about [topic]. Check any figures against the original sources and note any you can't confirm. Send me [number] short written reports a day at [times], each with the key points and links, and nothing more until I change the schedule.
```
