# Summarize investments and turn school calendars into iCal invites

> A commenter uses Claude to prepare monthly investment performance and net worth summaries and to convert school calendars and newsletters into iCal invites.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Claude  
**Source:** Reddit · Oct 1, 2026

## What they did

One commenter built a Claude co-work project for finances: they drop monthly statements into a folder, and Claude reads them to produce a multi-page monthly writeup on investment performance, net-worth-change bridges, and key market news for that month. A separate commenter uses Claude from photos of the school year calendar and newsletters to generate iCal invites, adding reminders two days before and two hours before, and marking no-school days as all-day blocks.

## How it went

The finance workflow yields a few pages of monthly summaries. The calendar workflow populated a shared calendar that their son also started using; neither commenter gave accuracy rates or other quantified results.

## Worth knowing

The clearest catch is setup format: one workflow depends on saving statements into a folder each month, and the other starts from photos of calendars and newsletters.

## Prompt

```text
Here is my [monthly investment statement/account data] and a list of my other assets and debts: [paste or attach]. Write a one-page summary of performance this month, my current net worth, and any notable changes. Then here is a [school calendar/newsletter]: [paste or attach]. Turn all dates into a .ics file I can import into my calendar, and list any dates you were unsure about.
```
