# Call a car rental company to add an infant seat

> Grok Bot with JooVoice phoned a rental company, navigated the phone menu, spoke with a person and got an email confirming the infant seat change. · Oct 13, 2026

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** x.com · Oct 13, 2026 · Submitted by @gtupak

## What they did

The user asked JooVoice to order an infant seat for an existing rental car booking. The agent called the company, navigated the automated menu, and remained on hold until connected.

## How it went

The request was handled successfully from start to finish.

## Prompt

```text
Please phone [rental company] at [phone number] about my reservation [confirmation number] under the name [my name] and ask them to add an infant seat for pickup on [date] at [location]. Navigate the phone menu, speak with a person, and get the change confirmed by email to [my email]. Before agreeing to any extra fee, pause and tell me the price, and when finished send me a summary of what was agreed.
```
