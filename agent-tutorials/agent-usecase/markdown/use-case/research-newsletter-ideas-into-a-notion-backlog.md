# Research newsletter ideas into a Notion backlog

> A Content Researcher Bot researches an idea across YouTube, the web via Tavily and X, and adds findings to the author's Notion content backlog.

**Category:** Content & Creative  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** aimaker.substack.com · Undated

## What they did

Wyndo created a named Content Researcher bot in Grok Bot. He gave it background on his newsletter, AI Maker, and connected Supadata for YouTube data, X, Notion and Tavily. Given a newsletter idea, the bot researches it across YouTube, the web and X, then adds the findings to his Notion content backlog. He authorized X and Notion through a sign-in button the bot raised in chat.

## How it went

Wyndo says he returns to Notion and finds source material waiting instead of an empty idea card. He reviews it manually. He shows a live test but gives no quantified results.

## Worth knowing

The bot's progress view is sparse, so you can't see why a task is slow. The X API connection involves funded access, so check current pricing first.

## Prompt

```text
Research this newsletter idea: [your idea]. Look across YouTube, the web, and X for what people are already saying, the best sources, and gaps I could cover. Add a new entry to my Notion content backlog at [Notion database name] with a summary, links, and a suggested angle. Stop once the entry is saved and show me the link.
```
