# Find concert tickets across email, calendar, and wallet

> The poster asked Muse to search calendar, Ticketmaster, email, photos, and wallet to locate their concert tickets; the thread discusses it tracing them across sources, with disappointment.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** Reddit · Sep 18, 2026

## What they did

The poster gave Muse permission to several sources and asked it to look at next month’s concerts in their calendar, then hunt down the matching tickets across Ticketmaster, email, photos, and wallet. In the thread, Muse reported it could not access Photos or the iPhone Mail app, and commenters said a workaround was logging into web versions like iCloud or Ticketmaster inside Muse’s browser.

## How it went

It did not complete the cross-source ticket-finding task the poster wanted. The thread suggests Muse could pull Ticketmaster tickets, but not reliably gather emailed or photo-stored tickets for an Apple-centric setup.

## Worth knowing

The main catch was Apple access: commenters said Photos was not available yet, and iCloud or Mail might only be reachable by signing into their web versions inside Muse.

## Prompt

```text
Find my tickets for [event] on [date]. Search my email, calendar, Google Wallet, and photos for confirmations or ticket barcodes, including [ticketing service, e.g. Ticketmaster]. Give me a summary of where each ticket is, the seat info, and the entry time, and don't forward, transfer, or delete anything without asking me first.
```
