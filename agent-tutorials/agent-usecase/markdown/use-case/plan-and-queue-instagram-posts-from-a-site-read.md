# Plan and queue Instagram posts from a site read

> An agency owner had Grok Bot read his sitemap, draft an Instagram strategy and queue posts for scheduling; nothing published until he approved.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Grok Bot  
**Source:** aliakbarfakhri.com · Undated

## What they did

He pointed one Grok Bot agent at his sitemap and had it read the actual pages in the browser. It returned an Instagram strategy with content pillars, a posting cadence and a visual direction. It then generated images with Grok Imagine, using five reference images to keep a mascot consistent. It sent the posts to Zernio's scheduling API, and he approved the queue because the bot pauses before publishing.

## How it went

The images held together across a full grid, though it took about 25 mascot variations to get a character that did. The Zernio connector failed on him, as connectors did for other reviewers.

## Worth knowing

Usage allowance isn't published; he used 34% of his weekly allowance in 12 days across four workflows, so budget for that ceiling rather than the $20 Cursor Pro price.

## Prompt

```text
Read the sitemap at [your website URL] and summarize what the business offers and who it serves. Then draft an Instagram strategy and [number] post drafts with captions and hashtags, and queue them in [scheduling tool] as drafts. You're done when the drafts are queued; do not publish anything, and show me the full set for approval before scheduling any of them.
```
