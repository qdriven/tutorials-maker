# Handle family logistics, reminders and research with a personal assistant

> The poster uses Muse with its own VM to book kids' workshop spots, send medication reminders, draft interview questions and a house-help offer, and price-match.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** Reddit · Oct 13, 2026

## What they did

The poster uses Muse as an always-on personal assistant with its own VM that can browse the web, remember context, and run recurring tasks. They describe having it send medication reminders every 10 minutes until confirmed, watch for kids’ workshop registration and book spots, draft hiring materials for part-time house help, research exact TPMS part numbers for price matching, and manage routine reminders; in comments they say they have not given it access to email.

## How it went

The reported result is that it handles ongoing family and household tasks more reliably than one-off phone reminders, especially for repeated alerts and calendar-like chores. The post gives no hard savings numbers, and the examples are anecdotal rather than measured.

## Worth knowing

The main catch mentioned is caution on permissions: the poster says they have not connected Muse to email.

## Prompt

```text
Help me with family logistics this week: [tasks, e.g. find and book a spot in a kids' workshop on [date], set medication reminders for [person] at [times], and draft [a message or interview questions]]. When each task is done, give me a short confirmation of what you did. Before booking, paying, or sending any message, pause and show me the details so I can approve.
```
