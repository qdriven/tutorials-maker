# Pay bills, clear inbox, and order groceries

> A Muse agent called Scout reportedly paid bills, cleared the inbox, and ordered groceries for its user.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Meta Muse  
**Source:** X · Sep 15, 2026

## What they did

User introduced Scout as their Muse agent. Scout paid bills, reached inbox zero, and ordered groceries.

## How it went

All tasks finished before the user's coffee went cold.

## Prompt

```text
Go through my inbox and flag bills and messages that need action, then archive the newsletters and promotions you're sure are clutter. Also prepare payment for these bills: [list of bills], and a grocery order from [store] for [items or usual list]. Before paying anything, ordering anything, or deleting anything, show me exactly what you're about to do and wait for my OK.
```
