# Hunt jobs, tailor resumes and apply automatically

> An agent called Resume finds and ranks jobs around schedule and pay wants, drafts tailored resumes and submits applications; the author reports 3 interviews and 1 offer.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Devin  
**Source:** X · Oct 6, 2026

## What they did

User described desired job type, schedule and pay to the Resume bot at x.ai. The bot searched listings, ranked them by fit, drafted tailored resumes for top matches, and tracked applicant status. No further user tools or steps described beyond initial prompt.

## How it went

Secured 3 interviews and 1 job offer with start date the following week that aligned with existing schedule and finances.

## Prompt

```text
Find open [job title] roles in [location/remote] that match my needs: [pay range, schedule, other preferences]. Rank the top [10] and, using my resume at [file/link], draft a tailored resume and cover note for each. Do not submit any application until I've reviewed it; show me each one and wait for my approval.
```
