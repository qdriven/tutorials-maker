# Order lunch through an AI agent

> Abhishek Upadhya had an AI agent order his lunch, giving it his CVV and OTP, and the food arrived without further intervention.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** X · Sep 11, 2026

## What they did

Abhishek gave an AI agent his CVV and OTP so it could place a lunch order. The agent completed the purchase and payment independently. Delivery arrived with no further steps or checks from him.

## How it went

The food was delivered without any intervention. He described the result as feeling surprisingly good and magical.

## Prompt

```text
Order me lunch from [restaurant or delivery app] to [address]: [food preferences, budget, dietary restrictions]. Get as far as the final checkout screen and then stop. Show me the items, total, and delivery time, and I will enter the payment details and any one-time codes myself.
```
