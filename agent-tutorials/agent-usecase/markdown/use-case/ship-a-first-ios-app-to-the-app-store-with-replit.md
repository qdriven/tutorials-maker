# Ship a first iOS app to the App Store with Replit

> The poster built their first app on Replit with ChatGPT help and got it approved on the App Store.

**Category:** Software & DevOps  
**Agent:** Replit Agent  
**Source:** Reddit · Undated

## What they did

The poster says they built their first-ever iOS app entirely in Replit, using ChatGPT heavily for planning and architecture and Replit for implementation. The coding took under a week; the hands-on work afterward was setting up the Apple Developer account, handling certificates and provisioning, fixing metadata, screenshots, and subscription wording, then resubmitting through repeated App Store reviews.

## How it went

The app was approved on the App Store, but approval took about a month and the poster says the review process was roughly five times harder than building the app. They specifically mention waits of about five days between reviews and multiple rejection points around store-listing details.

## Worth knowing

They warn that costs stack up fast across tokens, the Apple developer account, API usage, and domains.
