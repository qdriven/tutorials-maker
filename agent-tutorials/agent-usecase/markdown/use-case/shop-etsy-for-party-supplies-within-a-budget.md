# Shop Etsy for party supplies within a budget

> Chrome auto browse with Gemini searched Etsy, added items to the cart, applied discount codes and left the cart ready for the user to review.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** Gemini  
**Source:** aol.com · Undated

## What they did

In Chrome's Gemini sidebar, the user names the site (Etsy), the party theme and a budget. Gemini then clicks, types and scrolls through Etsy on its own. It searches for supplies, adds suitable items to the cart and looks up discount codes. If the user allows it, it can log in through Google Password Manager. A "Take over task" button lets the user step in.

## How it went

Gemini left the checkout cart ready for the user to review. The article describes Google's example and gives no results, savings or failure cases.

## Worth knowing

Auto browse works only in desktop Chrome and is rolling out to paying Google AI Pro and AI Ultra subscribers.

## Prompt

```text
Open Etsy and find party supplies for [type of party, e.g. a 6-year-old's dinosaur birthday] with a total budget of [amount]. Look for [items, e.g. banner, tableware, favors, balloons], add the best-value options to my cart, and try any available discount codes. Stop with the cart ready for me to review and do not check out or enter payment details. Show me the items, prices and total before I decide.
```
