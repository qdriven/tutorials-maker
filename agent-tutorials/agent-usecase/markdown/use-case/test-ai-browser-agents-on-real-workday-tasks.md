# Test AI browser agents on real workday tasks

> The author ran Comet and another agent through real workday tasks like booking a flight and handling calendar chaos; one took 16 minutes to book while the other did the calendar work in about 5.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** Perplexity Comet  
**Source:** natesnewsletter.substack.com · Undated

## What they did

The author ran eight everyday workday tests on Comet and on OpenAI's Operator, giving both the same tasks. They included rescheduling a meeting and drafting the follow-up email from a sidebar, finding a restaurant, sifting LinkedIn contacts affected by layoffs, and pulling insights from Amazon filings. The detailed per-test results are paywalled.

## How it went

The free portion says Comet delivered, handling calendar chaos in minutes, while Operator was slow and clumsy. The 16-minute flight booking and the full side-by-side are in the paid section, which I couldn't read.

## Worth knowing

The post says the tool costs $200/month, so the author recommends testing it on your own real work and checking the time saved before paying.

## Prompt

```text
Help me test you on real work tasks: first, find and compare flights from [origin] to [destination] on [date] and get as far as the checkout page without paying; second, look at my calendar for [week] and propose a fix for any conflicts or double-bookings. Tell me how long each task took and where you got stuck. Pause and ask me before submitting any form, sending any invite, or changing any calendar event.
```
