# Find target businesses with Manus

> The author tested Manus on identifying companies to target; it found specific firms and useful insights, but login barriers blocked some data and human verification was still needed.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Manus  
**Source:** dicloak.com · Undated

## What they did

The author gave Manus a research brief: find HVAC, roofing and plumbing companies across several states, including heat pump specialists, that fit set revenue and lead-generation spend requirements. Manus worked on its own, drawing on chamber of commerce directories and industry publications. It pulled revenue figures, marketing spend and decision-maker contact details.

## How it went

After a 35-minute run it returned only three leads and used nearly 90% of the free credits. Login walls on some sites blocked data, so the findings needed human checking.

## Worth knowing

Credits go fast: the standard plan is 3,900 credits for $40, and one complex search used almost all the free credits. Access also requires an invitation code.

## Prompt

```text
Find [number] companies that fit this profile: [industry, size, location, and the problem I solve]. For each, give the company name, what they do, a likely decision-maker role, and a reason they might need my offering, with source links. Mark any facts you couldn't verify or that were behind a login so I can check them myself.
```
