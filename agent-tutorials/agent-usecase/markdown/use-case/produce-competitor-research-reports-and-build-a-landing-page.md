# Produce competitor research reports and build a landing page

> A team ran a multi-source competitor research report and a working landing page build through Manus, logging output and fixes needed.

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** Manus  
**Source:** delta4.io · Undated

## What they did

The team gave Manus two single prompts. One asked for a formatted report on five real project-management competitors, covering pricing, audience, features and user complaints from review sites. The other asked for a landing page for a fictional SaaS, with a working call-to-action button, built with the Web App Builder. They logged the time taken and the fixes needed for each.

## How it went

The report took 8 minutes and worked well, but seat-minimum details were missing for 2 of 5 competitors. The landing page took 20 minutes with good design, but its form claimed an email was sent when none was.

## Worth knowing

Credits burn unpredictably on complex runs and "high service load" errors occur, so test with synthetic data first and check that every interactive element really works.

## Prompt

```text
Research [number] competitors of [my company or product] using their websites, pricing pages, reviews and recent news. Produce a report covering positioning, pricing, strengths, weaknesses, and gaps I could exploit, with sources cited. Then build a working landing page for [my product] based on those findings, and list anything you couldn't verify or had to work around.
```
