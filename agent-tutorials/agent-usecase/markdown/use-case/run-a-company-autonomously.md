# Run a company autonomously

> Andon Labs on why they built Pion, an agent designed to run any company on its own. · Sep 14, 2026

**Category:** Work & Operations  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent

## What they did

Andon Labs built Pion to run its real businesses: vending machines, a retail store in SF, a cafe in Stockholm and radio stations. Persistent agents get email, phone, banking, browser and secure computing environments. Pion is now open as a research preview, and people with a business or idea can join a waitlist. Andon says it relies on automated monitoring to catch unwanted behavior.

## How it went

Early agents made costly mistakes, and by late 2025 frontier models ran a vending machine profitably. The store and cafe lost money early and are still unprofitable, though they have improved with newer models.

## Worth knowing

The store and cafe lost a lot of money early, partly from rent and the salaries of the humans they hired. Expect incidents, since Andon says unchecked agents risk them.

## Prompt

```text
I want to test how far an AI agent can run a small business: [describe business, e.g. an online store for X]. Do not spend money, publish anything, or contact anyone until you've shown me the exact action and I've approved it.
```
