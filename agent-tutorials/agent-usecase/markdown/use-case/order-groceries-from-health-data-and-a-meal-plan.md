# Order groceries from health data and a meal plan

> Austen Allred's AI automatically orders groceries based on body scans, health data, and preferences, mapped to a meal plan.

**Category:** Shopping & Errands  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** X · Aug 15, 2026

## What they did

Austen fed the AI his recent body scans, health data, preferences, and meal plan. The agent then placed grocery orders automatically via HEB delivery. No further steps or human interventions are described.

## How it went

It became one of his favorite AI uses. No results, quantities, or shortfalls are stated.

## Worth knowing

HEB delivery lacks a Claude connector or MCP, which Austen said he might build himself.

## Prompt

```text
Using my health goals ([goals]), dietary preferences ([preferences]), and this data: [paste lab results, body scan numbers, or nutrition targets], create a one-week meal plan. Then add the ingredients to a cart at [grocery store or delivery service]. You are done when the cart matches the plan; show me the cart and total and wait for my approval before checkout.
```
