# Find a London flat to rent

> The poster used Claude Cowork to search for and find a flat to rent in London in five days.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** Claude Cowork  
**Source:** Reddit · May 5, 2026

## What they did

The poster had Claude Cowork run a repeat search across SpareRoom, OpenRent, Rightmove, and Zoopla twice a day. It screened out student flats and homes with three or more bedrooms, drafted personalised outreach for promising listings, and emailed the poster a digest they could act on from their phone.

## How it went

The poster says this process found them a London rental in five days. The source does not give harder numbers on viewings, replies, or which part of the process still needed manual handling.

## Worth knowing

The key catch is that the setup relied on Claude Cowork repeatedly scanning multiple rental sites and sending digest emails on a schedule.
