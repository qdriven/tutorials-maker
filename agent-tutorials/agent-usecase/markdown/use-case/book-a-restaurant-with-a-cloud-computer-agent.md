# Book a restaurant with a cloud-computer agent

> OpenAI's Dots agent, running on its own cloud computer, booked a restaurant on its own, though it was slow and required ChatGPT Pro.

**Category:** Travel & Bookings  
**Agent:** Dots  
**Source:** X · Oct 1, 2026

## What they did

OpenAI's Dots agent ran on its own cloud computer with screen takeover access. It researched options and booked a restaurant independently after the user launched the task through ChatGPT Pro. No further human steps occurred during execution.

## How it went

The booking succeeded on its own. It proved slow and still required a ChatGPT Pro subscription.

## Worth knowing

Needs ChatGPT Pro and runs slowly.

## Prompt

```text
Book a table for [party size] at [restaurant name or type of cuisine and neighborhood] on [date] around [time]. Check availability on their booking page or reservation sites, and tell me the options you find. Pause and show me the exact reservation details before you confirm anything or enter my contact info, and stop once I have a confirmation to review.
```
