# Fill and submit visa forms automatically

> The memU bot agent fills visa forms, finds the right documents on the device, handles image uploads and submits without manual prompts.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** custom-built or unnamed agent  
**Source:** X · Feb 2, 2026

## What they did

In one minute customize memU bot as a personal AI assistant that understands your files on the device. The agent then locates the right documents, fills visa forms automatically, handles image uploads, and submits with no further prompts or manual steps.

## How it went

It removes the usual pain of repetitive forms, missing documents, mismatched photos, and last-minute stress during visa applications.

## Worth knowing

Customization of the assistant takes one minute.

## Prompt

```text
Help me complete the [country/type] visa application at [website]. Use the documents in [folder path] to fill in each field and upload the required photos and scans in the right format. Stop before the final submission and payment and show me a summary of every answer for review.
```
