# Run marketing research and copywriting drafts with agents

> The author built OpenClaw-style agents for research, content structure and first-draft copy; research saved 2-3 hours a week and agents needed oversight.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** OpenClaw  
**Source:** cgtech.com.au · Undated

## What they did

Tony Nissen, a marketing lead at CG TECH, spent a few months on OpenClaw, the open-source agent platform. He built a handful of agents: one for research, one for structuring long-form content, one for copywriting drafts, plus a marketing agent that compared campaigns. He tested their outputs, reviewed them and adjusted their behaviour over time.

## How it went

The research agent saved two to three hours a week. The copywriting agent removed the blank-page first draft, and the marketing agent spotted campaign patterns faster than he could. None were perfect, and all needed oversight.

## Worth knowing

Expect an iteration phase of testing, reviewing outputs and adjusting agent behaviour before relying on them. The article gives few setup details.

## Prompt

```text
Act as my marketing research assistant for [product or business] aimed at [target audience]. Each week, research [competitors or topics], summarize key findings with sources, propose a content outline, and draft first-pass copy for [channel, e.g. landing page or newsletter]. Save everything in [folder] and do not publish or send anything; flag any claims you're unsure of so I can check them.
```
