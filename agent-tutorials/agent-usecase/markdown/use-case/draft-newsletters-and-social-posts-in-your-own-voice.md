# Draft newsletters and social posts in your own voice

> A marketer built a Cursor workflow that learns from his content and scraped posts to write newsletters, LinkedIn posts and threads from raw notes.

**Category:** Content & Creative  
**Agent:** Cursor  
**Source:** newsletter.qback.ai · Undated

## What they did

Harshit Jain saves messy raw thoughts as a .txt file in a Cursor workspace. Python scripts use Selenium to scrape LinkedIn posts from three marketing leaders and parse newsletter feeds. They also refresh his writing style from his own 32+ LinkedIn posts. The system then drafts a newsletter, LinkedIn posts and a Twitter thread into local folders pushed to Git, where he edits and publishes.

## How it went

The post says this newsletter itself was generated from a one-line input about being lazy. It gives no time-saved or engagement numbers, and the quality claim (audience can't tell) is unmeasured.

## Worth knowing

LinkedIn scraping needs browser automation, so you need to be comfortable running Python and Selenium scripts yourself.

## Prompt

```text
Load a few of my past newsletters or posts as style examples, then take these raw notes [paste notes] and draft a newsletter or LinkedIn post in my voice. Revise until the tone and structure match how I actually write, and stop once you have a polished draft ready for me to review.
```
