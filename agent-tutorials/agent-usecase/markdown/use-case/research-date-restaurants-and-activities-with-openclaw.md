# Research date restaurants and activities with OpenClaw

> A PR founder has OpenClaw research South Florida restaurants and activities and compile a linked document explaining each pick for the type of date.

**Category:** Life Admin & Home  
**Agent:** OpenClaw  
**Source:** techcrunch.com · Jul 2, 2026

## What they did

Jeff Weisbein, founder of a tech PR firm, meets dates in different parts of South Florida and doesn't know every area's restaurants or activities. He has his OpenClaw bot do the research and write a document. The document links to sources and explains why each place suits the type of date. He doesn't use it to handle conversations with the women.

## How it went

The source gives no figures. It describes the research step as replacing manual work. Weisbein was open about using AI, and one woman told him she hates AI agents.

## Worth knowing

Weisbein draws the line at delegating messages to AI. Being open about using an agent can put some people off.

## Prompt

```text
Research restaurants and activities in [city/area] for a [type of date, e.g. casual, romantic, adventurous] on [date] with a budget of about $[amount]. Create a document with [5-8] picks, each with a link, price range, and a sentence on why it fits. You are done when the document is complete. Do not book or reserve anything without showing me the details first.
```
