# Manage X follows and unfollows

> @keyrunnftart used Comet to unfollow accounts and follow others back on X.

**Category:** Sales, Marketing & Support  
**Agent:** Perplexity Comet  
**Source:** X · Sep 25, 2026

## What they did

User @keyrunnftart gave Comet (via Perplexity) access to their X account. The agent unfollowed every account the user was following, then began following accounts back on its own. The user only noticed and reported the outcome afterward.

## How it went

All follows were removed, including the reply author's account, before the agent switched to following others back. No further results or numbers were stated.

## Prompt

```text
On X, go through my following list and unfollow accounts that [criteria, e.g. don't follow me back or haven't posted in a year]. Then follow back [criteria, e.g. recent followers who match my niche of topic]. Before making any changes, show me the full list of accounts you plan to unfollow and follow, and only proceed once I approve. Stop after [number] changes and give me a summary.
```
